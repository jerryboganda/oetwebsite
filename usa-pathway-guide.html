USA overview

The USA is state based, not one national licence

National exams exist, but the actual licence is issued by a state board. That means the correct English test, credential report and application route can change by profession and state.

Important: This page is educational guidance, not legal, immigration, employment or licensure advice. Requirements change by state and cycle. Verify official sources before booking OET, TOEFL, IELTS or paying any application fee.

Doctors - ECFMG / Intealth pathway

ECFMGUSMLE and PathwaysOET Medicine

For international medical graduates aiming for ECFMG Certification, ERAS, the NRMP Match, residency training and later state medical licensure. OET Medicine is part of the communication skills requirement for Pathways applicants.

English-test planning: Official ECFMG 2027 guidance requires OET Medicine for Pathways applicants, with 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing in one test administration. This updates the older PDF score wording, so candidates must follow ECFMG current rules.

Pathway steps

Confirm school eligibilityCheck the World Directory Sponsor Note and create the correct MyIntealth account.
Pass USMLE stepsPlan Step 1 and Step 2 CK with your Pathway and OET timing.
Complete ECFMG Pathway and OETChoose the correct Pathway, sit OET Medicine, and release scores to ECFMG with the correct ID.
Apply for the MatchAfter certification, use ERAS/NRMP, then work with your program on visa and state licensure steps.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming USMLE scores remove the OET requirement.
  • Using OET@Home when a physical test venue is available and ECFMG rules do not allow it.
  • Missing Match-related timing for OET results.
  • Forgetting to release scores to ECFMG/MyIntealth correctly.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

Structured medical letters with selective relevance, formal tone and safe clinical summary.

Speaking

Consultations, diagnosis explanation, bad news, patient concerns and shared decisions.

Reading

Medical journal extracts, guideline summaries, drug information and workplace texts.

Listening

Multi-accent clinical consultations, ward briefings and presentations.

Nurses - State BON / NCLEX pathway

State Boards of NursingCGFNS/TruMeritNCLEX-RN

For internationally educated nurses applying state by state. NCSBN owns the NCLEX-RN, but each Board of Nursing decides application requirements, credential evaluation, English testing, fingerprinting, SSN policy and authorization to test.

English-test planning: Do not assume OET Nursing will count for US nursing. The PDF warns that TOEFL iBT or IELTS Academic is usually the relevant English route, depending on the state board and VisaScreen requirements.

Pathway steps

Choose a target stateCheck BON rules before paying for any test or credential report.
Order credential evaluationUse the report required by the state, such as CGFNS/TruMerit CES or a state-specific route.
Complete English and BON applicationMeet the state English rule, submit the BON application and receive authorization to test.
Pass NCLEX and plan VisaScreenSit Next Generation NCLEX and prepare VisaScreen/employer sponsorship where required.

Common mistakes

  • Booking OET Nursing for a US state without checking acceptance.
  • Choosing a state without checking SSN, fingerprinting and concurrency rules.
  • Ordering the wrong CGFNS/TruMerit report.
  • Preparing NCLEX with memorisation instead of clinical judgement.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

If you also target the UK, OET Nursing supports NMC preparation, but it may not support your US BON route.

Speaking

NCLEX communication preparation should focus on patient safety, prioritisation and clinical judgement.

Reading

Read state board instructions carefully, especially concurrency and report-type language.

Listening

Prepare for US accents and workplace communication, but choose the English test your target authority accepts.

Pharmacists - NABP / FPGEC pathway

NABPFPGEC and FPGEETOEFL iBT

For foreign pharmacy graduates seeking FPGEC Certification before state board eligibility, NAPLEX, MPJE or a state law equivalent, internship hours and final licensure.

English-test planning: NABP/FPGEC requires TOEFL iBT as part of FPGEC Certification. Current NABP guidance says all four sections must be completed in one testing session, MyBest scores are not accepted, and remote-proctored/at-home TOEFL is not accepted.

Pathway steps

Create NABP e-ProfileUse the correct NABP account and follow the current Candidate Application Bulletin.
Prepare TOEFL iBT correctlyUse the current NABP score scale and test-location rules before submitting the FPGEC application.
Submit ECE and documentsArrange ECE evaluation, license evidence, identity documents, translations and score reports.
Pass FPGEE then state examsComplete FPGEC Certification, then NAPLEX, MPJE/state law exam and internship hours as required.

Common mistakes

  • Booking OET Pharmacy for FPGEC.
  • Using TOEFL Home Edition or an ineligible test site.
  • Trying to use MyBest scores.
  • Submitting ECE or license documents incorrectly.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

OET Pharmacy can help UK or Australia planning, but it does not replace FPGEC TOEFL rules.

Speaking

Pharmacists need English preparation aligned with TOEFL iBT if the sole target is the US.

Reading

Read NABP document-sealing and ECE instructions carefully to avoid delays.

Listening

Plan FPGEE timing because exam windows and certification timelines are strict.

Physical therapists - FSBPT / State PT pathway

FSBPTCredential evaluationNPTE

For foreign-educated physical therapists applying through state boards. Each state decides evaluation agency, English testing, supervised practice and jurisprudence rules, while the NPTE is administered nationally through FSBPT.

English-test planning: The PDF positions TOEFL iBT as the standard US physical therapy English-test route. Do not book OET Physiotherapy for the US unless your exact state board explicitly confirms acceptance; OET Physiotherapy is primarily useful for UK HCPC and selected non-US bodies.

Pathway steps

Choose state and evaluatorCheck whether FCCPT, ECE, IERF or another agency is required by the target state.
Complete credential reviewUse the correct Coursework Tool and submit verified documents.
Meet English and state application rulesPrepare TOEFL iBT or the state-accepted evidence, then apply for authorization.
Pass NPTE and state stepsTake the NPTE, complete law exams or supervised practice if required, and plan Type 1/VisaScreen where relevant.

Common mistakes

  • Assuming OET Physiotherapy counts for US PT licensure.
  • Choosing an evaluator not accepted by the target state.
  • Missing NPTE registration windows.
  • Ignoring attempt limits or state law exam requirements.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

Use OET Physiotherapy only for countries and regulators that accept it, such as HCPC where criteria are met.

Speaking

For US PT, English preparation usually needs TOEFL-style planning.

Reading

Read state board rules for attempts, deadlines and evaluator choice.

Listening

Prepare communication around functional status, rehabilitation and patient education for workplace readiness.

Candidate checklist

Before you start the USA pathway

  • Identify the correct body: ECFMG/Intealth, state BON, NABP/FPGEC or FSBPT/state PT board.
  • Choose a target state early because rules are not uniform.
  • Confirm the accepted English test before booking.
  • Order the exact credential report required by your profession and state.
  • Plan 12 to 24 months where credentialing, exams and documentation overlap.
  • Separate licensure planning from visa planning and seek qualified visa advice where needed.

USA pathway FAQ

Short answers for healthcare professionals planning US licensure and English testing.

Do USA doctors need OET Medicine?

Yes, current ECFMG Pathways guidance requires OET Medicine for Pathways applicants and states there are no exceptions for native language, medical school language or citizenship.

What OET score does ECFMG currently require?

Current 2027 ECFMG guidance lists 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing in one test administration. Candidates must verify the current ECFMG cycle before booking.

Can nurses use OET Nursing for the USA?

Do not assume that. US nursing is state-board dependent and commonly uses TOEFL iBT or IELTS plus credential evaluation and NCLEX-RN planning.

Is US licensure the same as a visa?

No. Licensure, certification, credentialing and visas are separate. Candidates should verify official licensing rules and seek licensed immigration advice for visa questions.
