Canada Pathway Guide - Edition 2027

Canada Pathway Guide 2027: Where OET Is Accepted

Canada has no national health licence. Each provincial or territorial regulator decides which English test it accepts, and immigration uses a different list again. This guide shows where OET is accepted, where it is not, and what we could not confirm.

By Dr Ahmed Hesham, medical doctor and founder of OET with Dr Hesham · Published 27 Nov 2026

Canada overview

Choose your regulator and province first

National bodies in Canada run examinations or credential checks, but the licence comes from the regulator of one province or territory, and that regulator sets the language rule. OET is accepted by some regulators for medicine, nursing and pharmacy. For several other professions the national body lists other tests and not OET. Confirm the accepted test with the regulator of the province where you will work before you book anything.

Important: This guide is educational orientation only. It is not legal, immigration or regulator advice, and regulators can change their rules without notice. We read a limited number of regulators: where a province is not named below, its rule is not confirmed. We are an independent OET coaching service. We are not affiliated with OET, with any regulator or with any test centre, and test centres are not our locations.

Immigration

Immigration language tests are separate from registration

A language test for a visa and a language test for a licence are two different requirements, set by two different bodies.

IRCC does not list OET

The IRCC Express Entry language test page (dated 16 October 2026) lists CELPIP, IELTS and PTE Core for English, and TEF Canada and TCF Canada for French. OET is not mentioned. We did not read other IRCC programmes, so those are not confirmed.

You may need two tests

If your regulator accepts OET and you also apply through Express Entry, plan for one test for registration and another for immigration. An OET result does not replace an IRCC-approved test, and an IRCC-approved test may not satisfy your regulator.

Check before you pay

Some regulators accept tests that IRCC also lists. Read both lists side by side for your province and profession. If one test satisfies both bodies, that may be the cheaper route even if you prefer OET.

Doctors: MCC and the provincial colleges

Medical Council of CanadaProvincial collegesOET Medicine

The Medical Council of Canada (page updated 21 July 2026) sets no language test of its own. It says that showing proficiency in English or French is part of the licensure pathway set by each medical regulatory authority, meaning the college of your province. See the OET course for doctors if your college accepts OET.

English-test planning: British Columbia: the CPSBC policy (version 2.2, effective 7 April 2026, revised 11 September 2026) asks for OET Medicine with at least grade B in each component in one sitting. Results are valid for two years from the test date and combined scores from several attempts are not accepted. You send results by adding CPSBC as a verified organisation in your myOET account. It also accepts IELTS Academic 7 in each component or CELPIP General 9 in each. Alberta: CPSA asks for OET Medicine with at least grade B in each component, completed within 24 months before your application. Whether it must be one sitting is not confirmed. Ontario: we could not open the CPSO language proficiency page. A search extract suggests OET Medicine with grade B in each sub-test, in the same sitting, within 24 months, but treat that as not confirmed. Other provinces: not confirmed. OET itself says every college with an English requirement recognises OET, but its page names no college and no score, so that is OET's claim and not a regulator's rule. OET@Home is not confirmed for any of the three colleges.

Pathway steps

Pick the provinceThe college of that province decides the language rule, the exemptions and the route to a licence.
Open a physiciansapply.ca accountMCC uses it for its services, including source verification of your medical degree.
Plan the MCC examinationsMCC describes the MCCQE and the NAC Examination. The NAC is needed for the residency match and is used by some practice-ready assessment programmes. MCC changed some of its rules in 2026 and we have not confirmed the details: read its page.
Residency or practice-ready assessmentMCC describes two routes: a residency through the CaRMS match, or a Practice-Ready Assessment, a 12-week workplace assessment for doctors who already practised independently. Then the LMCC and the provincial licence.

Common mistakes

  • Booking OET for Express Entry: IRCC does not list it.
  • Combining two OET sittings for CPSBC, which refuses combined scores.
  • Assuming one college's rule applies in another province.
  • Booking OET@Home. No college we read confirms it.
  • Forgetting to add the college in myOET, where the college asks for results that way.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Referral, discharge and transfer letters with selective relevance and a formal tone.

Speaking

Consultations, explaining a diagnosis, breaking bad news and shared decisions.

Reading

Guideline extracts, drug information and workplace texts under time pressure.

Listening

Consultations, ward briefings and presentations in different accents.

Nurses: provincial nursing regulators

Provincial regulatorsOET Nursing where acceptedCELBAN or IELTS elsewhere

Nursing registration is provincial too, and the regulators do not agree on OET. We read Ontario, British Columbia and Alberta directly. We could not confirm the current language policy of NNAS, the national credential service: a search extract suggests it no longer collects language scores and that proof goes straight to the provincial regulator. BCCNM says internationally educated nurses applying after 31 January 2023 are assessed by Inspire Global Assessments. See the OET course for nurses if your regulator accepts OET.

English-test planning: Ontario: CNO accepts OET with Writing C+ (320), Speaking B (350), Listening B (350) and Reading C+ (330), reached in one complete attempt, with the test written within two years of registering. OET@Home is not confirmed. British Columbia: BCCNM (page updated 24 November 2026) accepts OET with Reading C+, Listening B, Writing C+ and Speaking B, in person only, with evidence no more than two years old. Whether sittings can be combined is not confirmed. Alberta: CRNA lists only CELBAN and IELTS Academic and says it does not currently accept other tests, so do not book OET for CRNA. Manitoba: the CRNM document (effective 26 March 2025) asks for a language test within the past two years and allows the minimum scores across two sittings of the same test, but it does not print the list of tests, so OET and its scores are not confirmed. Nova Scotia, Saskatchewan, Quebec, the Atlantic provinces, the territories and all practical nurse regulators: not confirmed.

Pathway steps

Choose the province and nurse categoryRegistered nurse and practical nurse regulators can be different bodies with different lists.
Follow the assessment route your regulator namesYour regulator tells you which credential assessment service to use. Do not order a report before you know.
Sit the test your regulator listsOET Nursing only where it is listed, at a venue, with the whole score line in one attempt unless the regulator says otherwise.
Send the result in timeEach regulator counts the two years in its own way. Check the wording.

Common mistakes

  • Booking OET for Alberta: CRNA does not accept it.
  • Booking OET@Home for British Columbia: BCCNM is in person only.
  • Splitting the score line over two sittings for CNO.
  • Assuming an OET Nursing result also counts for Express Entry.
  • Using one province's score line for another province.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Nursing referral, discharge and transfer letters with clear purpose and selective case notes.

Speaking

Patient education, reassurance and handover role-plays in a calm nursing register.

Reading

Fast Part A scanning, then careful reading of policies and care guidance.

Listening

Nurse and patient consultations and ward briefings in different accents.

Pharmacists: NAPRA policy and the provincial colleges

NAPRA model policyProvincial collegesOET Pharmacy

The NAPRA Language Proficiency Requirement Policy (approved November 2023, published February 2024) is a recommendation to the provincial pharmacy regulators, not a rule of its own. Pharmacists' Gateway Canada says most jurisdictions adopted or adapted it on 1 September 2024, Manitoba on 24 March 2025 and Ontario on 1 January 2026, and that Quebec is outside this scheme. It also says scores are valid for two years from the sitting and explains how OET candidates send their results letter. See the OET course for pharmacists for preparation.

English-test planning: NAPRA policy: OET is one of the recommended tests, with cut scores for pharmacists of Reading B, Listening B, Speaking B and Writing C+. All four sub-tests must be completed in a single testing session. The policy says scores are typically valid for two years. It says remotely proctored tests are acceptable if they are as secure, valid and reliable as on-site tests, without naming OET@Home, so each province decides. Ontario: the Ontario College of Pharmacists applies Reading B, Writing C+, Listening B and Speaking B from 1 January 2026, valid for two years, and accepts OET on Paper, OET on Computer and OET@Home. Other provinces: not confirmed here, so read your own college's page. PEBC: we did not read the Pharmacy Examining Board of Canada's pages, so its examination steps are not confirmed in this guide.

Also considering the United States? NABP will accept OET Pharmacy for FPGEC certification for tests taken on or after 11 January 2027, and it requires its English test to be taken at an approved computer-based test centre in an NABP jurisdiction, which covers the United States and the 10 Canadian provinces. Home editions are not accepted, and NABP had not published its minimum OET scores when we checked on 27 November 2026. Read OET for pharmacists in the USA and our USA pathway guide before you book one test for both countries.

Pathway steps

Choose the provinceRead your pharmacy regulator's own language page. Do not rely on the NAPRA policy alone, and remember Quebec is separate.
Check the examination routeThe PEBC steps are not confirmed here. Read them on the PEBC website before you plan dates.
Sit OET Pharmacy in one sessionAll four sub-tests together, reaching B, B, B and Writing C+ where the NAPRA cut scores apply.
Send the result within two yearsFollow the instructions on the Pharmacists' Gateway Canada language page for OET results.

Common mistakes

  • Treating the NAPRA policy as the rule of every province.
  • Booking OET@Home outside Ontario without checking the provincial college.
  • Planning a paper test in 2027: from January 2027 OET on Paper is for Nursing and Medicine only.
  • Splitting sub-tests across sittings.
  • Booking the same test for Canada and the United States without reading both sets of rules.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Letters to prescribers and other professionals with accurate medicine details.

Speaking

Medication counselling, adherence, side effects and safe refusal of a sale.

Reading

Medicine information, safety alerts and workplace policies.

Listening

Pharmacist and patient counselling and team handovers.

Do not book the wrong test

Test formats and venues

Paper, computer or OET@Home in Canada

Three separate questions decide your format: what OET lets you book in your country and profession, what your regulator accepts, and how Speaking is delivered.

OET on Paper from January 2027

OET announced on 16 November 2026 that from January 2027 the paper test continues for Nursing and Medicine only. The announcement names no countries. OET's customer care team told us by email in November 2026 that Canada is one of the 16 countries that keep paper testing. Paper sessions are never guaranteed: see our paper test centres 2027 list for Canada.

OET on Computer at a venue

OET says the computer test stays available for all 12 professions at approved venues, subject to availability. On 27 November 2026 the official OET finder listed 16 computer venue records in Canada, including Toronto, Vancouver, Calgary, Ottawa, Halifax and Winnipeg. Venues and sessions change, so check our OET test centres list for Canada and then the finder itself. Speaking on a computer test may be delivered by live video: that is not OET@Home.

OET@Home

OET says OET@Home is offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available, and Canada has venues. Regulators differ as well: BCCNM is in person only, the Ontario College of Pharmacists accepts OET@Home, NAPRA leaves it to each province, and CPSBC, CPSA and CNO do not say. Read our OET@Home guide first.

Sittings

CPSBC, CNO and the NAPRA policy all ask for the full score in one sitting or attempt. Only the Manitoba nursing document we read mentions two sittings, and we could not confirm that it covers OET. Compare regulators in our OET clubbing rules guide and our score requirements guide.

Candidate checklist

Before you book OET for Canada

  • Choose the province or territory where you will work.
  • Find the regulator for your profession in that province and open its language page.
  • Confirm that OET is on its list. If it is not, book a test that is.
  • Write down the score line, the sittings rule and how the two years are counted.
  • Check the format: venue test or OET@Home, and whether paper is still offered for your profession.
  • If you are immigrating through Express Entry, plan the IRCC-approved test separately.
  • Start with free OET practice or the free placement test to see how far you are from the score.

Canada pathway FAQ

Short answers for healthcare professionals planning Canadian registration and English testing.

Does Canada accept OET?

There is no single answer, because health licensing in Canada is provincial. Some regulators accept OET, for example CPSBC and CPSA for doctors, CNO and BCCNM for nurses and the Ontario College of Pharmacists. Others do not, so check your own regulator before booking.

Can I use OET for Express Entry?

No. IRCC's Express Entry page lists CELPIP, IELTS and PTE Core for English, and OET is not on that list. If you need both immigration and registration, you may need two different tests.

What OET score do nurses need in Ontario?

The College of Nurses of Ontario prints Writing C+ (320), Speaking B (350), Listening B (350) and Reading C+ (330), all in one complete attempt, with the test written within two years of registering.

Do nurses in Alberta or British Columbia need OET?

CRNA in Alberta lists only CELBAN and IELTS Academic, so OET is not accepted there. BCCNM in British Columbia accepts OET with Reading C+, Listening B, Writing C+ and Speaking B, in person only.

What OET score do doctors need in British Columbia and Alberta?

CPSBC asks for OET Medicine with at least grade B in each component in one sitting, valid for two years from the test date. CPSA asks for at least grade B in each component, taken within 24 months before the application.

Is OET accepted for pharmacists in Canada?

NAPRA's model policy recommends OET with Reading B, Listening B, Speaking B and Writing C+ in a single testing session, and each provincial regulator decides whether to adopt it. Ontario applies those cut scores from 1 January 2026 and accepts OET on Paper, OET on Computer and OET@Home.

Do physiotherapists, occupational therapists, dentists or vets need OET for Canada?

No. CAPR, ACOTRO, the Royal College of Dental Surgeons of Ontario and the CVMA National Examining Board publish lists of approved English tests, and OET is not on them. Book one of the tests they list instead.

Not sure whether OET fits your Canada plan?

Check your provincial regulator first. If it accepts OET, start with free practice to see your level, then choose the course for your profession. If you are unsure, message us with your profession and province and we will point you to the right official page.
