Singapore Pathway Guide: OET for SMC, SPC, SDC and AHPC

Singapore regulates each health profession through its own council or board. Four of them list OET for foreign-trained applicants: SMC for doctors, SPC for pharmacists, SDC for dentists and AHPC for allied health. The Singapore Nursing Board lists no English test. This guide sets out what each body publishes, with a link to every source.

By Dr Ahmed Hesham, medical doctor and founder of OET with Dr Hesham · Published 27 Nov 2026

Last verified: 27 November 2026. Every rule below was read on the regulator's own page on that date. Where a page was silent or was not read, we say "not confirmed".

Singapore pathway overview

Singapore has documented OET recognition, but not for every profession. The table covers all 12 OET professions honestly. "Accepted" means the regulator names OET on the page we read. "Not listed" means the regulator's page names no English test. "Not confirmed" means we read no primary source, so this guide states no rule.

Choose your regulator first. Then confirm the accepted test and the accepted format before you book. Do not assume OET is the route because it works for a colleague in another profession.

Important: This guide is educational orientation only. It is not legal, immigration or regulator advice, and regulators can change their rules without notice. We are an independent coaching service, not part of OET, any regulator or any test centre. Always read the rule on the regulator's own page before you book a test or pay a fee.

Four checks before you book

Singapore's rules are stricter on sittings than many other destinations. Run these four checks in order.

1. Your regulator

SMC, SPC, SDC, AHPC or SNB. Open its own page. If you are a nurse, read the nursing section first: SNB lists no English test.

2. Whether you need a test

Each council exempts some applicants, usually by where the qualification was taught. Check whether the rule applies to you before paying for any test.

3. Single sitting

All four councils that list OET want every score from one sitting. Combining results is not an option here. Compare other regulators in our guide to combining OET sittings.

4. Test format

OET@Home acceptance is not confirmed for any Singapore council. Whether you can book OET@Home is OET's rule. Whether your council accepts it is a separate question. See OET@Home: who accepts it.

Singapore Medical Council

Doctors: Singapore Medical Council (SMC)

The rules come from the SMC page Documents for submission and processing time (last updated 2 September 2026).

RuleWhat the regulator publishes
Who needs itInternational medical graduates whose basic medical degree is from a school where English is not the medium of instruction.
OETAccepted At least Grade B in each of the four components: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.
SittingsThe results must be obtained in a single sitting.
ValidityAccepted only within 2 years from the date of the test.
Paper, computer, OET@HomeNot confirmed The page states no delivery format for OET.
ExceptionClinical Observers on temporary registration do not need to submit English test results.
Other testsIELTS Academic with at least 7 in each component. TOEFL iBT is also listed, with scores that depend on the test date (before, or on and after, 21 January 2026).

Registration route

  • Registration depends on holding a recognised qualification and an offer of employment.
  • Not confirmed We did not read the registration categories or the list of recognised medical schools, so they are not described here. Read them on the SMC website before you plan.

Before you book

  • One sitting only: a strong result in three components does not carry over to a later test.
  • Count the 2 years from your test date to the date SMC will use your result.
  • Preparing for OET Medicine? See the OET course for doctors.
Singapore Nursing Board

Nurses: Singapore Nursing Board (SNB)

The SNB page for foreign-trained nurses and midwives (last updated 9 June 2026) lists no English test: not OET, not IELTS, not TOEFL.

Nurses, read this first: do not assume OET is your route to Singapore. SNB does not list it. Book OET only if your employer, or another regulator you are also applying to, asks for it in writing.
RuleWhat the regulator publishes
English testNot listed The page names no English test for foreign-trained nurses and midwives.
Job offerYou must have an offer of employment with an eligible healthcare institution (with inpatient services) in Singapore.
QualificationCompletion of a pre-registration or pre-enrolment nursing or midwifery programme, and nursing or midwifery registration from the country where you trained.
AssessmentYou may need to sit a Licensure Examination, undergo a competency assessment, or be placed on provisional registration.
DocumentsDocuments that are not in English need a certified official translation in English.
OET in practiceNot confirmed Whether SNB or an employer accepts or asks for OET is outside the page we read.

What to do instead

  • Start with the employer: the job offer comes first in the SNB process.
  • Ask the employer in writing whether it wants an English test, which one, and which score.
  • If you are also applying to another country, plan the test around that regulator's rule.

Nursing elsewhere

  • Other destinations publish clear OET rules for nurses. Compare them in OET score requirements.
  • Neighbouring systems: the Australia pathway guide and the New Zealand pathway guide.
  • Preparing for OET Nursing for another regulator? See the OET course for nurses.
Singapore Pharmacy Council

Pharmacists: Singapore Pharmacy Council (SPC)

The rules come from the SPC pages Evidence of proficiency in English language (last updated 15 July 2026) and Foreign-trained pharmacy graduates and pharmacists (last updated 9 October 2026).

RuleWhat the regulator publishes
Who needs itInternational pharmacy graduates who obtained their pharmacy degree outside the UK, USA, Canada, Australia or New Zealand, at the point of application for pre-registration training.
OETAccepted OET under the profession Pharmacy, with at least 350 in each of the four components.
SittingsThe results must be obtained at one sitting.
ValidityWithin 2 years before the date of application for pre-registration training.
Paper, computer, OET@HomeNot confirmed The page states no delivery format for OET.
Other testsIELTS Academic with an overall score of at least 7 and at least 7 in each of the four components. TOEFL iBT is also listed, with scores that depend on the test date.

Registration route in brief

  • A university degree accepted by SPC, and a job offer: SPC will not consider an application without one.
  • Pre-registration training, which ranges from 3 to 12 months.
  • The Competency Examination and the Forensic Examination, each with a pass mark of 60.
  • Conditional registration for at least 12 months under an approved supervisor, then full registration.

Before you book

  • SPC uses numbers, not grades: 350 in every component, at one sitting.
  • The 2 years are counted back from your application for pre-registration training, so time the test to the job offer.
  • Preparing for OET Pharmacy? See the OET course for pharmacists.
Singapore Dental Council

Dentists: Singapore Dental Council (SDC)

The English rule is printed on the SDC page Criteria for the Qualifying Examination (last updated 6 May 2026). The conditional registration page itself mentions no English test.

RuleWhat the regulator publishes
Who needs itForeign-trained applicants whose first language is not English.
OETAccepted At least Grade B in each of the four components: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.
SittingsThe scores must be obtained in a single sitting.
ValidityA 2-year validity period based on the date of the test.
Paper, computer, OET@HomeNot confirmed The page states no delivery format for OET.
Other testsIELTS Academic with at least 7 in all four components. TOEFL iBT is also listed, with scores that depend on the test date.

Registration route

  • Conditional registration means working under the supervision of a fully registered dentist approved by the Council.
  • Not confirmed A change that would make a Qualifying Examination compulsory before conditional registration at a future date could not be read on a primary page. Check the SDC announcements before you plan.

Before you book

  • Dentistry has no OET paper test from January 2027, and Singapore has no paper venue in any case. Plan for OET on computer.
  • One sitting only, Grade B in all four.
  • Preparing for OET Dentistry? See OET for dentists.
Allied Health Professions Council

Allied health: AHPC

The rules come from the AHPC page Requirements for registration application (last updated 24 July 2026). It covers physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, diagnostic radiography and radiation therapy.

Check the wording: we read the AHPC table as a summary, not as quoted sentences. Open the page and read your profession's row yourself. The radiography row shows OET Grade B beside a lower IELTS figure, so radiographers should confirm their row with AHPC.
ProfessionMinimum OET in the AHPC table
PhysiotherapyGrade B or above. The table adds that computer-based testing is also accepted for Physiotherapy.
Occupational TherapyGrade B or above.
Diagnostic Radiography and Radiation TherapyGrade B or above.
Speech-Language TherapyGrade B (400) in three components and Grade B (350) in one, with effect from 1 August 2025.

Rules that apply to all five

  • Only results taken within 2 years before the registration application date are accepted.
  • All components must be met in a single examination sitting. There is no limit on the number of attempts.
  • No English test is needed where the basic qualification is from Singapore, Australia, Canada (except Quebec), New Zealand, the Republic of Ireland, South Africa, the UK or the USA.
  • Everyone else must show English proficiency before conditional registration.
  • OET@Home: Not confirmed

Route and other professions

  • If your qualification is not on the AHPC recognised list, you take a Qualifying Examination. The examination provider does not accept an application without a referral letter from AHPC.
  • For diagnostic radiographers and radiation therapists, an AHPC announcement of 26 September 2022 said the OET and IELTS score requirement was unchanged for the time being.
  • Dietetics and podiatry do not appear in the table we read. Not confirmed Optometry and veterinary science have other regulators that we did not read. Not confirmed
  • Preparing for an allied health version of OET? See OET for allied health professionals and the OET course for physiotherapists.

Test formats and venues in Singapore

Test centres belong to OET's network, not to us. We list what OET's own venue finder showed on 27 November 2026. A listed venue is not a promise of an open seat on your date.

OET on computer at a venue

On 27 November 2026 the finder listed one computer venue record in Singapore. OET says the computer test at a venue stays available for all 12 professions. See OET test centres in Singapore.

OET on paper

The finder listed no paper venue in Singapore. From January 2027 the paper test continues for Nursing and Medicine only (OET, 16 November 2026), and Singapore is not among the 16 countries named in the OET customer care email of November 2026.

OET@Home

Not confirmed for SMC, SPC, SDC or AHPC: none of the pages we read mentions it. A venue test with Speaking by video is not OET@Home. Ask your council in writing before you book. See OET@Home: who accepts it.

Book only through OET's official booking page. OET's own fee and its terms are on oet.com.

Candidate checklist before you start

  • Name your regulator: SMC, SPC, SDC, AHPC or SNB.
  • Confirm that it lists OET for your profession. Nurses: SNB lists no English test.
  • Check whether you are exempt because of where your qualification was taught.
  • Write down the minimum score and plan to reach all of it in a single sitting.
  • Count the 2-year validity from the date your council uses.
  • Ask the council in writing about the test format if you are considering anything other than a test centre.
  • Take the free placement test and start with free OET practice to see how far you are from the score.

Official sources to verify

All read on 27 November 2026. Singapore's regulators now publish on their own domains. Regulators can change a rule without notice, so open the source again before you book.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers for candidates planning OET and registration in Singapore.

Is OET accepted in Singapore?

Yes for some professions. On 27 November 2026 the Singapore Medical Council, the Singapore Pharmacy Council, the Singapore Dental Council and the Allied Health Professions Council all listed OET as English evidence for foreign-trained applicants who need it. The Singapore Nursing Board page for foreign-trained nurses lists no English test at all.

Do nurses need OET for Singapore?

The Singapore Nursing Board page for foreign-trained nurses and midwives does not list OET or any other English test. It asks for a job offer from an eligible healthcare institution, your nursing qualification and your registration in the country where you trained. Whether an employer asks for an English test is not confirmed, so ask your employer before you book OET.

What OET score do doctors need for the Singapore Medical Council?

The Singapore Medical Council asks for at least Grade B in each of the four components, obtained in a single sitting. The result is accepted only within 2 years from the date of the test. The rule applies to international medical graduates whose basic degree is from a school where English is not the medium of instruction.

What OET score do pharmacists need for the Singapore Pharmacy Council?

The Singapore Pharmacy Council asks for OET Pharmacy with at least 350 in each of the four components, obtained at one sitting, within 2 years before the date you apply for pre-registration training. The rule applies to graduates whose pharmacy degree is from outside the UK, USA, Canada, Australia and New Zealand.

Does Singapore accept OET@Home?

Not confirmed. None of the four council pages we read mentions OET@Home or any delivery format for OET, apart from a note in the allied health table that computer-based testing is also accepted for Physiotherapy. Ask your council in writing before you book an at-home test.

Can I combine two OET sittings for Singapore?

No. The four councils that list OET all ask for the required scores in a single sitting. The Allied Health Professions Council sets no limit on the number of attempts, but each attempt must reach every score on its own.

Where can I sit OET in Singapore?

On 27 November 2026 the OET venue finder listed one computer test venue in Singapore and no paper venue. Seats and dates change, so confirm in the finder before you fix your plan.

Know your Singapore target score? Start preparing.

Confirm the rule on your regulator's own page first. Then tell us your profession, your target score and your test date, and we will point you to the right preparation step.
