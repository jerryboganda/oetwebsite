OET preparation course in Egypt

Candidates in Egypt can sit OET on Paper or OET on Computer at venues in Greater Cairo and Alexandria, and prepare for it fully online. This page sets out where the test runs in Egypt, what it costs, which scores the regulators abroad ask for, and how our course works from Egypt.

By Dr Ahmed Hesham, medical doctor and founder of OET with Dr Hesham · Published 19 Nov 2026

Where to take OET in Egypt

  • AMIDEAST, Dokki, Giza: OET on Paper and OET on Computer
  • Future University in Egypt (FUE), 90th Street, New Cairo: OET on Paper
  • AASTMT, Smart Village, Giza: OET on Paper
  • AASTMT, Miami campus, Alexandria: OET on Paper
  • American Center, Azarita, Alexandria: OET on Paper and OET on Computer

Egypt has test venues, and the venue search offered no OET@Home option, so plan for a sitting at a venue.

Venues as listed by the OET venue finder on 19 November 2026. Bookings close 24 days before an OET on Paper test and 7 days before an OET on Computer test. Venues, dates and seats change, so confirm in the finder before you plan travel. For the choice between the two formats see OET on Computer vs OET on Paper, and for dates see OET test dates and fees.

Is OET required in Egypt?

We found no page from the Ministry of Health and Population, the Egyptian Medical Syndicate or the Egyptian Health Council that requires or accepts OET. Doctors, nurses and pharmacists in Egypt take OET because the regulator in the country they are moving to asks for it.

One point matters for doctors applying to ECFMG: it accepts OET@Home only from applicants whose country has no test venue. Egypt has venues, so sit the test at one.

The scores regulators abroad ask for

Most candidates in Egypt take OET for registration in another country. These are the minimums for the test dates current on 19 November 2026.

RegulatorOET scores it asks for
GMC (UK doctors)Grade B (350) in all four sub-tests, in one sitting
NMC (UK nurses and midwives)350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking, 300 in Writing; two sittings can be combined
GPhC (UK pharmacists)Grade B in all four, in one sitting; OET@Home is not accepted
Medical Council of IrelandGrade B in each sub-test, in one sitting; OET@Home is not accepted
NMBI (Ireland, nurses and midwives)Grade B in three sub-tests and C+ in Writing, one result; OET@Home is not accepted
Ahpra (Australia)350 Listening, 360 Reading, 350 Writing, 360 Speaking for tests from 23 April 2026; at-home tests are not approved
ECFMG (USA, doctors)OET Medicine: 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking, 300 in Writing, in one sitting

Each regulator can change its rules, so confirm on its own page before you book. Sittings, validity and official links are in OET score requirements; pathways are in the UK, Australia, USA and Gulf guides.

How the course works from Egypt

The course is online, so nothing depends on where you live in Egypt. Lessons are recorded and specific to your profession, and you can watch them at any hour. In the packages that include personal Writing assessments, Dr Ahmed Hesham assesses your letters himself and returns an estimated score, a detailed correction and voice-note feedback. Private Speaking sessions are live and one to one with an expert tutor: when you book, give your local time (UTC+2 (UTC+3 during summer time, from late April to late October)).

Website practice follows the computer-based test for Listening and Reading and includes live virtual patient Speaking role-plays. Support is by WhatsApp in English or Arabic, and the same account works in the web app and the iOS and Android apps.

OET in Egypt: FAQ

Where can I take OET in Egypt?

On 19 November 2026 the OET venue finder listed venues in Greater Cairo (AMIDEAST in Dokki, Future University in New Cairo and AASTMT in Smart Village) and in Alexandria (AASTMT Miami and the American Center in Azarita). Venues change, so check the OET venue finder before you book.

Can I take OET on computer in Egypt?

Yes. On 19 November 2026 OET on Computer was listed at AMIDEAST in Dokki and at the American Center in Alexandria. The other three venues were listed for OET on Paper only.

How much does OET cost in Egypt?

OET charges AU$587 for the full test, paid by card when you book. OET publishes no price in Egyptian pounds, so your bank sets the exchange rate and may add a foreign-currency fee.

Can I prepare for OET from Egypt in Arabic?

Yes. The lessons are in English, because the test is, and support is available in Arabic. The course is recorded, so you study at any hour, and Writing letters are assessed personally by Dr Ahmed Hesham.
