How should you use OET sample tests?

A sample test only helps if you sit it like the real exam. Keep to the official timing, mark Listening and Reading with the answer key, and get Writing and Speaking judged by someone who knows the criteria.

Listening (about 40 minutes, 42 questions)

Three parts: Part A note completion from two consultations, Part B short workplace extracts and Part C longer presentations or interviews. Each recording is played once.

Reading (60 minutes, 42 questions)

Part A is a strict 15-minute scanning task across four short texts; Parts B and C share the remaining 45 minutes. OET says Grade B typically needs at least 30 out of 42 in Listening or Reading, but the boundary varies slightly by test version.

Writing (5 minutes reading, 40 minutes writing)

One letter from profession-specific case notes, with a body of about 180–200 words. There is no automatic penalty for going slightly over or under.

Speaking (about 20 minutes)

Two role-plays. You get 3 minutes to prepare each card and about 5 minutes to complete it, with the interlocutor playing the patient, a relative or a carer.

OET sample tests FAQ

More exam questions are answered on the full OET FAQ page.

Are OET sample tests free?

Yes. OET publishes official sample tests for each profession on its website, for the paper and computer formats. Our writing samples, speaking role-play cards, score calculator and placement test are also free to use.

Where can I find OET sample tests for nurses?

OET's official nursing sample test for the paper format is on oet.com, and the computer-format samples are listed on OET's computer sample-test page. For extra nursing practice, use our discharge and transfer letter samples and the nursing role-play cards.

How many marks do I need in Listening and Reading for a B?

Listening and Reading each have 42 questions. OET says candidates awarded Grade B (350) typically score at least 30 out of 42, but the exact boundary changes slightly with each test version.

Are OET recalls the same as sample tests?

No. Recalls are candidates' memories of past exam tasks shared after the test, not official OET material. Use OET's official sample tests to learn the real format and timing.

Can I get my sample letter marked?

Yes. Write the letter under timed conditions, then use OET writing correction: each letter is marked manually by Dr Ahmed with an estimated score, detailed correction and voice-note feedback via WhatsApp.
