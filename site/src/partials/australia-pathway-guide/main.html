Australia pathway overview

Australia uses a single national registration system administered by AHPRA with profession-specific National Boards. Every candidate must match the correct regulator route, English evidence, assessment body and document verification process.

Important: This guide is educational orientation only. It is not legal, immigration or regulator advice. Always verify requirements on AHPRA, the relevant National Board and assessment council before booking a test or paying a fee.
ProfessionMain routeOET relevance
DoctorsMedical Board, AHPRA and AMC: Standard, Competent Authority or Specialist Pathway.OET Medicine for English evidence and clinical communication readiness.

AHPRA English language standard at a glance

All four professions in this guide use the AHPRA English Language Skills registration standard. The PDF highlights a 2026 score transition, so your test date matters.

On or before 22 April 2026

OET minimums listed in the guide: Listening B, Reading B, Writing C+ and Speaking B. The writing minimum was lowered from B to C+ on 18 March 2025.

On or after 23 April 2026

OET numerical minimums listed in the guide: Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350 and Speaking 360.

Test-centre rule

AHPRA accepts paper or computer OET taken at an OET test centre. The guide notes that at-home OET is not accepted by AHPRA.

Two sittings rule

AHPRA can accept a maximum of two test sittings within 12 months, with all four sub-tests taken at each sitting.

Before booking: AHPRA also accepts IELTS Academic, PTE Academic, TOEFL iBT and Cambridge tests under specific rules. Verify the current standard and your National Board page before paying for any test.
Medical Board + AMC

Doctors: Medical Board and AMC pathway

For IMGs with a recognised primary medical qualification listed in the World Directory of Medical Schools. The main routes are Standard Pathway, Competent Authority Pathway and Specialist Pathway.

StepWhat to prepare
1Confirm your medical school is acceptable to AMC for your graduation year.
2Create an AMC account and establish your AMC portfolio.
3Complete Primary Source Verification through ECFMG EPIC / MyIntealth.
4Meet the AHPRA English Language Skills standard with OET Medicine or another approved test.
5For Standard Pathway, pass AMC CAT MCQ, then AMC Clinical Exam or an AMC-accredited WBA.
6Apply through AHPRA for limited or provisional registration and complete 12 months / minimum 47 weeks FTE supervised practice.
7For Competent Authority Pathway, confirm category A to F eligibility, secure supervised employment and complete the supervised practice route to general registration.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: referral, discharge and transfer letters with selective relevance and Australian clinical register.
  • Speaking: chronic disease, bad news, shared decision making, patient concerns and Indigenous health considerations.
  • Reading: clinical guidelines, drug information and workplace texts.
  • Listening: multi-accent medical consultations and ward briefings.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Choosing Standard Pathway when Competent Authority Pathway could avoid AMC exams.
  • Booking AMC Clinical before checking whether WBA and supervised practice are a better fit.
  • Using an OET sitting around the April 2026 transition without checking the correct score table.
  • Forgetting to release OET scores to AMC / Medical Board in the myOET portal.
NMBA + AHPRA

Nurses and midwives: NMBA outcomes-based assessment

For internationally qualified nurses and midwives who want RN, EN or midwife registration. Since March 2020, NMBA has used Outcomes-Based Assessment instead of the old bridging-program model.

StepWhat to prepare
1Complete the free AHPRA Self-Check on the IQNM Assessment Portal.
2If referred, pay the assessment fee and submit documents and portfolio.
3Complete Orientation Part 1 and receive the OBA referral letter if eligible.
4Pass the NCLEX-RN cognitive component.
5Meet the AHPRA English Language Skills standard with OET Nursing or another approved test.
6Travel to Adelaide Health Simulation for the OSCE behavioural component.
7Complete Orientation Part 2 and submit final NMBA registration documents for registration and AHPRA number.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: nursing referrals, discharge letters and patient education letters in a nursing voice.
  • Speaking: patient education, handover, distressed patients and culturally safe communication.
  • Reading: care plans, infection-control bulletins and workplace policies.
  • Listening: nurse-patient interactions and Australian ward briefings.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Skipping Self-Check before planning OBA.
  • Selecting the wrong graduation year or degree title and being misclassified.
  • Booking OSCE before passing NCLEX-RN.
  • Travelling for OSCE on an unsuitable visa or without checking attendance rules.
Pharmacy Board + APC

Pharmacists: Pharmacy Board and APC Knowledge Stream

For internationally qualified pharmacists outside Australia, Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, the UK or the USA who need the APC Knowledge Stream toward provisional registration.

2025 change: OPRA replaced KAPS from March 2025. Confirm the current APC route before paying for any exam.
StepWhat to prepare
1Confirm a recognised pharmacy qualification of at least four years full-time tertiary study and current or eligible home registration.
2Apply for APC Skills Assessment eligibility check through the APC Candidate Portal.
3Receive the Skills Assessment Eligibility Letter and book OPRA.
4Meet AHPRA English evidence with OET Pharmacy or another approved test.
5Pass OPRA, the computer-based exam that replaced KAPS from March 2025.
6Apply through AHPRA for provisional registration and approved supervised practice.
7Complete the internship and pass Intern Written and Intern Oral exams within the required window for general registration.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: community pharmacy letters to GPs, supplier requests and counselling notes.
  • Speaking: medication counselling, adherence, side effects and OTC sale refusals.
  • Reading: AMH-style entries, TGA alerts, medicine information and workplace policies.
  • Listening: pharmacist-patient counselling, ward conversations and pharmacy handovers.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Booking retired KAPS instead of OPRA.
  • Trying to book OPRA without a valid eligibility letter.
  • Missing the Intern Written and Intern Oral timing window.
  • Using OET Medicine or Nursing material instead of OET Pharmacy material.
Physio Board + APC

Physiotherapists: APEP and Physiotherapy Board route

For internationally qualified physiotherapists with an entry-level physiotherapy qualification and unrestricted right to practise who want Australian physiotherapy registration.

2025 change: APEP replaced the old Standard Assessment Pathway for new applicants from 1 October 2025.
StepWhat to prepare
1Confirm eligibility with a diploma-level or higher physiotherapy qualification and unrestricted right to practise.
2Apply to the Australian Physiotherapy Council for Eligibility Assessment.
3Complete Cultural Safety Training online.
4Use the APC Interim Certificate to apply for AHPRA limited registration for supervised practice where applicable.
5Meet the AHPRA English Language Skills standard with OET Physiotherapy or another approved test.
6Pass the APEP Written Assessment and Remote Capability Assessment.
7Attend the one-day Clinical Workshop in Melbourne and then apply for general registration.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: referrals, progress letters and discharge summaries in physiotherapy register.
  • Speaking: diagnosis, prognosis, home exercises, adherence and chronic pain conversations.
  • Reading: rehab guidelines, return-to-work guidance and clinical workplace texts.
  • Listening: physiotherapy consultations and multidisciplinary team briefings.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Preparing for the retired Standard Assessment Pathway instead of APEP.
  • Assuming the Clinical Workshop can be completed remotely.
  • Assuming FLYR eligibility without checking the current APC country list.
  • Preparing with non-physiotherapy OET role-play material.

Candidate checklist before you start

  • Confirm your profession route before choosing a test date.
  • Check your OET profession version and current AHPRA score table.
  • Prepare passport, qualification, transcript, registration and experience evidence early.
  • Plan PSV, council assessment, exams and supervised practice timing together.
  • Keep official AHPRA, National Board and assessment council links as your final source.

Official sources to verify

  • AHPRA registration process and English Language Skills standard.
  • Medical Board of Australia and Australian Medical Council for doctors.
  • NMBA and AHPRA IQNM Self-Check for nurses and midwives.
  • Australian Pharmacy Council and Pharmacy Board for pharmacists.
  • Australian Physiotherapy Council and Physiotherapy Board for physiotherapists.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for Australia pathway candidates planning OET and registration.

Is OET accepted for Australia healthcare registration?

OET is accepted across the AHPRA professions covered here, but the exact score table depends on test date and National Board rules. Verify the current AHPRA standard before booking.

What changed in 2026 for AHPRA English scores?

The guide notes a score transition from 23 April 2026. Tests on or before 22 April 2026 follow the previous grade table; tests on or after 23 April 2026 use numerical OET scores.

Can I combine two OET sittings for AHPRA?

AHPRA can accept a maximum of two sittings within a 12-month period when each sitting includes all four sub-tests. Always confirm the current standard before relying on combined results.

Which profession version of OET should I book?

Book the version that matches your profession: Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy or Physiotherapy. Using another profession version can make your preparation and evidence unsuitable.

Can Dr Hesham help with Australia OET preparation?

Yes. Support can focus on your profession, weak sub-tests, writing corrections, speaking role-plays and a realistic timeline before your test date.
