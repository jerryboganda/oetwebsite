This policy explains how OET with Dr. Ahmed Hesham ("we", "us", "our", or "the Platform") uses cookies and similar technologies at https://www.oetwithdrhesham.co.uk.

Essential cookies are always active because the site cannot work without them. All other cookies are used only where you have given consent, which you can change at any time.

01

1. What Cookies Are

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website. They keep you logged in, remember your preferences, support security, and help us understand how the site is used. We use similar technologies, such as pixels and local storage, for the same purposes.

02

2. The Cookies We Use

Type What they do Consent needed?
Essential Core site function and navigation No — always on
Login & session Keep you signed in and maintain your session across the Platform No — always on
Security Detect suspicious activity and help prevent fraud and unauthorised access No — always on
Performance & analytics Show us how the site is used so we can improve it Yes
Marketing Measure campaigns and show relevant content, where applicable Yes
03

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies are set by trusted providers acting on our behalf, such as analytics services and secure payment providers like Stripe and PayPal. These providers have their own privacy and cookie policies, which we encourage you to review.

04

4. Managing Your Preferences

You can manage non-essential cookies through any consent tool on our website, or through your browser settings, which let you block or delete cookies. Disabling essential or session cookies may stop parts of the Platform from working correctly.

05

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy to reflect changes in technology, law, or our practices. The "Last updated" date reflects the current version.
