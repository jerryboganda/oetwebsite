OET paper test centres 2027: the 16 countries and their venues

From January 2027, OET on Paper continues for Nursing and Medicine only. According to an OET customer-care email of November 2026, it stays available in 16 countries. This page lists those countries by region and the paper venues OET’s own finder shows in each one.

A listed venue is not a promise of a date or a seat, and OET’s data does not show whether an older paper venue continues in 2027. Confirm and book through the official finder. Last verified: 27 November 2026.

The rule from January 2027

OET published the change on 16 November 2026 (oet.com).

OET on Paper

  • From January 2027 it continues for Nursing and Medicine only.
  • Bookings for 2027 paper tests are open for those two professions, according to the oet.com homepage on 27 November 2026.

OET on Computer

  • It remains available for all 12 healthcare professions at approved test venues, subject to availability.
  • The test content, length and marking are the same in both modes.

The 16 countries, by region

OET’s announcement does not name any country, and no page on oet.com lists them. The list below comes from an email from OET’s customer care team (Cambridge Boxhill Language Assessment) in November 2026: from 2027, OET on Paper is available for Nursing and Medicine candidates in these 16 countries only.

The list limits paper, not computer

OET on Computer stays available for all 12 professions at approved venues, including in countries that are not on this list. See the full test-centre directory.

Being on the list guarantees nothing

Paper still depends on approved venues, dates and seats in each country. If OET publishes the list on oet.com, or changes it, the official page is the one to follow.

How to read the January 2027 flag

OET’s finder holds two kinds of paper record. They do not mean the same thing.

Flagged: added for 2027

33 paper records carry the title “Online Speaking test at venue” and a note about January 2027. They are the newest paper records in OET’s data and they match the announcement that Speaking may run over a live video connection at the venue. We read them as the venues set up for the 2027 paper test. That is our reading of the titles and the note, not a statement by OET.

Not flagged: older paper listings

The other paper records are older listings. OET’s data has no end date and no status for them, so it does not show whether each one continues after December 2026. Never assume that a centre which ran paper tests in 2026 will run them in 2027.

Turkey and Sri Lanka

The flagged records appear in 14 of the 16 countries. Turkey and Sri Lanka each have one older paper record and no flagged record yet. Both are on the customer-care list, so check the finder for a 2027 paper date before you plan on paper there.

What to do with a flag

Treat it as a strong hint, not as availability. Search your city in the official finder and check that it offers a bookable paper date for your profession.

If your profession is not Nursing or Medicine

Your format is the computer test

Pharmacists, physiotherapists, dentists, radiographers, vets and the other professions take OET on Computer at an approved venue from January 2027, wherever they are. Find computer venues by country and city in the OET test-centre directory.

Switch your preparation now

Type every practice letter, read on screen and practise Listening with headphones. See computer vs paper: how to prepare and the computer-based practice.

OET@Home is a separate question

Whether you can book OET@Home, and whether your regulator accepts it, are two different rules. Neither follows from the paper change. Read the OET@Home guide before you count on it.

Speaking and Listening on paper from 2027

What OET’s announcement says about test day.

Speaking

Paper candidates still attend an approved venue for the Speaking sub-test. Depending on the venue, you meet the interlocutor in person or over a live video connection.

Listening

At venues where Speaking runs over video, candidates are given headphones for both Speaking and Listening. The Listening audio plays through the headphones, with timing information shown on screen.

Booking, ID and fee

Paper tests are booked through your myOET account, bring the same original ID you booked with, and OET’s fee is AU$587. The steps and the ID rule are in the test-centre directory. Nurses and doctors can prepare with the OET course for nurses or the OET course for doctors.

Frequently asked questions

Which countries have OET paper test centres in 2027?

According to an OET customer-care email from November 2026, OET on Paper continues from January 2027 in 16 countries: Egypt, Saudi Arabia, the United Arab Emirates, Turkey, Ghana, Kenya, the United Kingdom, Ireland, India, Pakistan, Nepal, Sri Lanka, the Philippines, Australia, New Zealand and Canada. The list is not published on oet.com.

Who can take OET on Paper from January 2027?

Nursing and Medicine candidates only. OET announced this on 16 November 2026. The other ten professions take OET on Computer at an approved venue.

Will my 2026 paper test centre still run paper tests in 2027?

Do not assume so. OET's venue data does not show whether an older paper listing continues in 2027. Search the official finder for your city and check that a 2027 paper date is offered before you plan around it.

What does the January 2027 flag on a venue mean?

It marks a paper venue record that OET added with the title Online Speaking test at venue and a note about January 2027. We read these as venues set up for the 2027 paper test with Speaking by video at the venue. That is our reading of OET's data, not a statement by OET.

What are the OET paper test dates in January 2027?

OET has published 23 and 30 January 2027 in its Global table and 22, 23, 29 and 30 January 2027 in its Americas table, for Nursing and Medicine. Bookings close 24 days before the test date, subject to change.

Is Speaking by video at a paper venue the same as OET@Home?

No. You still attend an approved venue for every sub-test. At some venues the Speaking interlocutor joins over a live video connection. OET@Home is a different test mode, taken on your own computer.

Is a paper seat guaranteed in the 16 countries?

No. Paper availability depends on approved venues and dates in each country, and seats can fill. Confirm in the official finder before you plan around a paper date.
