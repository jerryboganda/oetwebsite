Who is this OET course for doctors for?

The course is built for international medical graduates who need OET Medicine as English evidence and want a structured route through all four sub-tests.

Doctors applying to the GMC

If you are preparing for GMC registration through PLAB or another approved route, OET Medicine is one of the accepted English tests. The UK pathway guide explains where OET fits in the GMC route.

Doctors on the ECFMG pathway

ECFMG accepts OET Medicine for its certification pathways. The USA pathway guide covers ECFMG, USMLE and the state-based licensing route.

Doctors heading to Australia or the Gulf

OET Medicine is also used for medical registration in other countries. Check your destination in the Australia pathway guide or the Gulf pathway guide before booking.

What OET score do doctors need?

For the UK, the GMC asks for grade B in all four OET Medicine sub-tests in one sitting. For the USA, ECFMG asks for 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing, also in one sitting.

How do doctors pass OET Writing?

OET Medicine Writing gives you 45 minutes (5 minutes of reading time and 40 minutes of writing) to turn case notes into one letter of about 180–200 words, usually to a medical colleague who will take over or continue care.

Referral to a specialist

Summarise the presenting problem, relevant history, investigations and management so far, then state exactly what you want the specialist to do and how urgently.

Discharge or referral back to the GP

Tell the GP what happened in hospital, what changed, and what follow-up, monitoring or medication review is needed in the community.

Transfer and urgent referral letters

Lead with the reason for transfer or the urgent concern, then give only the details the receiving team needs to act safely.

What the examiner checks

Purpose, content, conciseness and clarity, genre and style, organisation and layout, and language. Doctors often lose marks by writing a chronological history instead of a letter organised around the reader’s task.

The course trains a repeatable method for letter purpose, case-note selection and a specific final request on medical tasks, and the OET Writing tips show the core rules. If your letters keep landing just below B, OET writing correction shows you exactly what is costing marks.

What consultations will you practise for OET Speaking?

OET Speaking has two role-plays. You get 3 minutes to prepare each card and about 5 minutes to complete it, with the interlocutor playing the patient or a relative.

Typical Medicine scenarios

  • Explaining a diagnosis or test result in patient-friendly language
  • Discussing investigations and treatment options
  • Breaking bad news with empathy and a clear plan
  • Responding to a worried or angry relative
  • Persuading a reluctant patient, for example about smoking or medication adherence

How you are assessed

Examiners score clinical communication (relationship building, understanding the patient’s perspective, providing structure, gathering information and giving information) as well as intelligibility, fluency, appropriate language and grammar. Doctors usually need to replace medical jargon with plain explanations and check the patient’s understanding.

The course includes 100+ Speaking cards across different scenarios, and the live 1:1 session lets you rehearse them with feedback. Read the OET Speaking tips for the role-play method before your session.

How do you prepare for OET Listening and Reading?

Listening and Reading are the same for every profession, so doctors sit exactly the same papers as other candidates. What changes your score is method, not luck.

Listening (about 40 minutes)

Part A is note completion from two consultations, Part B is short workplace extracts and Part C is longer presentations or interviews. Train prediction before each extract and write exactly what you hear, with correct spelling. See the OET Listening tips.

Reading (60 minutes)

Part A is a strict 15-minute scanning task across four short texts; Parts B and C test detail, gist and inference. Use keyword anchors in Part A and eliminate distractors in Parts B and C. See the OET Reading tips.

The course practice library gives you timed papers with answer keys and rationales, and the separate Listening and Reading practice packages add more papers when you need volume before the exam.

OET course for doctors FAQ

Short answers before you enrol. More exam questions are answered on the full OET FAQ page.

What OET score do doctors need for the GMC?

The GMC asks for OET Medicine with grade B in all four sub-tests (Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking) achieved in one sitting. Paper, computer and OET@Home results are accepted, and results are valid for 2 years.

Is OET accepted by ECFMG?

Yes. For the 2027 pathways ECFMG accepts OET Medicine with 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking and 300 in Writing, in one sitting taken on or after 1 January 2025. From 14 May 2026 you need a MyIntealth ID to release your scores.

Is the OET Medicine course live or recorded?

The Medicine course is recorded, with 40+ hours of lessons you can replay for 6 months. It also includes 5 personalised Writing letter assessments and one private live 1:1 Speaking session.

What is the difference between the Medicine course and the TutorBook bundle?

Both include the full recorded Medicine course. The TutorBook bundle adds TutorBook, a 2026 recall-based book built around 8 full OET exams with model answers, rationales and Listening scripts, as a personalised PDF with permanent access.

Can I add more letter corrections or Speaking sessions?

Yes. You can add 3, 5, 7 or 10 Writing letter assessments to an active course, book one or two private 1:1 Speaking sessions, and sit 1, 3 or 5 full mock exams.
