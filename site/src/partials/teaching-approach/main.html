A practical OET method, not random English practice

The goal is to understand the exam task, identify relevant clinical information, write with purpose, speak with safe patient-centred communication and receive feedback that changes your next attempt.

Orientation first

Know the exam, the marking logic and the preparation path before you waste time on scattered materials.

Case-note strategy

Learn how to select, organise and prioritise patient details instead of copying everything.

Writing correction

Improve with correction-led feedback on purpose, tone, layout, grammar and task achievement.

Speaking confidence

Practice clinical role plays with clear explanations, empathy, safety advice and natural structure.

How the learning journey works

The page is designed to convert confusion into a clear preparation route.

1

Understand the task

Use orientation lessons to understand OET expectations and avoid common preparation mistakes.

2

Diagnose weak areas

Find whether writing, speaking, reading, listening, grammar or timing is holding you back.

3

Practice with feedback

Move from passive watching to corrected writing, role-play practice and targeted improvement.

4

Check readiness

Book the exam when your performance is more controlled, not when you are still guessing.

Who this approach is for

Doctors, nurses, pharmacists, physiotherapists and allied health professionals who want structured OET preparation rather than random tips.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you choose your next OET preparation step.

What makes this teaching approach different?

It starts with exam orientation and then builds practical writing, speaking and case-note skills through feedback. The focus is not generic English; it is healthcare communication for OET tasks.

Which video should I watch first?

If you are new to OET, start with the general orientation session. Doctors should also watch the doctor orientation. If writing is your main concern, move next to Routine Referral 1 and 2.

Can I get direct feedback after watching the free videos?

Yes. Use WhatsApp to ask about your profession, target country, exam date and weakest module. You can then be guided toward writing correction, speaking practice or readiness planning.

Not sure how to start your OET preparation?

Send your profession, exam target and weakest module. We will guide you to the right orientation video, practice route and next preparation step.
