OET Recalls: FAQ

What are OET recalls?

Recalls are the exam topics that candidates remember and report after their test. They show the real topics being asked right now, so you can practise with material that matches the current exam, not old question banks.

How often is this page updated?

After every OET test date. The page keeps roughly the last two months of topics, so what you see here reflects the current exam.

Are these official OET questions?

No. They are topics reported by our students from their own exam experience, shared for practice purposes. They are not official OET content and are not endorsed by OET.
