Online OET course FAQ

Quick answers before you choose a course.

Are the OET courses live or recorded?

The full courses are recorded video lessons with structured study materials, so you can repeat any lesson during your access period. The personalised parts are Writing letter assessments, private one-to-one live Speaking sessions and Q&A support.

How long do I have access to a course?

Full recorded courses give you 6 months of access from purchase. TutorBook is a personalised PDF with permanent access and content updates.

Which OET course is best for nurses?

Most nurses start with the Nursing Course + Assessment Package, which adds 5 Writing letter assessments and a private Speaking session to the Full Nursing OET Course. The Nursing Premium Bundle also adds the Basic English foundation course and TutorBook.

Do the courses include writing correction?

The full Medicine, Physiotherapy and Allied Health courses include 5 Writing letter assessments and 1 private Speaking session, and so does the Nursing Course + Assessment Package. You can add more letter assessments to an eligible course at any time.

What if my English is not ready for OET yet?

Take the free placement test to see your CEFR level. Candidates at Beginner to B1 level can start with the Basic English Course, which covers grammar, healthcare vocabulary and sentence formation before full OET preparation.
