Where to take OET in Qatar

  • Test Orbits (an authorised Prometric test centre), Al Emadi Business Center, C Ring Road, 1st floor, Doha: OET on Computer

Qatar is on OET's list of countries where OET on Computer candidates can take Speaking at the venue. The Department of Healthcare Professions gives the same building as the Prometric centre for its own qualifying exam, so many candidates will already know it. Single sub-test re-sits exist only for OET on Paper, so they cannot be booked at a computer-only venue.

Venues as listed by the OET venue finder on 19 November 2026. Bookings close 24 days before an OET on Paper test and 7 days before an OET on Computer test. Venues, dates and seats change, so confirm in the finder before you plan travel. For the choice between the two formats see OET on Computer vs OET on Paper, and for dates see OET test dates and fees.

Does the DHP ask for OET?

The Department of Healthcare Professions (DHP) guidelines for nurses, physicians and pharmacists that we checked on 19 November 2026 contain no English test requirement. The only language rule is that documents in other languages must be translated to Arabic or English. An employer may still ask for a score, so get it in writing.

The DHP route is: apply through its electronic registration and licensing system; complete primary source verification, which is mandatory (DHP names Dataflow, Quadrabay and Approved Verification Services); and pass the Prometric qualifying exam, which is 150 multiple-choice questions in three hours with an unlimited number of attempts. A preliminary evaluation is valid for six months. Our Gulf pathway guide has the pass marks and the exemption rules.

How the course works from Qatar

The course is online, so nothing depends on where you live in Qatar. Lessons are recorded and specific to your profession, and you can watch them at any hour. In the packages that include personal Writing assessments, Dr Ahmed Hesham assesses your letters himself and returns an estimated score, a detailed correction and voice-note feedback. Private Speaking sessions are live and one to one with an expert tutor: when you book, give your local time (UTC+3 all year).

Website practice follows the computer-based test for Listening and Reading and includes live virtual patient Speaking role-plays. Support is by WhatsApp in English or Arabic, and the same account works in the web app and the iOS and Android apps.

OET in Qatar: FAQ

Where can I take OET in Qatar?

On 19 November 2026 the OET venue finder listed one venue in Qatar: Test Orbits, an authorised Prometric test centre at Al Emadi Business Center on C Ring Road in Doha. It is for OET on Computer. Check the OET venue finder before you book.

Does Qatar require OET for a DHP licence?

The DHP guidelines for nurses, physicians and pharmacists that we checked contain no English test requirement. An employer may set its own, and regulators abroad do ask for OET.

Can I take OET on paper in Qatar?

No OET on Paper venue was listed for Qatar on 19 November 2026. The Doha venue is for OET on Computer, and Speaking can be taken there.

How much does OET cost in Qatar?

OET charges AU$587 for the full test. OET publishes no price in Qatari riyals, so your bank sets the exchange rate.
