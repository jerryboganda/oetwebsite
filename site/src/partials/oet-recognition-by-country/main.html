Who accepts OET? The 12 professions by country and regulator

OET is not accepted "by a country". It is accepted, or not, by one regulator for one profession. A nurse and a dentist heading to the same country can get opposite answers.

This page takes each of the 12 OET professions and shows, destination by destination, which regulator names OET, at what scores, and where the regulator's own website does not say. Where we could not confirm a point, the page says "not confirmed". It never leaves a blank for you to fill with hope.

Last verified: 27 November 2026, against each regulator's own page. Recognition and scores change without notice, so open the source link before you book.

OET has 12 profession versions

OET is an English test for healthcare professionals, and you sit it in the version for your own profession. The 12 versions are:

Recognition is decided regulator by regulator

A test version existing for your profession does not mean your regulator accepts it. The General Dental Council in the UK, for example, names only IELTS in its guidance, although OET Dentistry exists. In Canada, the pharmacy policy names OET while the physiotherapy and occupational therapy bodies do not list it.

"Not confirmed" is an answer, not a gap

OET's own website says its results are accepted worldwide and points to an organisation finder. That is OET's list. We rely on what each regulator publishes itself. When a regulator's page does not name OET for your profession, or we could not read the page, the status here is "not confirmed", never "accepted".

How to read the statuses

Every row in the matrix carries one status. They mean different things, and we keep them apart.

StatusWhat it meansWhat to do
AcceptedThe regulator's own page names OET for this profession and prints the scores.Read the scores, the validity period and the sitting rule on the source page, then book.
ConditionalOET is named, but with a condition that changes the answer for some candidates: a start date, scores not yet published, or a limit on the test format.Read the condition first. Do not book until you know you meet it.
Not acceptedThe regulator says it accepts only other tests, or says it does not accept OET.Take one of the tests the regulator names.
Not listedThe regulator publishes its list of English tests and OET is not on it.Treat it as not accepted unless the regulator tells you otherwise in writing.
Not confirmedWe could not read the regulator's page, the page is silent, or the only evidence is second-hand.Ask the regulator in writing before paying for any test.

Destination by destination

A short reading of each destination, with the guide that covers the full route. The matrix above has the scores.

United Kingdom

The GMC (doctors), the NMC (nurses, midwives and nursing associates) and the GPhC (pharmacists) each name OET. The HCPC lists OET scores for six professions: podiatry, dietetics, occupational therapy, speech and language therapy, physiotherapy and radiography. The General Dental Council's guidance names only IELTS and does not mention OET, so dentistry is not confirmed. Optometry and veterinary science in the UK are not confirmed.

UK pathway guide

Ireland

Ireland has the widest published coverage we found. NMBI (nurses and midwives), the Medical Council, the PSI (pharmacists) and the Dental Council each name OET, and CORU names it for physiotherapy, occupational therapy, dietetics, optometry, podiatry, radiography and speech and language therapy. For veterinary science, we could not read the Veterinary Council of Ireland's page: not confirmed.

Ireland pathway guide

Australia

Ahpra says all National Boards, except the Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Health Practice Board, accept the same tests at the same scores. For tests taken on or after 23 April 2026 that is Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350 and Speaking 360, and OET@Home is not accepted. A profession without its own OET version can use the OET test of another registered profession. ANMAC names OET for the nursing and midwifery skills assessment. If your profession is not registered through a National Board, OET recognition is not confirmed here.

Australia pathway guide

New Zealand

Eleven of the 12 professions have a board that names OET: medicine, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy, dentistry, occupational therapy, radiography, dietetics, podiatry, optometry and veterinary science. Scores and sitting rules differ from board to board, and several boards say nothing about OET@Home. For speech pathology we read no primary source: not confirmed.

New Zealand pathway guide

United States

ECFMG accepts OET Medicine for its 2027 Pathways. TruMerit, formerly CGFNS, lists OET for nurses in its Certification Program and does not accept at-home tests. NABP will accept OET Pharmacy for FPGEC certification for tests taken on or after 11 January 2027, but had not published the minimum scores when we checked: see OET for pharmacists in the USA. State boards of nursing and the other nine professions are not confirmed.

USA pathway guide

Canada

There is no national licence, so the answer changes by province. The medical colleges of British Columbia and Alberta name OET Medicine. The nursing regulators of Ontario and British Columbia name OET, while Alberta's (CRNA) lists only CELBAN and IELTS Academic. The NAPRA model policy and the Ontario College of Pharmacists name OET for pharmacists. The bodies we read for physiotherapy, occupational therapy, dentistry in Ontario and veterinary medicine do not list OET. Other provinces and professions are not confirmed.

Canada pathway guide

Singapore

The Singapore Medical Council, Pharmacy Council and Dental Council name OET, and the Allied Health Professions Council names it for physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy and radiography. All ask for the scores in a single sitting. The Singapore Nursing Board's page for foreign-trained nurses lists no English test at all, so OET for nurses is not confirmed. Dietetics, podiatry, optometry and veterinary science are not confirmed.

Singapore pathway guide

The Gulf

We found no published OET requirement on the Gulf regulators' websites as of 27 November 2026. That covers the DHA, DOH and MOHAP in the United Arab Emirates, the SCFHS in Saudi Arabia, the DHP in Qatar, the OMSB and Ministry of Health in Oman, the Ministry of Health in Kuwait and the NHRA in Bahrain. Licensing runs through each regulator's own exam and credential verification. This is not the same as "OET is not accepted": confirm with the regulator or your employer.

Immigration is separate from registration

A visa office and a health regulator are two different readers of your result. Meeting one does not meet the other.

Australia: Department of Home Affairs

  • Accepts OET for visa purposes at its own scores.
  • Tests on or after 7 August 2025: Competent English is Listening 290, Reading 310, Writing 290, Speaking 330. Proficient English is 350, 360, 380 and 360.
  • Results from within the three years before the visa application, depending on the visa subclass.
  • OET@Home is not accepted.
  • Proficient English needs Writing 380, while Ahpra registration needs Writing 350.
  • Source: Home Affairs.

Immigration New Zealand

  • Lists OET for skilled residence visas.
  • Principal applicants need grade C+ or higher in all four skills.
  • The test must be sat in person at a test centre, and the result must be no more than two years old.
  • C+ is lower than the New Zealand health regulators' requirements, so a visa-level score does not satisfy registration.
  • Other visa categories: not confirmed.
  • Source: Immigration New Zealand.

Canada: IRCC

  • IRCC does not list OET as an approved language test for Express Entry.
  • The approved English tests on its page are CELPIP, IELTS and PTE Core.
  • If you need both immigration and registration, you may need two different tests.
  • Other IRCC programmes: not confirmed.
  • Source: IRCC (page dated 16 October 2026).

What to do when your profession or regulator is "not confirmed"

"Not confirmed" does not mean no. It means nobody should spend your test fee on an assumption.

Ask the regulator four questions, in writing

  • Do you accept OET for my profession, and which OET profession version?
  • What is the minimum score in each sub-test, and how old can the result be?
  • Must all scores come from one sitting, or can two be combined?
  • Do you accept OET@Home, or only a test taken at a test centre?

While you wait for the answer

  • Do not rely on a forum post, an agent or another preparation site. Keep the regulator's written reply.
  • Check whether your employer or visa route asks for a different test from the regulator.
  • If the regulator names another test only, prepare for that test. Your clinical English practice still counts.
  • Keep preparing: free practice and the free placement test cost you nothing while you wait.

Frequently asked questions

Which countries accept OET?

No country accepts OET as a whole. Each health regulator decides for its own profession. On the pages we read on 27 November 2026, regulators in the United Kingdom, Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, the United States, Canada and Singapore name OET for at least one profession.

Is OET accepted for all 12 professions?

No. OET has 12 profession versions, but a regulator may name only some of them. A profession that a regulator does not list is not confirmed, and you should ask the regulator before booking.

Is OET accepted in Canada?

Partly. Some provincial regulators for medicine, nursing and pharmacy name OET. The national bodies we read for physiotherapy, occupational therapy and veterinary medicine do not list it, and IRCC does not list OET for Express Entry.

Do Gulf regulators require OET?

We found no published OET requirement on the Gulf regulators' websites as of 27 November 2026. Licensing there runs through each regulator's own exam and credential verification. Confirm with the regulator or your employer.

Can I use OET for a visa?

Sometimes. The Australian Department of Home Affairs and Immigration New Zealand accept OET at their own scores, which differ from the registration scores. IRCC does not list OET for Express Entry to Canada.

Does the General Dental Council accept OET?

Not confirmed. The GDC's guidance names only IELTS and does not mention OET. It says it will consider other credible evidence, which is not a promise. Ask the GDC before you book.

Is OET accepted for nurses in the USA?

TruMerit, formerly CGFNS, lists OET for its Certification Program at Listening 320, Reading 340, Writing 350 and Speaking 360, and does not accept at-home tests. State boards of nursing set their own rules, which we have not confirmed.
