How to read this directory

Four things to know before you rely on any venue below.

Where the data comes from

Every venue here was read from OET’s official venue finder. Names and addresses are shown as OET keys them, including its spelling and capitalisation. Last checked: .

What a listing proves

That the venue was in OET’s finder, with that name, city, address and test mode, on the date we checked.

What a listing does not prove

  • That a test date or a seat is open at that venue.
  • Which professions the venue offers: OET’s venue data shows no professions and no dates for each venue.
  • That an older paper venue will still run paper tests in 2027.

How to book an OET test

The official steps, from OET’s booking help article and its Test Regulations.

1. Search the finder

Open oet.com/test/book-a-test, enter your city and country and select Find a test. Filter by test mode or distance, then select Book now on the venue you want.

2. OET on Paper

Register or log in to your myOET account, choose Apply for the test and follow the prompts. Paper candidates upload a photo. Payment must be completed within 15 minutes to secure the booking.

3. OET on Computer and OET@Home

Book through OET’s booking portal from your OET account. You upload an image of your ID, and you have 15 minutes to complete the booking once you select a date.

4. Confirmation

A confirmation email follows, so check your junk folder. OET recommends booking on a desktop or laptop, with a Visa or MasterCard and your ID to hand. You can hold one booking for each test administration, across all modes.

ID on test day

OET publishes one general ID rule, not a country-by-country table (test day information, ID help article).

What is accepted

  • A valid passport.
  • Or a valid national ID issued by the government of the country where you sit the test, showing your full name, photograph and date of birth on the one document.
  • For OET on Computer and OET@Home, a national ID must be in English lettering. If it is not, bring a certified translated copy together with the original.

What catches candidates out

  • The ID you present must be the ID you booked with, and it must be the original document.
  • A driving licence is not accepted. OET states one exception for candidates testing in the United States, where REAL ID-compliant licences are accepted.
  • With the wrong ID you may be refused entry, and OET says you may not be entitled to a deferral or refund.
National ID cards

OET does not publish a rule for any specific country’s ID card. If you plan to use a national ID instead of a passport, check it against the rule above before you book, and use your passport if you are unsure.

The OET test fee

OET states a test fee of AU$587 for OET on Paper, OET on Computer and OET@Home. In the United States the fee is US$455 for OET on Computer at a venue and for OET@Home (OET fee help article).

OET publishes no tax amount and no local-currency price, so we do not state one. Your bank’s exchange rate decides what you pay in your own currency. For the full picture see OET exam fees and OET test dates and fees.

How the Speaking sub-test is delivered

OET on Computer at a venue

You sit Listening, Reading and Writing at the venue. Speaking is taken at home on a secure platform or, in select countries, at the venue (oet.com). OET asks you to make sure the Speaking mode you select is accepted by the organisation you are applying to.

Countries with Speaking at a venue

In an oet.com post dated 20 July 2026, OET listed: Kenya, South Africa, New Zealand, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Australia (Melbourne, Sydney), Nigeria (Lagos), the Philippines, Uganda, Nepal, Ghana (Accra), Bangladesh, India (Mumbai, Gurgaon) and Pakistan (Islamabad, Karachi and Lahore). Egypt, the United Arab Emirates, the United Kingdom, Ireland, Canada, Turkey and Sri Lanka are not on that list. Re-check before booking.

OET on Paper

Speaking is at the venue. Until December 2026 it is face to face. From January 2027 it is in person or over a live video connection, depending on the venue.

OET@Home

Speaking runs on the OET Speaking platform with a live interlocutor.

Speaking may be on a different day

For OET on Computer and OET@Home, Speaking can fall on a different day from the other three sub-tests. OET’s own pages give different figures for the gap, so rely on the date and time shown in your booking.

Rescheduling and cancelling, in brief

From section 9 of OET’s Test Regulations, read on 27 November 2026. Fees and deadlines can change, so read the current regulations before you act.

Rescheduling

  • A full test costs AU$120 to reschedule before the booking closing date and AU$200 after it.
  • OET on Paper cannot be rescheduled after 11:59pm local time on the day before the test.
  • OET on Computer: AU$200 applies up to 48 hours before the test and, through customer care, inside the last 48 hours.
  • You cannot change test mode or profession by rescheduling, or move a computer or OET@Home test to another country. Cancel and rebook instead.

Cancelling

  • The cancellation fee is AU$120 before the booking closing date and AU$200 after it.
  • OET on Paper can be cancelled up to one week before the test day.
  • OET on Computer and OET@Home can be cancelled up to 48 hours before the test start time, and not once a sub-test has been taken.
  • A no-show without extenuating circumstances gets no refund.

More detail: OET cancellation and refund policy.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find OET test centres near me?

Use OET's official venue finder at oet.com/test/book-a-test: enter your city and country and select Find a test. This page lists the venues we read in that finder on 27 November 2026 for 26 countries, grouped by country and city.

Does a venue on this list mean I can book a seat there?

No. A listing shows that the venue is in OET's finder. It does not show that a date or a seat is open. Confirm in the finder before you plan travel.

Are you affiliated with any OET test centre?

No. We are an independent OET coaching service. We are not affiliated with OET or with any test centre, the centres are not our locations and we do not take test bookings.

How much is the OET test fee?

OET states a fee of AU$587 for each test mode. In the United States the fee is US$455 for OET on Computer at a venue and for OET@Home. OET does not publish a tax or local-currency price.

What ID do I need on test day?

A valid passport, or a valid national ID issued by the government of the country where you sit the test, showing your full name, photograph and date of birth. It must be the same original document you used to book.

My country has no OET venue. Can I take OET@Home?

Those are two separate questions. OET decides whether you can book OET@Home from your country and profession, and your regulator decides whether it accepts an OET@Home result. Check both before you book.

Which paper test dates are published for 2027?

When we checked on 27 November 2026, OET had published paper dates on 22, 23, 29 and 30 January 2027, for Nursing and Medicine only. Bookings for OET on Paper close 24 days before the test date.
