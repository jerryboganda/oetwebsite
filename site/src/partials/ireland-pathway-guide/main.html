Ireland overview

Choose your regulator first, then your test

In Ireland the regulator depends on your profession: NMBI for nurses and midwives, the Medical Council for doctors, PSI for pharmacists, CORU for seven of the allied health professions that OET covers, the Dental Council for dentists and the Veterinary Council of Ireland for vets. OET is accepted by the first five, each with a different score line. Confirm the accepted test, the score and the format on your regulator's page before you pay for any test.

Important: This guide is educational orientation only. It is not legal, immigration or regulator advice, and regulators can change their rules without notice. Rules at a glance

Validity, sittings and dates: what each regulator says

Every Irish regulator we could read limits the age of your result to about two years, but each one counts the two years from a different point. Check the wording for your own regulator.

How old can the result be?

NMBI: less than two years old. Medical Council: a single sitting within the last two years, with the certificate dated within two years of the date of registration. PSI: valid for two years from the test date. CORU: dated within two years of the day you submit your registration application. Dental Council: obtained no more than two years before you submit your application.

Which sittings can be combined?

CORU only: a maximum of two sittings, from the same test provider, the repeat within six months of the first, with no OET sub-test below C+. CORU also says the overall minimum must be met on both sittings and it does not accept single sub-test retakes. NMBI, the Medical Council and PSI want one sitting. The Dental Council does not say: not confirmed.

Dates printed by the regulators

Medical Council: its current English rules apply to applications received on or after 1 March 2025. NMBI: its English requirement applies from 2 April 2018. Dental Council: applicants from 1 November 2018 must show proficiency. PSI: from 1 June 2026 it no longer accepts a university letter saying the course was taught in English.

OET@Home

NMBI, the Medical Council, PSI and the Dental Council do not accept at-home tests. CORU's page says online or at-home versions are accepted for OET only, but it does not name OET@Home, so we treat it as not confirmed. More detail is in our OET@Home guide and our clubbing rules guide.

Before booking: the grades behind the numbers, as NMBI and CORU print them, are B from 350 and C+ from 300. Compare your target with other countries in our OET score requirements guide and our recognition by country table.

Nurses and midwives: NMBI

NMBIOET NursingB in three, C+ in oneOne result only

For nurses and midwives who qualified outside the EU. NMBI describes two stages: a qualification recognition application, then a registration application. English evidence is needed at the second stage. NMBI also lists ways to show English without a test: nursing education taught and examined in English in a country it recognises (Australia, Canada, New Zealand, the United Kingdom or the United States), or three years of post-registration practice in English within the last five years. See the OET course for nurses for preparation.

English-test planning: NMBI asks for OET Nursing with grade B in three components and C+ in one. Its rule sentence does not name which component may be C+, although the table on the same page shows Writing, so aim for B in all four or ask NMBI. The result must be less than two years old. NMBI accepts one test result only and does not accept combined results. It does not accept OET at home, IELTS online or the IELTS One Skill Retake. The IELTS Academic alternative is an overall 7.0, with 7.0 in three components and 6.5 in one.

Pathway steps

Check whether you need a testRead NMBI's three English routes first. If your education or recent practice already qualifies, you may not need OET at all.
Apply for qualification recognitionThis is NMBI's first stage. NMBI may then ask for a compensation measure, such as a period of adaptation or an aptitude test. We have not confirmed those details: read NMBI's own guidance.
Sit OET Nursing at a venue, oncePlan to reach the full score line in a single result, on paper or on computer at a test venue.
Apply for registration in timeSend your English evidence with the registration application while the result is still less than two years old.

Common mistakes

  • Planning to combine two OET results: NMBI accepts one result only.
  • Booking OET@Home: NMBI does not accept it.
  • Sitting OET so early that the result is over two years old at the registration stage.
  • Assuming the UK rule applies. The NMC and NMBI are different regulators: see our UK pathway guide.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Nursing referral, discharge and transfer letters with clear purpose and selective case notes.

Speaking

Patient education, reassurance and handover role-plays in a calm nursing register.

Reading

Fast Part A scanning, then careful reading of policies and care guidance.

Listening

Nurse and patient consultations and ward briefings in different accents.

Doctors: Medical Council

Medical CouncilOET MedicineB in each moduleSingle sitting

The Medical Council says its current English requirements apply to all new applications received on or after 1 March 2025. Its page lists groups who do not need to send this evidence, including graduates of Irish, EU or EEA, UK, Australian, New Zealand and US medical schools, EU citizens, and applicants for specialist, visiting EEA or internship registration. The page adds that this is likely to change. All other applicants must provide the evidence. See the OET course for doctors for preparation.

English-test planning: The Medical Council asks for the OET Medicine category with a minimum of grade B in each module, in a single sitting within the last two years. Certificates must be dated within two years of the date of registration, and older certificates are not accepted. It does not accept OET@Home, IELTS Indicator or IELTS Online. Alternatives on the same page: IELTS Academic with an overall 7.0 and at least 6.5 in each module, or Cambridge at level C1 or C2 within the last two years.

Pathway steps

Check whether you are exemptRead the list of exempt groups on the Medical Council's English language page before you book anything.
Check the PRES positionThe Council's PRES questions page lists exemptions, for example an equivalent internship. It names internships in Australia, New Zealand, Pakistan, South Africa, Sudan and Malaysia, with start-date conditions for Pakistan and South Africa. The structure of the PRES itself is not confirmed here.
Sit OET Medicine onceAll four modules at grade B or above in the same sitting, at a test venue.
Apply while the certificate is validThe certificate must still be within two years on the date of registration, not only on the day you apply.

Common mistakes

  • Combining modules from two sittings: the rule is a single sitting.
  • Booking OET@Home: the Medical Council does not accept it.
  • Assuming a PLAB, USMLE, AMC or MCCQE pass removes the PRES. We could not confirm that: ask the Medical Council.
  • Letting the certificate pass two years before registration is complete.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Referral, discharge and transfer letters with selective relevance and a formal tone.

Speaking

Consultations, explaining a diagnosis, breaking bad news and shared decisions.

Reading

Guideline extracts, drug information and workplace texts under time pressure.

Listening

Consultations, ward briefings and presentations in different accents.

Pharmacists: PSI

PSIOET Pharmacy350, 350, 350 and Writing 300Same sitting

The PSI language requirements apply to all pharmacists who trained outside Ireland. PSI uses level C1 of the Common European Framework as its minimum standard and currently accepts IELTS, TOEFL, CAE and OET. For pharmacists who qualified outside the EU, the PSI third-country route starts with recognition of your qualification. See the OET course for pharmacists for preparation.

English-test planning: PSI's table asks for OET Pharmacy with Listening 350, Reading 350, Speaking 350 and Writing 300. Results are valid for two years from the test date. All components must be attempted at the same sitting and PSI does not accept one-skill retakes. Home-based tests are not accepted. PSI's table also prints base scores for super-scoring, but we could not read the full rule, so it is not confirmed: plan to pass in one sitting. From 1 June 2026 PSI no longer accepts a university letter as proof that your course was in English.

OET on Computer with Speaking at home: PSI's page mentions that OET now delivers the Speaking sub-test at home through a new platform for OET on Computer candidates, but it does not clearly say that such results are accepted. That is not confirmed: ask PSI before you book.

Pathway steps

Apply for recognition of your qualificationPSI says this is the first step for a qualification gained outside the EU.
Holistic AssessmentPSI lists a Holistic Assessment as the next step after recognition.
MCQ and, or, OSCEThe examinations are run by RCSI. PSI's page printed these 2027 dates on 27 November 2026, subject to change: 16 to 17 February, 28 to 29 July, 17 to 18 September and 19 to 20 November.
Register with your language evidencePSI asks for the language assessment when you apply for registration, so time OET to be valid at that point.

Common mistakes

  • Booking a home-based test: PSI does not accept it.
  • Planning a one-skill retake or splitting components across sittings.
  • Relying on a university letter about English-medium teaching after 1 June 2026.
  • Sitting OET at the start of a long route, so the two years run out before registration.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Letters to prescribers and other professionals with accurate medicine details.

Speaking

Medication counselling, adherence, side effects and safe refusal of a sale.

Reading

Medicine information, safety alerts and workplace policies.

Listening

Pharmacist and patient counselling and team handovers.

CORU professions: physiotherapy, occupational therapy, dietetics, optometry, podiatry, radiography, speech and language therapy

CORUSeven OET professionsThree score linesMaximum two sittings

CORU is the regulator for health and social care professionals, with a registration board for each profession. Its language page lists OET for seven of them. You may not need a test if you hold a CORU-approved qualification, if you qualified in the previous two years through English or Irish, or if you lived and practised through English in an English-only country for at least two of the past five years. Course pages: OET for physiotherapists and OET for allied health professions.

English-test planning: Book the OET version for your own profession and read the score line for your own board. Certificates must be dated within two years of the day you submit your registration application. CORU accepts results from a maximum of two sittings: the repeat must be with the same test provider and within six months of the first test, no OET sub-test may be below C+, and CORU says the overall minimum must be met on both sittings. Pathway steps

Find your registration boardCheck the score line for your profession. The three lines are not the same.
Recognition of your qualificationInternational qualifications are assessed first. CORU may offer a compensation measure where it finds substantial differences. We have not confirmed the details or fees: read CORU's pages.
Sit your profession's OETAim to pass in one sitting. If you need a second, keep to the same provider and the six-month limit.
Apply within two yearsThe certificate date is counted back from the day you submit your application.

Common mistakes

  • Sitting OET Nursing or Medicine because more practice material exists for them.
  • Planning a third sitting, or a retake of one sub-test only.
  • Radiographers aiming for C+ in one component: CORU asks for B in all four.
  • Speech and language therapists aiming for 350 across the board: CORU asks for 400 in three components.
  • Assuming OET@Home is accepted without written confirmation from CORU.

Dentists: Dental Council

Dental CouncilOET DentistryB in each moduleOET@Home not accepted

The Dental Council says applicants for registration from 1 November 2018 must be proficient in English. Besides a test, it lists two other routes: a qualification that was undertaken in English, or practice as a fully registered dental professional in a member state where English is an official language. Dentists who qualified outside the EEA are normally required to pass the Council's special examination. See the OET course for dentists for preparation.

English-test planning: The Dental Council asks for OET Dentistry with a minimum of grade B in each module, or IELTS Academic with at least 7.0 in each module. The certificate must have been obtained no more than two years before you submit your application for registration. The Council does not currently accept OET@Home or IELTS Online. Its page does not say whether sittings can be combined: not confirmed, so plan to pass in one sitting. From January 2027 OET on Paper is for Nursing and Medicine only, so dentists sit OET on Computer at a venue.

Pathway steps

Check the three English routesA test, a qualification undertaken in English, or registered practice in a member state where English is official.
Part 1 of the examinationThree written papers and an operative bench test, with a maximum of two attempts.
Part 2 of the examinationWritten papers, an OSCE and clinical vivas, with a maximum of three attempts.
Watch the 2027 calendarOn 27 November 2026 the Council's page gave an application week of 11 to 15 January 2027, Part 1 in Dublin on 22 to 25 March 2027 and Part 2 in Cork in June 2027, dates to be confirmed. Places are allocated by random selection, so read the page before you plan.

Common mistakes

  • Booking OET@Home or IELTS Online: neither is currently accepted.
  • Sending an English certificate that is more than two years old when the completed application is received.
  • Assuming two sittings can be combined. The page does not say so.
  • Missing the short application week for the examination.

OET preparation focus

Writing

Referral letters to specialists and medical practitioners with accurate dental detail.

Speaking

Treatment options, consent, anxious patients and oral health advice.

Reading

Clinical guidance and workplace texts read at speed.

Listening

Dentist and patient consultations and professional talks.

Veterinary Council of IrelandNot confirmed

Test formats and venues

Paper, computer or OET@Home in Ireland

Three separate questions decide your format: what OET lets you book in your country and profession, what your regulator accepts, and how Speaking is delivered. They have different answers.

OET on Paper from January 2027

OET announced on 16 November 2026 that from January 2027 the paper test continues for Nursing and Medicine only. The announcement names no countries. OET's customer care team told us by email in November 2026 that Ireland is one of the 16 countries that keep paper testing. Paper sessions are never guaranteed: see our paper test centres 2027 list for Ireland.

OET on Computer at a venue

OET says the computer test stays available for all 12 professions at approved venues, subject to availability. On 27 November 2026 the official OET finder listed computer venues in Dublin and Cork. Venues and sessions change, so check our OET test centres list for Ireland and then the finder itself.

OET@Home

OET says OET@Home is offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available. Ireland has venues, so do not plan on booking it from inside Ireland. If you sit elsewhere, your Irish regulator's rule still decides whether the result counts: four of the five we read refuse at-home tests.

Speaking by video is not OET@Home

On a computer test, and at some paper venues from 2027, the Speaking sub-test can be delivered by live video. That does not make the test OET@Home. If your Speaking will be remote, ask your regulator to confirm it accepts that format. PSI mentions it on its page without clearly saying yes.

Candidate checklist

Before you book OET for Ireland

  • Name your regulator: NMBI, Medical Council, PSI, CORU, Dental Council or the Veterinary Council of Ireland.
  • Check whether you need a test at all. Several regulators list education or practice routes.
  • Book the OET version for your own profession.
  • Write down your exact score line and whether one sitting is required.
  • Choose a venue test unless your regulator has confirmed OET@Home in writing.
  • Count the two years from the point your regulator uses, and time the test near your application.
  • Start with free OET practice or the free placement test to see how far you are from the score.

Ireland pathway FAQ

Short answers for healthcare professionals planning Irish registration with OET.

Does Ireland accept OET?

Yes for most health professions, but each regulator sets its own rule. NMBI, the Medical Council, PSI, CORU and the Dental Council all list OET on their English language pages. We could not confirm the Veterinary Council of Ireland's rule, so vets should check with it directly.

What OET score do nurses need for NMBI?

NMBI asks for OET Nursing with grade B in three components and C+ in one, in a single result that is less than two years old. NMBI does not accept combined results or OET at home.

What OET score do doctors need for the Irish Medical Council?

For applications received on or after 1 March 2025, the Medical Council asks for OET Medicine with at least grade B in each module, in a single sitting within the last two years. It does not accept OET@Home.

Can I combine two OET sittings for Ireland?

Only CORU's page allows it: a maximum of two sittings from the same test provider, with the repeat taken within six months and no OET sub-test below C+. NMBI, the Medical Council and PSI ask for one sitting. The Dental Council's page does not say, so plan for one sitting.

Is OET@Home accepted in Ireland?

NMBI, the Medical Council, PSI and the Dental Council say they do not accept at-home tests. CORU's wording is unclear, so treat it as not confirmed and ask CORU in writing before you book.

Can I still take OET on paper in Ireland in 2027?

From January 2027 OET on Paper continues for Nursing and Medicine only. OET's customer care team listed Ireland among the 16 countries that keep paper testing, but paper sessions are never guaranteed. All other professions sit OET on Computer at a venue.

Which OET version do CORU professions need?

The version for your own profession. CORU lists OET Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Dietetics, Optometry, Podiatry, Radiography and Speech Pathology, with three different score lines, so read the line for your registration board.
