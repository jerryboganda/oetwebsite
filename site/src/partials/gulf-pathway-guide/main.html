Qatar pathway overview

Most Qatar healthcare applicants follow the same broad workflow: an application on DHP's Registration and Licensing System, primary source verification (PSV), preliminary evaluation, the qualifying examination unless an exemption applies, a licensed employer and licence issuance.

Important: This guide is educational orientation only. It is not legal, immigration, employment or regulator advice. Always verify with DHP / MOPH, DataFlow and your employer before paying any fee.

Key Qatar Prometric and English facts

DHP uses computer-based qualifying exams; book the OET version for your profession and check your employer's English requirements before booking.

Prometric format

Every exam on the DHP qualifying examination list is a computer-based Prometric exam of 150 MCQs in 3 hours.

Pass mark

DHP lists 60 percent for General Practitioner, General Dentist and Pharmacist, 50 percent for nursing, midwifery, physiotherapy and other allied health exams, and 65 percent for most specialist physician exams.

Attempts

DHP says applicants may attempt the qualifying exam an unlimited number of times. Confirm with DHP how long a pass stays valid for your application.

OET target

The DHP guidelines we checked do not set an OET score. Ask your employer in writing which English test and score it needs, and book the OET version for your profession.

DHP / MOPH Qatar

Doctors: Qatar DHP physician pathway

For IMGs and specialists applying as General Practitioner, Specialist or Consultant in Qatar. Classification depends on qualification, experience and specialty rules applied in DHP's evaluation.

StepWhat to prepare
1Apply on DHP's Registration and Licensing System and submit the evaluation request for your scope.
2Submit documents for primary source verification through a DHP-approved provider such as DataFlow. DHP lists PSV as mandatory for all healthcare practitioners.
3Receive the preliminary evaluation, which is valid for six months.
4Sit the Qatar qualifying exam for your specialty. Circular DHP/2025/24 (30 December 2025) limits qualifying exam exemptions to Qataris, offspring of Qatari women and graduates of universities and higher education institutions in Qatar, so confirm any other exemption with DHP in writing.
5Ask your employer which English evidence it requires, for example OET Medicine; the DHP guidelines we checked do not set an OET score.
6Secure employment with a licensed healthcare facility in Qatar.
7Submit the licensing documents, including the verification report, a police clearance certificate and a medical report.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: referral, discharge and transfer letters for Gulf hospital systems.
  • Speaking: chronic disease, diabetes, cardiovascular disease, Ramadan medication adjustment and culturally sensitive bad news.
  • Reading: abstracts, clinical guideline summaries and workplace texts.
  • Listening: multi-accent consultations in a diverse Gulf workforce.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Assuming PLAB, USMLE or MRCP removes the Qatar qualifying exam or PSV without written confirmation from DHP.
  • Not following up the Certificate of Good Standing, which DHP makes the applicant’s responsibility.
  • Booking the wrong physician specialty Prometric exam.
  • Letting the six-month preliminary evaluation expire.
DHP / MOPH Qatar

Nurses: Qatar DHP nursing pathway

For internationally qualified nurses, specialist nurses, nurse practitioners, clinical nurse specialists, educators and midwives targeting Qatar healthcare employers.

StepWhat to prepare
1Apply on DHP's Registration and Licensing System and submit the nursing evaluation request.
2Send degree, transcript, registration, experience letters and passport for PSV through a DHP-approved provider such as DataFlow.
3Receive the DHP preliminary evaluation, which is valid for six months.
4Sit the Qatar nursing qualifying exam: 150 MCQs in 3 hours, with a 50 percent cut score for Registered General Nurse and Registered Midwife.
5Ask your employer which English evidence and score it requires, for example OET Nursing; the DHP Guidelines for Nurses do not set an OET score.
6Secure an employment offer from a Qatari healthcare facility.
7Submit the licensing documents (including a police clearance certificate from the Qatari Ministry of Interior and a medical report), pay any fees and receive a temporary or permanent DHP licence; a temporary licence lasts up to six months and cannot be renewed.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: nursing referrals, discharge letters and patient education for bilingual Arabic / English settings.
  • Speaking: education, calming distressed patients and culturally sensitive family communication.
  • Reading: care plans, infection-control bulletins and workplace policies.
  • Listening: ward briefings and nurse-patient interactions in multinational teams.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Booking OET Medicine instead of OET Nursing.
  • Assuming a US NCLEX pass automatically bypasses all Qatar requirements.
  • Letting English certificates expire during the application.
  • Submitting police clearance without required attestation.
DHP / MOPH Qatar

Pharmacists: Qatar pharmacy licensing pathway

For pharmacists and pharmacy technicians applying through DHP. The Pharmacist qualifying exam on the DHP list is 150 MCQs in 3 hours, with a 60 percent cut score.

Police clearance note: DHP’s Guidelines for Pharmacists list a valid police clearance certificate from the Qatari Ministry of Interior, addressed to the Ministry of Public Health and valid for 3 months, as a licensing requirement. A temporary licence applied for without a QID needs a home-country police clearance attested by MOFA.
StepWhat to prepare
1Apply on DHP's Registration and Licensing System and submit the pharmacy or pharmacy technician evaluation.
2Submit degree, transcript, home registration, experience letters and passport for PSV through a DHP-approved provider such as DataFlow.
3Receive the preliminary evaluation, which is valid for six months.
4Sit the Qatar Pharmacist qualifying exam unless you fall in an exempt category under Circular DHP/2025/24.
5Ask your employer which English evidence it requires, for example OET Pharmacy; DHP’s Guidelines for Pharmacists do not set an OET score.
6Secure a job offer from a DHP-recognised community, hospital or institutional pharmacy.
7Submit the licensing documents, including the verification report, a Qatari Ministry of Interior police clearance and a medical report, and pay any fees.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: pharmacy letters to GPs or consultants, supplier requests and dispensing-incident notes.
  • Speaking: medication counselling, Ramadan advice, adherence and OTC sale refusal.
  • Reading: SmPCs, MoPH drug safety alerts and pharmacy policies.
  • Listening: counselling, ward conversations and pharmacy team handovers.

Our OET course for pharmacists trains these pharmacy letters and counselling role-plays with pharmacy scenarios.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using OET Medicine or Nursing preparation for OET Pharmacy.
  • Submitting expired home registration evidence.
  • Assuming a Gulf exemption transfers automatically to Qatar.
  • Letting the six-month preliminary evaluation expire.
DHP / MOPH Qatar

Physiotherapists: Qatar allied health pathway

For physiotherapists applying under DHP allied healthcare practitioner rules, whether you are aiming for hospital, primary care, sports medicine or private clinic roles.

StepWhat to prepare
1Apply on DHP's Registration and Licensing System and submit the physiotherapy evaluation.
2Submit degree, transcript, registration, experience letters and passport for PSV through a DHP-approved provider such as DataFlow.
3Receive the preliminary evaluation, which is valid for six months.
4Sit the Qatar Physiotherapist qualifying exam (150 MCQs in 3 hours, 50 percent cut score) unless DHP confirms an exemption.
5Ask your employer which English evidence it requires, for example OET Physiotherapy; the DHP guidelines we checked do not set an OET score.
6Secure employment from a DHP-recognised physiotherapy provider.
7Submit the licensing documents, including the verification report, a police clearance certificate stamped by MOFA and addressed to MOPH, and a medical report, and pay any fees.

OET preparation focus

  • Writing: referrals, progress letters and discharge summaries for rehabilitation teams.
  • Speaking: diagnosis, prognosis, home exercises, sports rehab and return-to-play advice.
  • Reading: rehab guidelines, return-to-work and return-to-sport documents.
  • Listening: physiotherapy consultations and MDT briefings.

Physiotherapists can follow the OET course for physiotherapists, and other allied health professionals will find their OET route in OET for allied health professions.

Common mistakes to avoid

  • Using non-physiotherapy OET speaking cards.
  • Using vague functional language instead of specific physiotherapy terminology.
  • Letting English evidence expire during licensing.
  • Submitting documents in a language other than Arabic or English without an official translation.

Wider Gulf at a glance

Across the GCC, the workflow is broadly similar: online portal, PSV, licensing exam, employer sponsorship and final regulator approval. Each country has its own rules.

Score caution: The UAE Unified PQR (MOHAP, DOH, DHA and SHA) lists OET at grade C or above as one accepted proof of English in specific cases, such as some experience or exam exemptions based on a licence from a listed country. The Qatar DHP guidelines and SCFHS classification requirements we checked do not set an OET score. Employers can set their own requirement, so confirm the test and score in writing before booking.

Candidate checklist before you start

  • Create your account on DHP's Registration and Licensing System or the correct Gulf regulator portal.
  • Identify your exact scope, specialty and profession category.
  • Start primary source verification early with an approved provider and keep document source details consistent.
  • Book the correct OET profession version and check how recent your result must be.
  • Track the six-month preliminary evaluation window.
  • Prepare the police clearance, medical report, official translations and employer documents.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for Qatar and Gulf pathway candidates.

Is this Gulf guide only for Qatar?

The detailed pathway focuses on Qatar: DHP's Registration and Licensing System, primary source verification and the Qatar qualifying exam. The page also includes a wider Gulf comparison for the UAE, Saudi Arabia, Bahrain, Kuwait and Oman.

Is DataFlow required for Qatar?

DHP lists primary source verification as a mandatory requirement for all healthcare practitioners, and the verification report is attached to the online evaluation application. DataFlow is one of the DHP-approved verification providers.

How many Qatar Prometric attempts are allowed?

DHP's qualifying examination page says applicants may attempt the qualifying exam an unlimited number of times. Each exam on its list is 150 MCQs in 3 hours; confirm with DHP how long a pass stays valid for your application.

What OET score should Gulf candidates target?

There is no single Gulf score. The UAE Unified PQR lists OET at grade C or above as English evidence in specific cases, while the Qatar DHP guidelines and SCFHS requirements we checked set no OET score. Use the score your regulator or employer confirms in writing.
