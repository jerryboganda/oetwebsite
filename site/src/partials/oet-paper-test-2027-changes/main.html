OET on Paper ends for 10 professions in January 2027: what changes for you

On 16 November 2026, OET published an official update: from January 2027, the OET Test on Paper will continue for Nursing and Medicine only. Every other profession, including Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Dentistry and the rest of the 12 OET professions, moves to the OET Test on Computer at approved venues.

This is the biggest delivery change since OET@Home launched, and it lands in the middle of thousands of preparation plans. Here is exactly what was announced, who is affected, and what to do next.

What OET actually announced

The official announcement (oet.com, 16 November 2026) says:

Paper and computer

  • From January 2027, the OET Test on Paper continues for Nursing and Medicine only.
  • The OET Test on Computer remains available for all 12 healthcare professions at approved test venues, subject to availability.

Speaking and Listening on paper

  • Paper candidates still attend an approved venue for the Speaking sub-test, meeting the interlocutor in person or over a live video connection depending on the venue.
  • At venues where Speaking runs over video, candidates get headphones for both Speaking and Listening, and the Listening audio plays through the headphones with timing on screen.
Tests taken in 2026

Nothing changes for tests taken in 2026. If your paper test is booked for this year, it goes ahead as planned.

The 16 countries that keep OET on Paper

The announcement page on oet.com does not list countries. OET’s customer care team (Cambridge Boxhill Language Assessment) confirmed the list by email in November 2026: from 2027, the OET Test on Paper will only be available for Nursing and Medicine candidates in these 16 countries:

Two things to read carefully:

1. The list limits paper, not computer.

OET on Computer remains available for all 12 professions at approved venues, subject to local availability, including in countries not on this list.

2. Being on the list does not guarantee a seat.

Paper availability still depends on approved venues and dates in each country. Check the OET booking page for your city before you plan around a paper date.

Outside the 16 countries

If you are a nurse or doctor outside these 16 countries, plan for the computer test from January 2027.

Who is affected

Nurses and doctors

You keep the choice, if your country is on the list above. Paper remains available for Nursing and Medicine from January 2027, and the computer test stays open to you as well. Check the booking page for your city before you plan around a paper date.

Pharmacists, physiotherapists, dentists, radiographers, vets and the other professions

From January 2027, the OET Test on Computer is your format, wherever you are. If you were planning a paper sitting in 2027, your preparation needs to switch now, not after you book.

What this means for your preparation

The test itself does not change. The OET Test on Computer has the same structure, content, length and marking as the paper test. What changes is the medium, and the medium is where marks are lost:

1. Writing becomes typing.

A letter you can handwrite in 45 minutes is not the same as one you can type in 45 minutes. Start typing every practice letter now, and time yourself honestly.

2. Listening through headphones.

At venues with video Speaking, the Listening audio plays through headphones rather than speakers. Practise with headphones so the sound profile is familiar.

3. Reading on screen.

Skimming and scanning a screen uses different habits from paper. Do your timed Reading practice on a computer, not a printout.

4. Book earlier.

Venue seats for popular professions will concentrate on computer dates from January 2027. If you have a registration deadline (GMC, NMC, Ahpra, ECFMG), work backwards and book with margin.

Our advice, in one line

Prepare for the computer test from today

Whatever your profession, prepare for the computer test from today. If you are a nurse or doctor in one of the 16 countries and paper is available near you, choosing it stays open, but training on the computer costs you nothing and keeps every option alive.

Frequently asked questions

Is the OET Test on Paper cancelled?

No. From January 2027 it continues for Nursing and Medicine only, in 16 countries. For the other ten professions it is no longer offered anywhere.

Which countries keep OET on Paper from 2027?

Australia, Canada, Egypt, Ghana, India, Ireland, Kenya, Nepal, New Zealand, Pakistan, the Philippines, Saudi Arabia, Sri Lanka, Turkey, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom, for Nursing and Medicine candidates only, per an official OET customer-care email from November 2026.

Does the computer test have different content or marking?

No. Both modes share the same structure, content, length and marking. Only the delivery medium differs.

I booked a paper test for 2026. Is it affected?

No. The change applies from January 2027. 2026 sittings go ahead as booked.

I am a nurse or doctor in one of the 16 countries. Is paper guaranteed for me?

Not guaranteed. Paper availability depends on approved venues and dates in each country. Check the OET booking page for your city before counting on a paper date.

Is OET@Home affected?

The announcement concerns venue-based paper and computer delivery. Check oet.com for the delivery modes currently offered for your profession and country.
