Where to take OET in the UAE

  • OET on Computer: AMIDEAST Dubai and AMIDEAST Abu Dhabi
  • OET on Paper in Dubai and Sharjah
  • OET on Paper in Abu Dhabi and Al Ain
  • OET on Paper in Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah

OET on Paper is offered for seven professions (Medicine, Nursing, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Radiography and Veterinary Science). Candidates in the other OET professions need one of the two computer venues. The UAE is not on OET's list of countries with Speaking at the venue, so OET on Computer candidates usually take Speaking online from home.

Do DHA, DOH and MOHAP ask for OET?

One rulebook covers all of them: the Unified Healthcare Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR), third version, April 2025, issued by MOHAP, the Department of Health Abu Dhabi, the Dubai Health Authority and the Sharjah Health Authority. Its general licensing requirements include no English test.

The PQR asks for proof of English only in specific cases, such as some experience or exam exemptions based on a licence from a listed country. In those cases OET at grade C or above is one of the accepted proofs.

The route for most applicants is primary source verification of your documents, then the authority's examination, which is held in English, with three attempts across the authorities. In Dubai you apply through the Sheryan portal, with verification by DataFlow and a computer-based test at Prometric. In Abu Dhabi you apply through the TAMM service "Issue a Healthcare Professional Licence".

OET in the UAE: FAQ

Where can I take OET in the UAE?

On 19 November 2026 the OET venue finder listed 12 venues: OET on Computer at AMIDEAST in Dubai and Abu Dhabi, and OET on Paper in Dubai, Sharjah, Abu Dhabi, Al Ain, Ras Al Khaimah and Fujairah. Venues change, so check the OET venue finder before you book.

Do I need OET for a DHA, DOH or MOHAP licence?

Not as a general rule. The Unified PQR has no general English test requirement. It accepts OET at grade C or above as proof of English in specific exemption cases.

Can I take OET Speaking at a venue in the UAE?

The UAE is not on the list OET published on 20 July 2026 of countries where OET on Computer Speaking can be taken at a venue, so it is usually taken online from home. OET on Paper Speaking is taken at the venue.

How much does OET cost in the UAE?

OET charges AU$587 for the full test, paid by Visa or Mastercard when you book. OET publishes no price in dirhams, so your bank sets the exchange rate.
