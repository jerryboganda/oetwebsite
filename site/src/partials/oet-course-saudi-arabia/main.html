Where to take OET in Saudi Arabia

  • Riyadh: computer venues at Akaria Mall and AMIDEAST Riyadh; paper at Alfaisal University and an AMIDEAST listing in Al Murabba
  • Jeddah: computer venues at the Saudi Business Center and the SCFHS building on Palestine Street; paper at Dar Al Hekma University
  • Al Khobar: one computer venue (MindMerge Training Center)
  • Taif, Hail, Tabuk and Sakaka: computer venues, several with separate centres for men and women

Does SCFHS ask for OET?

The SCFHS classification and registration requirement pages we checked on 19 November 2026 name no English test. The only language rule we found is that documents must be in Arabic or English. An employer can still set its own English requirement, so ask for it in writing.

The licensing route itself runs through the Mumaris Plus portal: certificates from outside the Kingdom are verified at source through DataFlow, and the licensure exams (such as SMLE for doctors, SNLE for nurses and SPLE for pharmacists) are booked at Prometric centres in Saudi Arabia or abroad. The applicant guides describe 200 multiple-choice questions and up to four attempts a year.

Most candidates in Saudi Arabia who take OET do so for registration in the UK, Ireland, Australia or the USA. The scores are in the table below.

OET in Saudi Arabia: FAQ

Where can I take OET in Saudi Arabia?

On 19 November 2026 the OET venue finder listed 16 venues in Riyadh, Jeddah, Al Khobar, Taif, Hail, Tabuk and Sakaka. Thirteen were for OET on Computer and three for OET on Paper. Venues change, so check the OET venue finder before you book.

Does SCFHS require OET?

The SCFHS classification and registration pages we checked do not ask for an OET or IELTS score. An employer may set its own English requirement, and regulators abroad do ask for OET.

Can I take the OET Speaking test at a venue in Saudi Arabia?

Yes. Saudi Arabia is on the list OET published on 20 July 2026 of countries where OET on Computer candidates can take Speaking at a venue. Confirm that your regulator accepts the Speaking mode you choose.

How much does OET cost in Saudi Arabia?

OET charges AU$587 for the full test, paid by Visa or Mastercard when you book. OET publishes no price in riyals, so your bank sets the exchange rate.
