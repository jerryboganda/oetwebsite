OET is delivered in three ways. The mode changes which professions you can book, how Speaking works, when bookings close and how fast results arrive.

Before you book OET@Home, check that your regulator accepts it. The NMC and the GMC accept OET@Home. OET on Computer and OET@Home dates

OET on Computer does not follow the paper calendar. In the computer dates we checked on OET's booking page (March to July 2026), Global computer sessions ran from Monday to Saturday in most weeks, with a few weeks that had none. All 12 professions were available, and each date showed a results date, usually the Friday of the following week.

Computer and OET@Home bookings close 7 days before the test at 11:59 pm UTC, so you can book much closer to your exam than on paper. Dates and venues vary by country. Use the official OET date finder to see what is available where you live.

When do OET results come out?

OET reports each sub-test separately on a scale from 0 to 500. There is no overall OET score or grade.

  • Computer and OET@Home: results in as little as 48 hours after you finish all four sub-tests, and 95% within 6 calendar days.
  • Paper: from 13 days after the test date, or from 5 days in India and the Philippines.
  • Doctors applying to ECFMG: ECFMG says OET Medicine results are typically released 10 business days after the test.

OET describes all timeframes as indicative. OET itself sets no validity period for results: each regulator decides how long it accepts them, so check the OET score requirements for your destination. To see which grade each score gives, use the OET score calculator.

Leave room for a re-sit

If your regulator needs all four sub-tests in one sitting, plan a second full test date in case one sub-test falls short.

OET test dates and fees FAQ

Short answers to the questions candidates ask before booking. More exam questions are on the full OET FAQ page.

When are the OET test dates in 2026?

OET on paper runs on fixed Saturdays roughly every two weeks, with an Americas session on the Friday before each one. OET on Computer runs on many more dates, often Monday to Saturday in most weeks, depending on your country. The last 2026 paper sessions are Friday 18 December (Americas) and Saturday 19 December (Global).

How much does the OET exam cost?

The full OET test costs AU$587 in every mode. In the USA, OET on Computer and OET@Home cost US$455. A paper re-sit of one, two or three sub-tests costs AU$315, AU$415 or AU$515 after a full test, but not in the USA.

How long do OET results take?

Computer and OET@Home results can arrive in as little as 48 hours, and 95% are released within 6 calendar days. Paper results arrive from 13 days after the test, or from 5 days in India and the Philippines. OET describes these timeframes as indicative.

Are the 2027 OET test dates available?

Not yet. As of 24 October 2026, OET has not published any 2027 test dates. Check the official booking page, which lists each date with its booking deadline and results date.

Can I retake only one OET sub-test?

Only on paper, only after a full OET test, and not in the USA. Many regulators will not accept it: the NMC, Ahpra, the Medical Council of New Zealand and ECFMG all need all four sub-tests in each sitting.
