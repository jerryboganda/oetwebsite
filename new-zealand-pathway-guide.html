New Zealand Pathway Guide: OET scores by board

New Zealand has no single English rule for health professionals. Each responsible authority sets its own OET score, sitting rule and validity period. This guide lists what eleven authorities and Immigration New Zealand publish, profession by profession, with a link to every source.

By Dr Ahmed Hesham, medical doctor and founder of OET with Dr Hesham · Published 27 Nov 2026

Last verified: 27 November 2026. Every rule below was read on the regulator's own page or policy on that date. Where a source was silent or could not be read, we say "not confirmed".

New Zealand pathway overview

In New Zealand each health profession has its own responsible authority: a council or a board. There is no shared English standard, so two colleagues in different professions can face different OET scores, different sitting rules and different validity periods. The table covers all 12 OET professions. "Accepted" means the authority names OET in the document we read. "Not confirmed" means the document is silent or we could not read a primary source, so you must ask the authority.

Choose your regulator first. Then confirm the accepted test and the accepted format before you book. OET does not work the same way for every profession.

Important: This guide is educational orientation only. It is not legal, immigration or regulator advice, and regulators can change their rules without notice. We are an independent coaching service, not part of OET, any regulator or any test centre. Always read the rule on the regulator's own page before you book a test or pay a fee.

Four checks before you book

Most wasted test fees come from booking before reading the regulator's rule. Run these four checks in order.

1. Your regulator

Find the council or board for your profession in the table above and open its own document. A score that satisfies one board can fail another.

2. Sittings

Some authorities let you combine sittings inside a fixed window. Others want one sitting. The detail is in each section below and in our guide to combining OET sittings.

3. Test format

Paper, computer at a venue and OET@Home are three different formats. A computer test at a venue with Speaking by video is not OET@Home. Whether you can book OET@Home is OET's rule. Whether your regulator accepts it is a separate question. See OET@Home: who accepts it.

4. Validity

Count the validity period from the date the authority uses: the application date, the exam date or the day it receives your file. The Dietitians Board uses 12 months, not 2 years.

Medical Council of New Zealand

Doctors: Medical Council of New Zealand

The rules come from the Council's Policy on English language requirements, dated September 2026. OET is one of several ways to meet the requirement.

Not confirmed: an NZREX Clinical application form may print a different score wording from the September 2026 policy. We could not read that form directly. If you are an NZREX candidate, ask the Council which wording applies before you book.
RuleWhat the regulator publishes
OET versionThe Medical module of OET.
Minimum scoresSpeaking 350, Listening 350, Reading 350, Writing 300.
SittingsOne sitting. Or a maximum of two sittings in a 12-month period, only if you are tested in all four components at each sitting and no score from either sitting is below 300.
ValidityWithin 2 years of an application for registration, or within 2 years of the NZREX Clinical examination date for a new candidate.
Paper, computer, OET@HomeNot confirmed The policy says nothing about the delivery format. Ask the Council before you book OET@Home.
Without a testThe policy lists other routes, for example a New Zealand primary medical qualification, or English as a first language with a qualification from Australia, the UK, Ireland, the USA or an English-instruction Canadian or South African school, or recent work as a registered doctor in an English-speaking setting with two senior-doctor referees. Read the exact conditions in the policy.
Other testsIELTS Academic: Speaking 7.0, Listening 7.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 6.5.

Registration route: NZREX Clinical

  • The NZREX Clinical is an OSCE with 12 stations and lasts 3 hours.
  • Before it you need a pass, within 5 years of your allocated exam date, in one of: PLAB Part 1, MCCQE Part I, the AMC MCQ, or USMLE Steps 1 and 2 CK.
  • Your English result must be valid, that is passed within two years, at your allocated exam date.
  • A pass is valid for 5 years and must be followed by a PGY1 post with an accredited provider.
  • Exam dates printed on the Council page when we checked: 6 March 2027 and 4 September 2027.

What this guide does not cover

  • The Council has other registration pathways besides NZREX Clinical. We did not read them, so they are not described here.
  • Do not assume the two-sitting rule lets you resit a single sub-test: all four components must be tested each time.
  • Preparing for OET Medicine? See the OET course for doctors.
Nursing Council of New Zealand

Nurses: Nursing Council of New Zealand

The rules come from the Council's English Language Competence Policy Guidance (dated 19 August 2022) and its requirements page for internationally qualified nurses.

RuleWhat the regulator publishes
Minimum scoresAt least 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking, and at least 300 in Writing.
SittingsThe scores can be reached over more than one sitting. All required scores must be met within 12 months of your first sitting, and all sittings must fall within three years of starting your application. No minimum score per sitting is printed.
One test onlyYou cannot mix tests, for example OET for two skills and IELTS for the other two.
Paper and computerAccepted The test must be taken at an OET test centre, on paper or on computer.
OET@HomeNot accepted The Council does not currently accept tests taken completely online, such as OET@Home.
Speaking by video at a venueAccepted OET on computer at an approved venue, with the Speaking sub-test hosted online, is accepted. That is not OET@Home.
Without a testNursing education in Canada, Ireland, Singapore, the United Kingdom or the United States; or current registration in Canada, Ireland, the UK or the USA where you sat a test to gain it. Nurses registered in Australia apply under the Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition Act 1997 and need no English evidence.
Other testsIELTS Academic: 7 in Reading, Listening and Speaking, 6.5 in Writing. The Council page says it does not accept PTE.

Registration route in brief

  • Identity check and application through TruMerit.
  • Current overseas registration and an equivalent nursing qualification.
  • At least 1,800 hours of practice after registration.
  • For some registered nurses and all enrolled nurses, a competence assessment: an online theory exam at a Pearson test centre, then a two-day orientation and a three-hour OSCE in Christchurch.
  • The Welcome to Aotearoa New Zealand Programme and a criminal history check.

Before you book

  • Nursing is one of the two professions that keep the paper test from January 2027, where a paper session is offered.
  • Watch both clocks: 12 months from your first sitting for the scores, three years for all sittings.
  • Preparing for OET Nursing? See the OET course for nurses.
Pharmacy Council

Pharmacists: Pharmacy Council

The rules come from the Council's English language requirements page and its page for pharmacists from other countries.

RuleWhat the regulator publishes
Minimum scoresAn A or B score in each of the four bands: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.
ValidityAchieved within two years of the date of application.
SittingsIf you do not reach all the scores at one sitting, results from earlier OET tests obtained within a two-year period are accepted. The page prints no minimum per sitting and no maximum number of sittings.
Paper, computer, OET@HomeNot confirmed The page does not mention the delivery format.
Without a testNot required if you are registered in Australia, Canada, Ireland, the UK or the USA.
Other testsIELTS Academic: at least 7.0 in each of the four bands.

Registration route for other countries

  • Preliminary Review first. You cannot continue with any other step until it is approved.
  • Then OPRA, run by the Australian Pharmacy Council (two attempts in total), and the New Zealand Pharmacy Legislation course, both within 2 years of the Preliminary Review approval.
  • Then intern registration, the Intern Training Programme, which starts in February each year, and the Assessment Centre.
  • Then registration as a pharmacist.

Before you book

  • Timing note printed by the Council: applicants from 2 July 2026 will not be able to meet all the requirements in time for the 2027 intern programme.
  • Pharmacy has no paper test from January 2027. Plan for OET on computer at a venue and ask the Council about the format if you are considering anything else.
  • Preparing for OET Pharmacy? See the OET course for pharmacists.
Physiotherapy Board

Physiotherapists: Physiotherapy Board

The rules come from the Board's Help Portal article English proficiency evidence (last updated 5 June 2025). The Board's main website blocked our reading on 27 November 2026, so some details stay "not confirmed".

RuleWhat the regulator publishes
Minimum scoresAt least 350 for Reading, Listening and Speaking, and at least 300 for Writing.
SittingsOET scores may be obtained across two sittings in six months. Not confirmed The further conditions (all areas tested at both sittings, and a lowest allowed score per sitting) could not be read on a primary page. Ask the Board before you rely on two sittings.
ValidityTest results must be less than 2 years old on the date you submit your application.
Paper, computer, OET@HomeNot confirmed The article does not mention the delivery format.
Without a testConfirmation that English is your first language; or a physiotherapy qualification from the United Kingdom, Ireland, Canada or South Africa; or other evidence with the Registrar's prior permission.
Other testsIELTS Academic (7 for Reading, Listening and Speaking, 6.5 for Writing), PTE Academic and TOEFL iBT are also listed.

Registration route

  • The Board publishes an International Express Pathway and an International General Pathway.
  • Not confirmed We could not read either pathway page, so the steps are not described here. Read them on the Board's website.

Before you book

  • Physiotherapy has no paper test from January 2027. Plan for OET on computer at a venue.
  • The six-month window is short. Book the second sitting early if you may need it.
  • Preparing for OET Physiotherapy? See the OET course for physiotherapists.

Dentists, vets and allied health: board by board

Seven more authorities name OET. Their rules differ in small but costly ways: one sitting or several, 12 months or 24, in person only or silent on format. For allied health preparation see OET for allied health professionals.

Dentistry: Dental Council

  • Accepted for dentists, dental specialists, oral health therapists, dental therapists, hygienists and clinical dental technicians.
  • Minimum: a pass with A or B grades in each of the four components.
  • Sittings: the test must be passed in one sitting. One-skill retakes are not accepted.
  • Validity: a pass is valid for two years.
  • OET@Home: Not confirmed The page is silent.
  • Route: you need a prescribed qualification recognised by the Council; otherwise individual assessment or the registration examination. The examination details are not confirmed here.
  • Source: Dental Council, registration: what you need to know. Course: OET for dentists.

Veterinary Science: Veterinary Council (VCNZ)

  • Accepted Minimum: a pass with A or B grades in each of the 4 sections at 1 sitting.
  • Validity: 2 years. It may be extended up to 5 years for applicants who lived and worked in an English-first country for 4 of the previous 5 years.
  • OET@Home and the required OET profession version: Not confirmed
  • The policy was last reviewed in December 2017. Ask the Council whether it is still current before you book.
  • Source: VCNZ Policy on English Competence and English Tests.

Occupational Therapy: OTBNZ

  • Accepted OET Occupational Therapy, minimum B in each of the four components.
  • Sittings: you may combine the results of more than one attempt, but the standard must be reached within 12 months of sitting the first test.
  • Validity: results must be no older than 24 months when they are submitted.
  • OET@Home: Not confirmed The policy is silent.
  • Without a test: education in New Zealand, Australia, Canada, the Republic of Ireland, South Africa or the United Kingdom.
  • Route: overseas qualifications are assessed individually. See OTBNZ overseas applicants.
  • Source: OTBNZ Policy 704, English language policy for registration (version 2.0, reviewed September 2025).

Optometry: ODOB

  • Accepted OET Optometry, a minimum B pass (or a score of 350) in each of the four components, in one result.
  • Not accepted for online and distance tests. Tests must be taken in person, handwritten or on computer.
  • Validity: no more than two years old when the Board receives the application.
  • Exemptions are narrow and defined in the policy.
  • Source: ODOB English Language Policy for Registration (version 1.3, approved 21 September 2026).

Radiography: MRTB

  • Accepted Minimum: a pass with B in all four sections.
  • Validity and sittings for OET: Not confirmed The policy prints them for IELTS (one sitting, within two years) but not for OET.
  • OET@Home: Not confirmed We could not read a primary page on it. Plan for a test centre unless the Board tells you otherwise.
  • Without a test: several defined routes, including a Board-accredited qualification.
  • Source: MRTB Policy: English language proficiency (August 2024).

Dietetics: Dietitians Board

  • Accepted Minimum: an A or B in all four sections.
  • Validity: all components must be passed and dated within the 12 months before the Board receives the application. That is 12 months, not 2 years.
  • Combining sittings and OET@Home: Not confirmed
  • Source: Dietitians Board, English language requirements.

Podiatry: Podiatrists Board

  • Accepted The registration form asks for an OET certificate, "Grades A and B".
  • It applies to applicants for whom English is a second language, with exceptions printed on the form.
  • Validity, sittings, OET@Home and the OET profession version: Not confirmed The form states none of them.
  • Source: Podiatrists Board application for registration (updated August 2024).

Speech Pathology

  • Not confirmed We read no primary source for speech-language therapy in New Zealand, so this guide states no OET requirement.
  • Ask the body that would recognise your qualification, and your employer, which English evidence it wants before you book any test.

Immigration New Zealand is a separate rule

Registration and immigration are two different decisions by two different bodies. Immigration New Zealand accepts OET for skilled residence, at its own threshold. That threshold is lower than the score of every health regulator in this guide.

Do not aim for the visa score. A visa-level OET result does not satisfy registration. If you need both, the regulator's score is the one to plan for.
RuleWhat Immigration New Zealand publishes
OET acceptedAccepted OET is on the list of accepted tests for skilled residence.
Principal applicantGrade C+ or higher in all four skills: Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking.
Non-principal applicantsGrade C or higher in all four skills.
FormatNot accepted for at-home tests. You must sit the test in person at a test centre.
ValidityThe result must be no more than 2 years old when you apply.
Other visa categoriesNot confirmed We read the skilled residence instructions only.

Sources: Immigration New Zealand, English language requirements for skilled residence visas and Amendment Circular 2026-21 (7 August 2026).

Test formats and venues in New Zealand

Test centres belong to OET's network, not to us. We list what OET's own venue finder showed on 27 November 2026. A listed venue is not a promise of an open seat on your date.

OET on computer at a venue

OET says the computer test at a venue stays available for all 12 professions. On 27 November 2026 the finder listed one computer venue record in New Zealand. See OET test centres in New Zealand.

OET on paper from January 2027

OET announced on 16 November 2026 that the paper test continues for Nursing and Medicine only. New Zealand is one of the 16 countries named in an email from OET customer care (November 2026), not on oet.com. Paper sessions are never guaranteed. See paper test centres 2027: New Zealand and what changes in January 2027.

OET@Home

Three separate questions: can you book it from your country and profession (OET's rule), will your regulator accept it, and is a venue test with Speaking by video the same thing (it is not). In New Zealand the Nursing Council, ODOB and Immigration New Zealand do not accept at-home tests. For the others it is not confirmed. See OET@Home: who accepts it.

Book only through OET's official booking page. OET's own fee and its terms are on oet.com.

Candidate checklist before you start

  • Name your responsible authority and open its own English policy.
  • Write down four things: the OET profession version, the minimum scores, the sitting rule and the validity period.
  • Confirm the accepted format in writing if the policy is silent: paper, computer at a venue, or OET@Home.
  • If you also need a visa, read the Immigration New Zealand rule separately and plan for the higher regulator score.
  • Check the venue and the date in the OET finder before you fix your study plan.
  • Count the validity period back from your planned application date, not from today.
  • Take the free placement test and start with free OET practice to see how far you are from the score.

Frequently asked questions

Short answers for candidates planning OET and registration in New Zealand.

Is OET accepted for health registration in New Zealand?

Yes for most professions. The eleven responsible authorities we read on 27 November 2026 all list OET: medicine, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy, dentistry, veterinary science, occupational therapy, optometry, medical radiation technology, dietetics and podiatry. We could not confirm a rule for speech-language therapy. Each authority sets its own scores, sittings and validity.

What OET score do nurses need for New Zealand?

The Nursing Council of New Zealand asks for at least 350 in Reading, Listening and Speaking and at least 300 in Writing. The scores can come from more than one sitting, but all of them must be reached within 12 months of your first sitting.

What OET score do doctors need for the Medical Council of New Zealand?

The Medical Council policy dated September 2026 asks for the Medical module with at least 350 in Speaking, Listening and Reading and at least 300 in Writing. You can use one sitting, or two sittings within 12 months if all four components are tested each time and no score is below 300. The wording on the NZREX Clinical application form is not confirmed, so check with the Council before you book.

Does New Zealand accept OET@Home?

It depends on the authority. The Nursing Council does not accept OET@Home, but it accepts OET on computer at a venue with the Speaking sub-test hosted online. The Optometrists and Dispensing Opticians Board accepts tests taken in person only. Immigration New Zealand does not accept at-home tests. For the other authorities it is not confirmed, so ask in writing before you book.

Can I combine two OET sittings for New Zealand?

Some authorities allow it and some do not. The Nursing Council, the Medical Council, the Pharmacy Council, the Physiotherapy Board and the Occupational Therapy Board publish rules for using more than one sitting. The Dental Council, the Veterinary Council and the Optometrists and Dispensing Opticians Board ask for one sitting or one result.

Is the OET score for a New Zealand visa the same as for registration?

No. Immigration New Zealand accepts OET for skilled residence at Grade C+ or higher in all four skills for the principal applicant. That is lower than the score of every health regulator in this guide, so a visa-level result does not satisfy registration. Plan for the regulator score.

Can I still take OET on paper in New Zealand in 2027?

From January 2027 OET says the paper test continues for Nursing and Medicine only. New Zealand is on the 16-country list that OET customer care sent by email in November 2026, but paper sessions are never guaranteed. Every other profession should plan for OET on computer at a venue.
