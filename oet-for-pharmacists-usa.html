OET for Pharmacists in the USA 2027: NABP, FPGEC, Scores and Test Centres

Yes: from 11 January 2027 the National Association of Boards of Pharmacy (NABP) accepts OET for Pharmacy as an alternative to TOEFL iBT for FPGEC Certification. It applies to candidates applying to sit the FPGEE in 2027 and later, and only to OET tests taken on or after that date.

Two things decide whether this helps you. NABP has not yet published the OET scores it will require, and the test must be taken on computer at a test centre in the United States or Canada. This guide sets out what NABP has confirmed, what it has not, and how to prepare in the meantime.

What changed, and from when

Until now, TOEFL iBT was the only English test NABP accepted for FPGEC Certification. NABP announced the change in a news release dated 6 October 2026 and wrote the rule into Step 3 of the FPGEC Candidate Application Bulletin (October 2026 edition).

OET for Pharmacy from 11 January 2027

  • NABP accepts OET for Pharmacy results from tests taken on or after 11 January 2027.
  • It applies to candidates applying to sit the FPGEE in 2027 and later. It cannot be used for the FPGEE 2026 application cycle.
  • Every NABP source names OET for Pharmacy. No other OET profession is mentioned.

NABP: FPGEC bulletin, Step 3

Earlier OET tests do not count

  • An OET taken before 11 January 2027 is not accepted, including one taken in late 2026.
  • NABP gives its own example: a passing OET result received in December 2026, with an application submitted in late January 2027, is still not accepted.

NABP help: does the OET replace the TOEFL iBT requirement?

OET or TOEFL iBT, not both

  • OET does not replace TOEFL iBT. NABP still accepts TOEFL iBT, and either test meets the requirement.
  • There are no exceptions or waivers. The bulletin says this applies to US citizens, US residents and native English speakers as well.

NABP help: which English exam does NABP accept?

TOEFL MyBest from 1 November 2026

  • From 1 November 2026, TOEFL MyBest Scores can be submitted to meet the acceptance requirement for the 2027 FPGEE administration.
  • MyBest combines your highest score in each section from valid TOEFL iBT tests taken within a rolling two-year period.

NABP help: MyBest scores for TOEFL iBT

OET scores: not yet published

This is the question every pharmacist asks first, and today there is no official answer.

OET scores: not yet published. NABP has not stated a minimum OET score or grade for any sub-test. Its help article said passing scores would be published “by the end of November 2026” (NABP help). We checked NABP's bulletin, help articles, programme page and news releases on 27 November 2026 and found no OET score. We will update this page when NABP publishes them. Until then, please do not guess, and do not trust any website or group that quotes an “NABP OET score”.

What NABP has said so far

The bulletin says more information, including score requirements, will be provided when available. The news release says minimum scores will be posted after NABP completes its standard setting later in 2026. The programme page points candidates to the bulletin for score requirements.

Bulletin, Step 3 and NABP foreign pharmacy programme page

What this means for booking

You cannot yet know your target, so you cannot yet know whether your level is enough. If you can wait, wait for the scores before you pay for a test and travel. If you prepare now, aim high in every sub-test rather than at a guessed minimum.

If you live in Egypt or the Gulf (our reading of the bulletin)

NABP does not print a sentence about Egypt, Saudi Arabia or the UAE. This is our reading of the rule as written. The test centre must be in the United States, a listed US territory or one of ten Canadian provinces. So an OET Pharmacy test taken in Cairo, Riyadh, Dubai or Doha would not meet the FPGEC rule, even at an approved OET venue and even on computer. OET@Home does not solve it either, because home editions are not accepted. The same location rule applies to TOEFL iBT, so this is not a reason to prefer one test over the other.

In practice, a pharmacist living outside North America would need to travel to a test centre in the USA or Canada for either test. That is a real cost. Before you plan a trip, wait for NABP to publish the OET scores and confirm your own case with NABP in writing.

The older TOEFL minimums of Reading 22, Listening 21, Speaking 26 and Writing 24 apply only to applications submitted before 1 January 2020. They are not the current minimums. Questions about the new TOEFL score scale go to ETS, not NABP (NABP help: ETS score scale).

Which one should you choose?

Nobody can answer that honestly until NABP publishes the OET scores. TOEFL iBT is a general English test. OET Pharmacy tests English through pharmacy tasks: a letter from case notes and counselling role-plays. If your working English is strongest in a pharmacy setting, OET may suit you better, but only the published scores will show which target is realistic for you.

Test centres: the jurisdiction rule

The bulletin says the OET must be completed in a secure testing environment at an approved computer-based OET test centre, located in an NABP member or associate member jurisdiction (Bulletin, Step 3).

The jurisdictions NABP lists

  • The 50 US states and the District of Columbia.
  • Guam, Puerto Rico and the Virgin Islands.
  • Ten Canadian provinces: Alberta, British Columbia, Manitoba, New Brunswick, Newfoundland and Labrador, Nova Scotia, Ontario, Prince Edward Island, Quebec and Saskatchewan.

NABP publishes no list of approved OET venues

The bulletin says “approved” but NABP gives no list of OET venues. Its help article on test locations states the location rule for TOEFL and, for OET, only tells candidates to check with OET for dates and locations (NABP help: where can I take the exam?). The bulletin is the governing document, so follow its location rule. We cannot tell you that any specific venue is approved by NABP, and nobody else can until NABP says so.

What OET's own venue finder shows

OET's finder (oet.com: book a test) lists computer-based venues in every US state and in Canada. When we read it on 27 November 2026, it held 228 computer venue records for the United States and 16 for Canada. A venue in the finder is an OET venue. It is not proof of an open date, and it is not an NABP approval. Search the finder for your state or province, choose OET on Computer, then confirm with NABP before you pay if you have any doubt.

The FPGEC steps, in order

This is a short map, not a substitute for the bulletin. NABP's Candidate Application Bulletin and foreign pharmacy programme page set the real requirements, fees and deadlines.

1. NABP e-Profile

Everything runs through your NABP e-Profile. For score reports, NABP says to make sure your email address matches the one on file with NABP.

2. FPGEC application

You apply for FPGEC Certification through NABP. The bulletin says an official score report and the other required documents must be submitted before evaluation of the application can begin.

3. Credential evaluation (ECE)

Your pharmacy degree and licence documents are evaluated as part of the application. Follow the bulletin exactly for which documents are needed and how they must be sent.

4. English: TOEFL iBT or OET Pharmacy

The programme page says you must pass TOEFL iBT or OET before you can be accepted for the FPGEE. NABP says it takes about four weeks to process a score after you request the report, and you check its status in the Document Status section of your e-Profile (NABP help: score report status).

5. FPGEE

Once accepted, you sit the Foreign Pharmacy Graduate Equivalency Examination. Passing it, with the other requirements met, leads to FPGEC Certification. Check the programme page for the exam schedule and time limits.

6. NAPLEX and MPJE with your state board

FPGEC Certification is not a licence. The licence comes from a state board of pharmacy, which sets its own steps such as NAPLEX, MPJE or a state law exam, and practice hours. Check your target state early.

The wider picture for doctors, nurses and physical therapists is in our USA pathway guide.

How to prepare for OET Pharmacy in the meantime

The OET Pharmacy test itself has not changed. The skills are the same whichever regulator you send the result to, so preparation started now is not wasted.

Writing

You write one letter from pharmacy case notes. Study a marked example first: our OET Writing sample for pharmacy shows what to select and how to organise it.

Speaking

Two role-plays in a pharmacy setting, usually counselling a patient or carer. Practise with our pharmacy role-play cards.

Practise on computer

NABP's rule is a computer-based test, so type your letters and read on screen from day one. Start with the free practice hub.

Know the delivery rules

Read how OET@Home differs from the computer test at a venue, and check venues on the OET test centres page. For other regulators, see OET score requirements.

Frequently asked questions

Does NABP accept OET for pharmacists?

Yes, from 11 January 2027. NABP will accept OET for Pharmacy as an alternative to TOEFL iBT for FPGEC Certification, for candidates applying to sit the FPGEE in 2027 and later. Only tests taken on or after 11 January 2027 count.

What OET score does NABP require?

NABP has not published the OET minimum scores yet. It said they would be published by the end of November 2026, and none was on its website when we checked on 27 November 2026. Do not rely on any number until NABP publishes it.

Can I use an OET Pharmacy result from 2026?

No. NABP says OET results from tests taken before 11 January 2027 will not be accepted, even if the application is submitted after that date.

Does NABP accept OET@Home?

No. NABP does not accept home edition scores for OET or TOEFL iBT. The OET must be taken on computer at an approved test centre.

Can I take OET in Egypt, Saudi Arabia or the UAE for FPGEC?

Not under the rule as the NABP bulletin writes it. The test centre must be in an NABP member or associate member jurisdiction: the 50 US states, the District of Columbia, Guam, Puerto Rico, the Virgin Islands or one of 10 Canadian provinces. This is our reading of the bulletin, so confirm with NABP before you book.

Do I need both OET and TOEFL iBT?

No. Either test meets the requirement. OET does not replace TOEFL iBT, which NABP still accepts, and there are no waivers for native English speakers or US citizens.

Does NABP accept TOEFL MyBest scores?

Yes, from 1 November 2026, for the 2027 FPGEE administration. Without MyBest, all four TOEFL iBT sections must be passed in one testing session.

What are the current TOEFL iBT minimums for FPGEC?

Reading 21, Listening 22, Speaking 25 and Writing 22 on the 0 to 30 scale, which is 4, 5, 5 and 4.5 on the scale ETS uses for tests from 21 January 2026. Scores are valid for 2 years.
