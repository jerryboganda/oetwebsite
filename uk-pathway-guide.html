Regulator overview

Choose the regulator before choosing the test date

The UK route is profession-specific. Each regulator has its own accepted English evidence, application portal, exam route, document standards and processing timeline.

Important: This page is educational guidance, not legal, immigration or regulator advice. Requirements, scores, fees and document rules can change. Verify on the official regulator website before booking OET or paying any fee.

Doctors - GMC pathway

GMCPLAB or approved postgraduate routeOET Medicine

For international medical graduates preparing for GMC registration with a licence to practise. The route commonly includes GMC Online, EPIC verification, PLAB 1 and PLAB 2, or an approved postgraduate qualification such as MRCP or MRCS.

English-test planning: OET Medicine is accepted by GMC as English evidence when it meets the current GMC requirements. Candidates must release scores to GMC through the OET portal and confirm certificate validity before applying.

Pathway steps

Confirm the routeCheck your primary medical qualification and choose PLAB, postgraduate qualification, sponsorship or another approved GMC route.
Prepare English evidenceBook the correct OET Medicine test and prepare all four subtests, especially writing and speaking.
Complete exams and documentsPlan PLAB 1, PLAB 2 or your approved alternative route, plus EPIC verification and certificates of good standing.
Apply and move to practiceSubmit through GMC Online, attend ID checks where required, then move toward NHS/private roles and visa planning.

Common mistakes

  • Booking a non-Medicine OET version.
  • Preparing with IELTS-style essays instead of OET letters.
  • Submitting before checking current GMC evidence rules.
  • Forgetting to release OET results to GMC.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

Referral, discharge and transfer letters with selective relevance and formal medical register.

Speaking

History taking, explaining diagnosis or investigations, refusals, concern handling and safety-netting.

Reading

Medical abstracts, NHS-style guidance, drug information and workplace clinical texts.

Listening

Doctor-patient consultations, clinical presentations and workplace exchanges in varied accents.

Nurses and midwives - NMC pathway

NMCCBT and OSCEOET Nursing

For internationally educated nurses and midwives joining the NMC register. The pathway usually includes NMC Online, eligibility checks, CBT, English evidence, OSCE in the UK, health and character checks, and receiving the NMC PIN.

English-test planning: NMC accepts OET Nursing with B in Listening, Reading and Speaking and C+ in Writing. Combining rules can apply only when all NMC conditions are met, so candidates must check the official NMC page before relying on combined scores.

Pathway steps

Self-assess and applyUse the NMC route for internationally trained applicants and prepare identity, qualification and registration evidence.
Pass CBTComplete the computer-based test through the accepted testing route.
Submit English evidenceUse OET Nursing or another accepted route according to current NMC rules.
Complete OSCE and final checksTravel to the UK, pass OSCE, complete health/character checks and receive your PIN.

Common mistakes

  • Treating Writing as easy because the minimum is C+.
  • Using doctor-style letters for nursing tasks.
  • Booking OET Medicine by mistake.
  • Trying to combine scores without meeting all NMC combining conditions.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

Nursing referrals, discharge letters, community team updates and patient education letters in a nursing voice.

Speaking

Patient education, calming anxious patients or relatives, health promotion and safe handover-style communication.

Reading

Care plans, infection control guidance, medicines safety bulletins and workplace policies.

Listening

Nurse-patient interactions, ward briefings and nursing-focused presentations.

Pharmacists - GPhC pathway

GPhCOSPAPOET Pharmacy

For pharmacists qualified outside Great Britain who need the Overseas Pharmacists Assessment Programme, foundation training and the GPhC registration assessment before joining the register.

English-test planning: The PDF guide highlights OET Pharmacy and IELTS routes for English evidence, but candidates must verify current GPhC and OSPAP university requirements because universities can set additional entry conditions.

Pathway steps

Confirm OSPAP eligibilityCheck degree equivalence and GPhC eligibility before applying to an OSPAP provider.
Prepare English evidenceUse OET Pharmacy, IELTS or accepted evidence only if it matches current GPhC and university rules.
Complete OSPAP and trainingFinish the postgraduate diploma and 52-week foundation training at an approved site.
Pass registration assessmentComplete the knowledge and calculations papers, then meet fitness-to-practise and registration requirements.

Common mistakes

  • Using Medicine or Nursing materials for Pharmacy writing.
  • Underestimating the calculations paper.
  • Missing university-specific English rules.
  • Assuming training or assessment steps can be skipped.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

Community pharmacy letters, dispensing-incident notes, supplier requests and GP communication.

Speaking

Medication counselling, adherence conversations, side-effect explanation and OTC sale refusal.

Reading

SmPCs, BNF-style entries, MHRA drug-safety alerts and pharmacy workplace policies.

Listening

Pharmacist-patient counselling, ward conversations and pharmacy team handovers.

Physiotherapists - HCPC pathway

HCPCInternational applicationOET Physiotherapy

For internationally trained physiotherapists applying through the HCPC international route. The pathway requires evidence of qualification, registration, programme content, clinical experience and standards mapping where applicable.

English-test planning: HCPC accepts profession-specific OET for physiotherapists when it meets the current score rules. Current HCPC guidance lists OET Physiotherapy with a total of 1400 and no element below 300, taken at an official test centre.

Pathway steps

Check HCPC eligibilityConfirm the international route and whether any English-language exemption applies.
Collect evidencePrepare qualification certificates, transcripts, programme details, clinical hours and registration evidence.
Submit English and mapping evidencePrepare OET/IELTS/TOEFL evidence and the Standards of Proficiency mapping document where required.
Respond and registerAnswer HCPC evidence requests promptly and move toward NHS or private-sector applications after registration.

Common mistakes

  • Practising with doctor or nurse role-play cards only.
  • Using vague language for mobility, gait, balance and rehabilitation progress.
  • Submitting an English certificate that is too old.
  • Skipping the Standards of Proficiency mapping document when required.

OET / English preparation focus

Writing

Referral, discharge and progress letters using physiotherapy terminology.

Speaking

Explaining conditions, home exercises, rehabilitation plans, adherence and chronic pain concerns.

Reading

Rehabilitation guidance, return-to-work material, ergonomic policies and clinical texts.

Listening

Physiotherapy consultations, MDT briefings and rehabilitation presentations.

Candidate checklist

Before you start the UK pathway

  • Identify the correct UK regulator for your profession.
  • Read the current English policy before booking OET.
  • Book the correct OET profession version, not the nearest available version.
  • Plan Writing and Speaking with profession-specific feedback.
  • Check certificate validity and document timelines before application.
  • Verify separate UK visa English requirements where relevant.

UK pathway FAQ

Short answers for healthcare professionals planning OET and UK registration.

Is OET accepted for UK healthcare registration?

OET is accepted by several UK healthcare regulators for specific professions and routes, including GMC, NMC and HCPC routes when the exact score and test version match current official rules.

Which OET profession should I book for the UK?

Book the profession version that matches your regulator route: Medicine for doctors, Nursing for nurses and midwives, Pharmacy for pharmacists, and Physiotherapy for physiotherapists.

Can I use the same OET score for registration and visa?

Regulator English evidence and UK immigration English requirements are separate. Candidates must verify UKVI rules independently.

Can Dr Hesham help me choose the right pathway?

Yes. Send your profession, target regulator, current OET level and timeline on WhatsApp for preparation guidance. This is educational support, not legal or regulator advice.

Not sure which UK pathway applies to you?

Send your profession, current OET level, target regulator and timeline. We will guide you toward the right preparation step.
