# OET "clubbing" (combining sittings) and regulator matrix: research notes

Researched 27 Nov 2026. Primary sources only (each regulator's own website or document). Every fact carries a short quote, the URL and the page's own date. "NOT CONFIRMED" means the regulator's own page could not be read or does not state the fact; nothing here is filled in from memory or from third-party blogs.

How pages were read: direct fetch where the site allowed it; the GMC, ECFMG, Home Affairs and ANMAC pages block automated fetches and were read in a real browser; the GPhC and GDC guidance PDFs were downloaded and read in full.

Grade/number equivalence used by the regulators themselves (NMC page): B = 350-440, C+ = 300-340, C = 250-290.

## 2. OET's own position (oet.com)

Source: OET Test Regulations, https://oet.com/en-us/test-regulations (no version date on page; footer "(c) 2026 OET"; read 27 Nov 2026).

- OET uses the word "clubbing" itself, and leaves the decision to the regulator: "Some Recognising Organisations accept Clubbing of scores from tests taken on different dates".
- "Check with your Recognising Organisation if your OET Test results are required to be held in one sitting."
- First attempt must be complete: "All four OET Test sub-tests must be taken the first time you sit the test to be able to receive your OET Test results."
- **Single sub-test resit exists at OET's end.** On a re-sit "you are not required to book all four sub-tests"; the page ties this to OET Test on Paper and to the Recognising Organisation's requirements. For OET on Computer / OET@Home it says a candidate can book "one or more additional OET sub-tests" after the first test, and to check with the Recognising Organisation first. (The page wording was read through a summarising fetch; re-read the regulation clause before quoting it verbatim on the site.)
- OET news post, 29 Oct 2026, https://oet.com/post/smarter-pathway-for-nurses : "the OET Test offers the option to retake an individual sub-test." It adds a warning to "check whether your credentialling organisation and state board of nursing accept results from a single sub-test retake."
- **Does OET issue one combined certificate for two sittings? NOT CONFIRMED.** Neither the test regulations nor https://oet.com/test/after-the-test (no date; "(c) 2026 OET") says OET merges two sittings into one Statement of Results. What the results page does say: "You will receive a Statement of Results with your score for each of the sub-tests on a scale from 0-500."
- **How a candidate shows results to a regulator** (same results page): "You can do this directly through your online account by granting permission to specific organisations." The page recommends organisations accept results "shared with them digitally". The practical inference (each sitting is shared separately and the regulator does the combining) is consistent with NMC and Ahpra wording but is an inference, not an OET statement.
- Validity is not OET's call (same page): "The length of time the results are recognised as valid is decided by the authorities that recognise the OET Test".
- No support.oet.com article on clubbing was found by search: NOT CONFIRMED.
- Related, OET news 16 Nov 2026, https://oet.com/post/oet-test-on-paper-update-2027 : "The OET Test on Paper will continue to be available for Nursing and Medicine only." (from January 2027). Consequence to check before publishing: the paper-specific single sub-test resit wording in the regulations will, from January 2027, only be relevant to Nursing and Medicine on paper.

## 3. United Kingdom

### GMC (doctors)
URL: https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/before-you-apply/evidence-of-your-knowledge-of-english/using-your-oet-certificate (no page date shown; read in a browser 27 Nov 2026; plain fetch returns 403).

- Formats: "We can accept the following versions of OET: OET on paper, OET on computer, OET@Home".
- Version: "You must take the Medicine version of the test."
- Score: "you achieved at least a grade 'B' in each testing area (speaking, listening, reading and writing)".
- No combining: "you achieved those grades in the same test" and "you obtained the grades in your most recent sitting of the test".
- Validity: "OET certificates are valid for two years."
- Timing: for PLAB the certificate "must be valid on the day you sit PLAB part 1"; for registration "it must be valid when we approve your application."
- Numeric equivalent of B is not printed on the GMC page (350 is the NMC/OET band figure).

### NMC (nurses, midwives, nursing associates)
URLs: https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/ and .../accepted-english-language-tests/oet/ (both "Last updated: 08/03/2024").

- Scores: "At least grade B (350 or above) for reading, listening, and speaking" and "At least grade C+ (300 or above) for writing".
- Combining allowed: "You can combine scores across two OET test sittings".
- Window: "Sit the tests within 12 months of each other." Both tests must also fall within the two years before the NMC assesses the complete application.
- Floors in every sitting used: "Grade C+ (300-340) for listening", "Grade C+ (300-340) for reading", "Grade C+ (300-340) for speaking", "Grade C (250-290) for writing".
- Target across the two sittings: B in reading, listening and speaking at least once, and "One writing score of at least Grade C+ (300-340)".
- Whole test each time: "you cannot take (or retake) individual test domains separately."
- Same test type only: "you cannot combine an IELTS test with an OET test."
- Validity: "Your language test scores are valid for two years."
- Formats: "We accept the OET on Paper, OET on Computer, and OET@Home test." and "we can accept OET@Home combined with OET on Paper or OET on Computer."
- Version: "All applicants should choose the Nursing examination" (no midwifery version mentioned).
- Wording to watch: the general page says a candidate may take the test "again, one or more times" but combining is "across two test sittings". Read as: unlimited attempts, any two qualifying sittings combined. More than two sittings combined: not offered.

### GPhC (pharmacists)
Source: "Guidance on evidence of English language skills", updated June 2025, https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/2025-06/gphc-guidance-on-evidence-of-english-language-skills-june-2025_0.pdf (PDF read in full, evidence type 1b, page 4).

- "A recent pass of the ... Pharmacy Occupational English Language Test (OET) Test on Paper or OET Test on Computer".
- Score and sitting: "a score of at least a B in each of the four areas of reading, writing, listening, and speaking, at one sitting".
- "We do not accept the OET@Home Test."
- Recency: "evidence of English language skills that is less than two years old" (at the point of application).
- Older results: "We may accept Pharmacy OET scores that are more than two years old if you can provide evidence that you have maintained" proficiency.
- Pharmacy technicians with international qualifications complete UK qualifications and "will not usually need to provide additional evidence".
- The GPhC web page https://www.pharmacyregulation.org/pharmacists/registering-pharmacist/internationally-qualified-pharmacists returned 403: its text is NOT CONFIRMED; the PDF above is the governing document.

### HCPC (allied health professions)
URL: https://www.hcpc-uk.org/registration/getting-on-the-register/international-applications/english-language-proficiency/ ("Page updated on: 05/11/2026").

- "We accept certificates from the below three test providers." (IELTS, TOEFL iBT, OET).
- "We accept profession-specific OET tests for the below professions if they meet the minimum scores."
- Formats: "We accept both the OET Test on Computer and the OET Test on Paper".
- Scores: "1400 with no elements below 300" (podiatry, dietetics, occupational therapy, physiotherapy, radiography); "1800 with no elements below 400" (speech and language therapy). These are TOTALS across four sub-tests plus a floor, not a per-sub-test B.
- Retake: "We we only accept retakes for one element of the OET" [sic] and "A retake must be completed within six months of the original test date".
- Validity: "The certificate must be no more than two years old when we receive your application."
- OET@Home: NOT CONFIRMED. The "official test centre and not at home" sentence appears under IELTS and TOEFL; the OET section names only Computer and Paper.

- HCPC professions without an OET entry on this page (e.g. paramedics, biomedical scientists, operating department practitioners): OET acceptance NOT CONFIRMED.

### GDC (dental professionals)
Sources: https://www.gdc-uk.org/registration/route-to-registration/english-language-controls (no date; "(c) 2026") and "Evidence of English language competence: Guidance for applicants" https://www.gdc-uk.org/docs/default-source/registration/english-language-controls/gdc-guidance-on-english-language-controls.pdf?sfvrsn=88adf167_2 (version log ends v4.1, July 2017; PDF read in full).

## 4. Australia

### Ahpra and the National Boards (re-verified)
URL: https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests ("Page reviewed 23/04/2026").

All four points held by the site are confirmed:

- New minimums: "Changes to the minimum scores required are in effect from 23 April 2026." Table 2 (tests taken on or after 23 April 2026): Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360. Table 1 (tests on or before 22 April 2026): B for listening, reading, speaking and C+ for writing.
- Professions: "All National Boards (except the Aboriginal and Torres Strait Islander Health Practice Board of Australia)" accept the same tests and "require the same minimum scores." **Writing does not differ by profession** on this page (350 for everyone under Table 2).
- Two sittings: "National Boards will accept test results either from one sitting, or a maximum of two test sittings within a 12-month period."
- Floors: "when using two test sittings no score in any component of the test(s) for either test sitting is below:" 320 listening, 340 reading, 350 writing, 350 speaking (Table 2). Under Table 1 no component in either sitting below C+.
- Whole test each time: "you are tested in all four components in each sitting".
- No OET single-skill retake for Ahpra: "National Boards have agreed to accept the IELTS One Skill Retake"; the page says it is not available for the other accepted tests.
- Different providers cannot be combined.
- Validity: "English language test results are valid for two years before the date you lodge your application for registration." With two sittings the two years run from the earlier sitting.
- Formats: "OET - Occupational English Test (on paper or computer based in testing centre)". At-home / remote-proctored versions "are not approved by National Boards and are not accepted."
- One nuance to state correctly on the site: the computer-based test at a test centre "has one remote proctored element which has been assessed by National Boards as acceptable." So "fully remote OET@Home not accepted" is right; "anything remote is rejected" would be wrong.
- Professions without their own OET version: an OET test from another registered profession is accepted if it meets the standard.
- Which table applies depends on the TEST date, not the application date.

## 8. Points that will trip up a "clubbing" page if stated loosely

1. Only three bodies in this research publish true two-sitting combining rules for OET: **NMC, Ahpra, ANMAC**. HCPC publishes a one-element retake instead. GMC, GPhC and ECFMG explicitly require one sitting.
2. The combining floors are different everywhere: NMC C+ / C+ / C (writing) / C+ ; Ahpra 320 / 340 / 350 / 350 ; ANMAC nothing below C+.
3. Both NMC and Ahpra require all four sub-tests in each sitting, so OET's single sub-test resit does not help for either. HCPC is the only regulator found that says it accepts a one-element OET retake.
4. NMC accepts OET@Home (and mixing it with paper/computer when combining). GPhC, Ahpra, ANMAC, Home Affairs and TruMerit do not accept OET@Home. GMC accepts it. ECFMG accepts it only where no venue exists in the country.
5. Validity clocks differ: NMC counts back two years from assessment of the complete application; Ahpra two years before lodging, from the earlier sitting; GMC valid when the application is approved (or on the day of PLAB 1); Home Affairs three years.
6. Writing thresholds differ by purpose in Australia: Ahpra 350, Home Affairs Proficient 380.
7. OET does not say it issues a combined certificate. Say "each sitting has its own Statement of Results; the regulator combines them", and flag it as our reading.
8. From January 2027 OET on Paper is Nursing and Medicine only (OET news 16 Nov 2026), which narrows who can use any paper-only resit option.
