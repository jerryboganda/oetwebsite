# OET@Home: verified facts for the guide

Researched 27 Nov 2026. Primary sources only (oet.com, cambridgeenglish.org, each regulator's own site).
Every line gives: the fact, a short verbatim fragment (kept under 15 words), the URL, and the date printed
on the page ("no date" = the page prints none; all pages were read on 27 Nov 2026).

How pages were read: plain fetch where it worked; a real browser for pages that block fetchers
(GMC, ECFMG, Home Affairs, HCPC accordion, oet.com posts and regulations). PDFs (GPhC, MCNZ, NAPRA) were
downloaded and text-searched. "NOT CONFIRMED" means the page could not be read or does not state the fact;
the reason is given each time. Nothing below is from memory.

Status words in Part B: ACCEPTED / CONDITIONAL / NOT ACCEPTED / NOT CONFIRMED.

## Part A. What OET@Home is and who can book it

### A1. Definition

- OET@Home is one of three delivery modes. "delivered in three modes: OET Test on Paper, OET Test on Computer, OET@Home" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, definitions; no date, footer 2026).
- It is fully remote-proctored on the candidate's own computer. "delivered online with full remote proctoring" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; no date).
- Delivery partner is Prometric. "provides the OET Test on Computer and OET@Home" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, definitions; no date).
- Fee shown on the page: "AUD $587"; booking closes "7 days before test day" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; no date).

### A2. Professions: THE TWO OFFICIAL SOURCES DISAGREE

- oet.com (test owner's product page): all 12. "All OET professions can sit OET@Home®." followed by the 12 names: Medicine, Nursing, Dentistry, Dietetics, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Podiatry, Radiology, Occupational Therapy, Veterinary Science, Optometry, Speech Pathology (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home, read in a browser; no date).
- cambridgeenglish.org: four only. "available for the medicine, nursing, pharmacy, physiotherapy professions" (https://www.cambridgeenglish.org/exams-and-tests/oet/test-overview/; no date, page mentions the "2026 catalogue").
- Neither page is dated, so which one is current is NOT CONFIRMED. Safe wording for the guide: "OET's own page lists all 12 professions; Cambridge's overview lists four. Check your profession in the booking tool before you rely on OET@Home." Do not publish either list as settled fact.

### A3. Countries: no official list exists

- oet.com publishes NO list of eligible or excluded countries (checked: the OET@Home page, the regulations, the who-recognises pages). NOT CONFIRMED as a list.
- The rule instead: only where there is no venue. "offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home, FAQ "Am I eligible to take OET at home?"; no date).
- Regulations, section 6.2, criterion 1: "Sitting the test where there is no Venue available" (https://oet.com/test-regulations; no date).
- Regulations, section 6.2, criterion 2 (exception): "approved on a case-by-case basis by a senior OET staff member", example given is disability or medical condition; apply through OET Support (same URL).
- Country match, section 4.2.1: must book "in the same country in which they will physically sit the test" (same URL).
- Proof of residency: "you must provide proof of residency to demonstrate your physical presence in the country" (same URL, section 6.2).
- Sanctions: "We do not offer testing or receive payments (funds) from candidates residing in Sanctioned Countries." (same URL, section 4.1). The NAMES of the sanctioned countries were not visible in the text read today: NOT CONFIRMED (the page refers to its FAQs).
- General caveat: "not all OET Test delivery dates and testing modes are available in all countries" (same URL, section 4.1).
- ECFMG repeats the no-venue rule in its own words (see B4).
- Consequence (inference, not an oet.com sentence): candidates in countries with venues (Egypt, Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Oman all have venues per the repo's country research of 19 Nov 2026) cannot normally book OET@Home.

### A4. Technical, room and ID requirements

- System minimums live in a separate document: "must meet the system minimum requirements set out in the OET@Home User Guide" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, 6.2). The User Guide itself was not opened: exact OS, browser and speed figures are NOT CONFIRMED (Prometric's page https://www.prometric.com/exams/oet/ returned HTTP 403).
- Software: "you must download the ProProctor application from Prometric on your device" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, 6.2).
- Hardware: "a video-enabled camera, speakers, and microphone for all sub-tests" (same URL, 6.2; fetched text).
- Headset for Listening: "With a wired headset at your computer." and FAQ "Bluetooth headphone is NOT allowed" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; no date).
- Screen and extras: "Additional monitors are not supported or allowed."; Windows resolution 1920 x 1080 at 100% scale; VPN, virtual machines and Bluetooth off (https://oet.com/post/oethome-test-day-guide; 19 June 2026).
- Room: "in a walled, indoor, well-lit space (with no transparent glass walls/partitions)", no CCTV, alone (https://oet.com/post/oethome-test-day-guide; 19 June 2026). Regulations: "ensuring that you are alone in a quiet room" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, 6.2).
- ID: the original document used at booking; "copies won't be accepted" (https://oet.com/post/oethome-test-day-guide; 19 June 2026). Booking: "you need to upload an image of your identification" (https://oet.com/test-regulations).
- Check-in: "You must log on at least 15 minutes before your scheduled test start time" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, 6.2; fetched text).
- Monitoring: "All tests are monitored by video and audio recording." (same URL, 6.2; fetched text).
- Break: "a 10-minute scheduled break", only after Reading Part A (same URL, 6.2; fetched text).

### A5. How Speaking is delivered in OET@Home

- Live human, OET's own platform: "Conducted via the OET Speaking platform with a live interlocutor." (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; no date).
- Timing: "usually 8 days before or after your Listening, Reading and Writing sub-tests" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, section 4.4).
- Platform change: Zoom was replaced. "From June, the OET Speaking sub-test will be delivered at home via a new platform." and it "applies for all OET@Home® and OET Test on Computer candidates" (https://oet.com/post/new-speaking-platform; page date 27 Aug 2026).
- Candidate installs a Secure Browser; laptop/desktop with webcam; no headphones for Speaking: "not permitted during the Speaking sub-test" (https://oet.com/post/oethome-test-day-guide-part-3; 19 June 2026).
- Environment check: "An interlocutor will check you in and conduct a 360-degree environment check" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, 6.2; fetched text).

### A6. Results timing

- OET@Home: "as early as 48 hours within completing all sub-tests" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; no date).
- For comparison, computer at venue: "in as little as 48 hours", "with 95% released within 6 calendar days" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; no date). Whether the 95% / 6-day figure also applies to OET@Home is not stated on the OET@Home page: NOT CONFIRMED.
- ECFMG states a different expectation for OET Medicine in general: "typically 10 business days after you take the test" (https://www.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/oet.html; no date, "2027 Pathways"). This is ECFMG's sentence, not OET's.

### A7. Does the Statement of Results show the mode?

- NOT CONFIRMED. The regulations describe the Statement of Results (scores 0-500 per sub-test, blank for a missed sub-test) and say nothing about printing the delivery mode. No oet.com page read today states it. Do not claim either way. (Regulators such as GPhC, NMBI and Ahpra verify results at source, so assume they can see the mode; that is an inference.)

### A8. OET on Computer at a venue vs OET@Home (the distinction that matters)

- OET on Computer: Listening, Reading, Writing are sat in a test centre. "delivered digitally in an approved test centre environment" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; no date).
- Its Speaking may still be at home: "Conducted at home via a secure platform or for select countries, conducted at venue." (same URL).
- Countries where computer candidates can sit Speaking at a venue (OET news post): Kenya, South Africa, New Zealand, Kuwait, Qatar, Saudi Arabia, Australia (Melbourne, Sydney), Nigeria (Lagos), Philippines, Uganda, Nepal, Ghana (Accra), Bangladesh, India (Mumbai, Gurgaon), Pakistan (Islamabad, Karachi, Lahore) (https://oet.com/post/speaking-sub-test-available-for-select-countries; 20 July 2026; list read through a fetch summary, re-check before publishing).
- OET@Home: all four sub-tests at home under remote proctoring (A1).
- Why it matters: several regulators that refuse OET@Home explicitly accept OET on Computer even with its remotely delivered Speaking: Ahpra (B3), Nursing Council of New Zealand (B5), and PSI mentions it (B2). So "Speaking by video" does not make a test "OET@Home".
- OET's own instruction: "Check with your Recognising Organisation if the OET Test delivery test mode you have selected is accepted" (https://oet.com/test-regulations).

### A9. January 2027: paper for Medicine and Nursing only

- OET has published it: "From January 2027, there are changes to how the OET Test on Paper is delivered." and "will continue to be available for Nursing and Medicine only" (https://oet.com/post/oet-test-on-paper-update-2027; 16 Nov 2026; read in a browser).
- Computer stays for everyone: "will remain available for all 12 healthcare professions at approved test venues" (same URL).
- Paper Speaking from 2027: at the venue, with the interlocutor "either in person or via a live video connection" (same URL).
- "16 countries": NOT CONFIRMED on oet.com. The post names no country and no number. The site's 16-country table comes from the OET customer-care email (as AGENTS.md already records), so keep attributing it to that email.
- The post says nothing about OET@Home. Any statement that OET@Home is or is not affected in 2027 is NOT CONFIRMED.
- Note: https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-paper still lists seven paper professions (Medicine, Nursing, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Radiology, Veterinary Science) with no 2027 note; it describes 2026.

### A10. Dead URLs (do not link)

- https://oet.com/test/oet-at-home and https://oet.com/test/test-delivery-modes return HTTP 404. The live page is https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home.

## Part B. Who accepts OET@Home (each regulator's own page)

### B0. OET's own claims (label as OET's claim, not the regulator's)

- OET publishes NO per-mode recognition list. The who-recognises country pages (UK, USA, Australia, New Zealand, UAE read today) name organisations but show no test-mode information (https://oet.com/test/who-recognises-oet/recognising-organisations/united-kingdom and siblings; no date).
- OET's general warning: "may not be accepted by all recognising organisations or authorities in every country" and FAQ "Not all regulators accept OET@Home." (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home).
- OET claim, UK doctors: "We will, therefore, accept remotely proctored OET results for both PLAB and registration." (GMC quoted in https://oet.com/post/uks-general-medical-council-to-accept-oethome; page date printed 13 November 2023, although the text describes a pilot "at the end of June" and a September launch, so the printed date may be a re-publication date).
- OET claim, US nurses: "OET@Home, the remotely proctored version of the test, is not accepted by these organisations." (the four USCIS-authorised nurse credentialling organisations; https://oet.com/post/smarter-pathway-for-nurses; 29 October 2026).
