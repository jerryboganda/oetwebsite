# OET pathways research: Canada, Ireland, New Zealand, Singapore

Research date: 27 Nov 2026. Read-only web research for the pathway guides. Primary sources only (each regulator's own site); oet.com is used only as a pointer and is labelled as OET's own claim.

## How to read this file

- **Evidence level** is stated for every fact:
  - **DIRECT** = the regulator's page or PDF was fetched on 27 Nov 2026 and the text read (PDFs were text-extracted locally with `pdftotext`, so those quotes are exact).
  - **DIRECT (summarised fetch)** = the page was fetched, but through a summarising fetch tool. Quotes are the tool's verbatim extracts; re-open the page before publishing a quote character-for-character.
  - **SNIPPET** = the regulator's page appeared in a web search result and the text comes from the search extract; the page itself could not be fetched (HTTP 403/404/429). Treat as a lead, re-check before publishing.
- **NOT CONFIRMED** = the page could not be fetched or the fact is not stated on the source. Never fill these from memory.
- "Page date" is the date printed on the page/PDF. Where a page prints none, that is said ("no date on page").
- OET grade/score equivalence used by the regulators themselves: B = 350-440, C+ = 300-340 (as printed by NMBI and CORU).
- Nothing here is legal or immigration advice. Every regulator can change its rules without notice.

### 1.2 Nursing and midwifery: NMBI

Source: https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/English-Language-Requirements (DIRECT, summarised fetch; no "last updated" date on page).

- Three pathways: trained in English in a recognised country, registered and practised in English, or a test. Quote: "taught and examined in English in one of the NMBI recognised countries"; "3 years post registration practice within the last five years".
- Recognised countries listed on the page: Australia, Canada, New Zealand, The United States of America, United Kingdom.
- OET: "OET (Nursing) with Grade B in three components and C+ in one component." The page table prints Listening B (350-450), Reading B, Writing C+ (300-340), Speaking B.
- IELTS Academic: "Overall IELTS score of 7.0 with a minimum of 7.0 in any three components and 6.5 in any one component."
- Validity: "We only accept IELTS/OET test results that are less than two years old."
- Combining: "We only accept one test result and do not accept combined results".
- At home: "We do not accept IELTS online or OET at home." Also "We do not accept IELTS One Skill Retake".
- Effective date printed on page: the English requirement applies "From 2 April 2018".
- Inconsistency to flag: the table shows Writing as the C+ component, while the rule sentence says "C+ in one component" without naming it. Publish the rule sentence, not the table.
- Route (two stages on this page): "Qualification recognition application" then "Registration application" (English evidence is required at the second stage).
- Compensation measures (SNIPPET from https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/Outcomes-of-Assessment/Compensation-Measures and NMBI guidance PDFs, not directly fetched): a compensation measure is "either a period of adaptation or an aptitude test"; the aptitude test (run by RCSI: theory paper + OSCE) is offered for the General Nurse division; "You will have 12 months to complete a compensation measure." Re-open before publishing.

### 1.3 Medicine: Medical Council (Comhairle na nDochtúirí Leighis)

Source: https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/english-language.html (DIRECT, summarised fetch).

- Effective date: "These requirements apply to all new applications received on or after 1st March 2025."
- OET: "Applicants must have passed the OET Medicine category and attained a minimum grade B in each module" "in a single sitting within the last two years".
- IELTS: "Academic IELTS examination with an overall band score of 7.0" and "a minimum score of 6.5 in each module". Cambridge CAE at "Level C1 or C2 within the last two years" is also listed.
- Validity: "All certificates must also be dated within two years of the date of registration." "Certificates older than two years are not acceptable."
- At home: "We do not accept OET @ Home online tests." Also "We do not accept the IELTS Indicator or IELTS Online tests."
- Combining: not allowed by implication of "single sitting"; no clubbing rule is printed.
- Who is exempt (paraphrase of the page): graduates of Irish, EU/EEA, UK, Australian, New Zealand and US medical schools, EU citizens, and applicants for specialist, visiting EEA or internship registration; the page says this "will likely change in the future". "All other applicants are required to provide this evidence."
- Route / PRES: source https://medicalcouncil.ie/Registration-Applications/First-Time-Applicants/Pre-Registration-Examination-System-PRES-Frequently-Asked-Questions.html (DIRECT, summarised fetch). The page lists PRES exemptions: "I have an equivalent internship which meets the standards for exemption from the PRES"; a higher qualification plus "an internship of 12 months which includes 3 months in Medicine and 3 months in Surgery"; or having "completed 3 years in an accredited training programme". Countries whose internships are listed as equivalent: Australia, New Zealand, Pakistan, South Africa, Sudan, Malaysia (start-date conditions for Pakistan and South Africa).
- NOT CONFIRMED: the structure of PRES (levels, MCQ/OSCE) and whether PLAB/USMLE/AMC/MCCQE passes exempt from parts of it. The FAQ page fetched does not state them; the main PRES page and the Guide to Registration PDF were not read.

### 1.4 Pharmacy: PSI (Pharmaceutical Society of Ireland)

Source: https://www.psi.ie/registration/pharmacists/first-time-registration/language-requirements (DIRECT, summarised fetch; no page date, footer "© 2026").

- Who: "Language competence requirements apply to all qualified pharmacists who have completed their training outside of Ireland".
- Standard: PSI "adopted the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR) C1 level as the minimum required standard".
- Accepted tests: "Currently IELTS, TOEFL, CAE and OET tests are accepted by the PSI."
- OET (Pharmacy) minimums from the page table: Listening 350, Reading 350, Writing 300, Speaking 350. IELTS Academic: Speaking 7, Writing 6.5, Reading 7, Listening 7, Overall 7.
- Validity: "Test results are valid for two years from date of test."
- Sittings: "All components of the IELTS, TOEFL, CAE, and OET tests must be attempted at the same sitting". "The PSI does not accept one-skill retakes."
- Super-scoring note: the fetched table also carries "base scores for super-scoring" (IELTS 6.5 each / 7 overall; OET 300 each). The exact super-scoring rule (how many sittings, window) was NOT read in full. NOT CONFIRMED: do not publish a PSI combining rule until the table is re-read.
- At home: "Please note that home-based tests are not accepted." The page also notes "From June 2026, the OET Speaking sub-test will be delivered at home through a new platform." (the page's reading is that this concerns OET on Computer candidates; confirm with PSI whether such results are accepted).
- Change: "from 1 June 2026, we will no longer accept a letter from a university stating that the pharmacy course was conducted" (in English as proof).
- Route (TCQR): https://www.psi.ie/registration/pharmacists/first-time-registration/qualification-obtained-outside-eu (DIRECT, summarised fetch). "The first step is an application for recognition of your qualification." Then a Holistic Assessment, then an MCQ and/or OSCE run by RCSI, then registration. Language: "Assessment of language competence must be submitted when making an application for registration." Exam dates printed: 2027: 16-17 February, 28-29 July, 17-18 September, 19-20 November ("these dates are subject to change"). Fees printed on the page: EUR 500 application, EUR 1,000 holistic assessment, EUR 2,250-3,000 exam.

### 1.5 CORU professions (physiotherapy, OT, dietetics, radiography, speech and language therapy, podiatry, optometry)

Source: https://www.coru.ie/health-and-social-care-professionals/registration/registration-requirements/language/ (DIRECT, summarised fetch, read three times; no page date).

Exact table cells returned:

- Physiotherapists RB: OET (Physiotherapy) "A minimum of Grade B (350) in three components and a minimum of Grade C+ (300) in one component"; IELTS Academic overall 7.0, no element below 6.5.
- Occupational Therapists RB and Dietitians RB: same OET rule (OET Occupational Therapy / OET Dietetics); IELTS 7.0, none below 6.5.
- Optical RB: "OET (Optometry) A minimum of Grade B (350) in three components and a minimum of Grade C+ (300) in one component"; IELTS 7.0, none below 6.5.
- Podiatrists RB: "OET (Podiatry) A minimum of Grade B in three components and a minimum Grade C+ (300) in one component"; IELTS 7.0, none below 6.5.
- Radiographers RB: "OET (Radiography) A minimum of Grade B (Minimum Score of 350) in all components"; "IELTS Academic Overall score 7.0 (with no element below 7.0)".
- Speech & Language Therapists RB: "OET (Speech Pathology) A minimum of Grade B (400) in three components and B (350) in one component"; "IELTS Academic Overall score 8.0 (with no element below 7.5)".
- Social Workers, Social Care Workers, Medical Scientists: no OET listed (IELTS only).

Rules:

- Validity: "All language test certificates must be dated within two years of the date an applicant submits their application for registration."
- Combining: "CORU will accept results from a maximum of two test sittings." The repeat "must be taken from the same provider as the previous/initial test" and "must be taken within six months of the initial test." Also "the overall minimum score must be met on both test sittings." Floors: IELTS no component below 6.5; OET no sub-test below C+.
- Partial retakes: "CORU does not accept IELTS One Skill retakes or OET individual sub-tests (Listening, Reading, Writing, or Speaking)".
- At home / online: the page sentence is "While CORU recognises both IELTS and OET, please note that online/at-home versions are exclusively accepted for the OET; IELTS results must come from a physical test." Read literally this says at-home is accepted for OET only. The sentence is ambiguous enough that it must be confirmed with CORU before the site states "CORU accepts OET@Home". Status: stated on page, meaning NOT CONFIRMED.
- Exemptions (paraphrase): a CORU-approved qualification; a qualification gained in the previous two years "acquired through the medium of English or Irish"; or having lived and practised through English in an English-only country for "not less than 2 years in the past five-year period".
- Route (SNIPPET, CORU recognition policy July 2023 and Compensation Measures page, https://coru.ie/health-and-social-care-professionals/registration/international-applicants/compensation-measures/): recognition of the international qualification first; where substantial differences remain "you will be offered a compensation measure" (period of adaptation or aptitude test); the aptitude test "incurs a fee of €500" per the snippet; a period of adaptation cannot exceed three years. Re-open before publishing fees.
- CORU note: the SLT IELTS figure (8.0 / none below 7.5) is unusually high; it is what the page prints.

### 1.6 Dentistry: Dental Council of Ireland

Sources: https://www.dentalcouncil.ie/registration/language-proficiency and https://www.dentalcouncil.ie/registration/examination-details (both DIRECT, summarised fetch; no page date).

- Requirement date: "applicants, from 1 November 2018, for registration must be proficient in the English language".
- OET (Dentistry): "minimum score of B" in each module. IELTS Academic: "minimum score of 7.0" in each module.
- Validity: "This Certificate must have been obtained no more than 2 years prior to the submission of your application for registration."
- At home: "We do not currently accept OET@Home or IELTS Online."
- Combining sittings: NOT CONFIRMED (the page does not address it).
- Alternatives: qualification "was undertaken in English", or practice as a fully registered dental professional in a member state where English is official.
- Route for non-EEA dentists: "normally required to present for and pass a special examination." Part 1 (three written papers + operative bench test) then Part 2 (written papers, OSCE, clinical vivas); maximum two attempts at Part 1, three at Part 2. English evidence must be dated "within the two years prior to receipt of the completed application." 2027 sitting: applications "during the week of 11th – 15th January 2027"; Part 1 Dublin 22-25 March 2027; Part 2 Cork June 2027 (dates to be confirmed). Places are allocated by random selection (80% to applicants established in Ireland).

### 2.2 Medicine: MCNZ

Source: "Policy on English language requirements", https://www.mcnz.org.nz/assets/Policies/English-language-policy.pdf (DIRECT, PDF text read; footer "Policy - English language requirements - September 2026"; PDF created 25 Sep 2026; version table lists revisions to "September 2026" and next review "September 2029").

- Requirement E: "Pass the Medical Module of the Occupational English Test (OET) by achieving a minimum score in each component of the test."
- "Minimum required scores: Speaking 350 Listening 350 Reading 350 Writing 300".
- Sittings: "One sitting; or A maximum of two test sittings in a 12-month period only if the applicant: is tested in all four components in each sitting".
- Floor: "when using scores from two test sittings, no score in any component from either test sitting is below 300."
- Validity: "OET results are valid within: 2 years of an application for registration. 2 years of NZREX Clinical examination date (new candidate)."
- IELTS Academic (requirement D): Speaking 7.0, Listening 7.0, Reading 7.0, Writing 6.5; same two-sitting rule with floor 6.5; "Council also accepts the IELTS One Skill Retake."
- Other ways to meet the requirement (no test): NZ primary medical qualification; English as first language plus a qualification from Australia, UK, Ireland, USA, or an English-instruction Canadian or South African school; work as a registered doctor "for at least 2 years within the 5 years immediately prior to application" where English was the first and prime language, with two senior-doctor referees; and others (F, G, H).
- OET@Home / OET on computer: NOT CONFIRMED. The policy text contains no mention of "home", "online" or "computer".
- Inconsistency to flag: a search extract of MCNZ's NZREX application form (NZREX4, Sept 2025) reportedly states 350 in each of four components in one result. The form was not read directly. The policy (September 2026) is the newer document; confirm with MCNZ before publishing NZREX-specific scores.
- Route: NZREX Clinical, https://www.mcnz.org.nz/registration/getting-registered/registration-exam-nzrex/ (DIRECT, summarised fetch). "We use the Objective Structured Clinical Examination (OSCE) format." 12 stations, 3 hours. Prerequisite: "Have passed one of the following examinations within 5 years of the NZREX Clinical date you are allocated to." (PLAB Part 1, MCCQE Part I, AMC MCQ, USMLE Steps 1 and 2 CK). English results "must be valid (passed within two years) of your allocated NZREX Clinical exam date." A pass is valid 5 years and must be followed by a PGY1 post with an accredited provider. Next exams printed: 6 March 2027 and 4 September 2027.
- NOT CONFIRMED: the other MCNZ pathways (comparable health system, competent authority, vocational/specialist) were not read in this session.

### 2.3 Nursing: Nursing Council of New Zealand

Sources: "English Language Competence Policy Guidance", dated 19 August 2022, https://nursingcouncil.org.nz/common/Uploaded%20files/Public/Nursing/IQN/English%20Language%20Competence%20Policy%20Guidance.pdf (DIRECT, PDF text read); and https://nursingcouncil.org.nz/IQN/IQN/H5.aspx (DIRECT, summarised fetch; no page date).

- "For the OET, you must achieve a minimum score of 350 in reading, listening, and speaking. You must achieve a minimum score of 300 in writing."
- IELTS Academic: 7 in reading, listening, speaking; 6.5 in writing. "We do not accept PTE." (H5 page).
- Clubbing: "The minimum scores in the English language tests can be achieved over more than one sitting of OET or IELTS."
- Window: "all required scores must be met within 12 months of first sitting the test, and all sittings must be completed within three years of commencing your application."
- One test brand only: "You cannot, for example, use OET results for reading and writing and IELTS results for listening and speaking."
- Mode: "Tests must be conducted at an OET or IELTS testing centre, either on paper or on computer."
- At home: "The Nursing Council does not currently accept testing conducted completely online, such as OET@home." Exception: the speaking component of OET's on-computer venue-based option.
- No minimum floor per sitting is printed.
- Education pathway (no test): initial nursing education in Canada, Ireland, Singapore, the United Kingdom, the United States of America. Registration pathway: currently registered in Canada, Ireland, UK or USA where a test was sat to gain that registration. Australian registrants: no evidence needed under the Trans-Tasman Mutual Recognition Act 1997.
- Route (H5 page, paraphrase): identity check and application via TruMerit; current overseas registration; equivalent qualification; at least 1,800 hours of post-registration practice; competence assessment for some RNs and all ENs: "an online theoretical exam that tests nursing knowledge, taken at a Pearson test centre overseas or in New Zealand", then a two-day orientation and three-hour OSCE in Christchurch; the Welcome to Aotearoa New Zealand Programme; criminal history check.

- This language rule is printed on the Qualifying Examination (QE) criteria page. The conditional registration page itself mentions no English test.
- Conditional registration: work under "the supervision of a fully registered dentist approved by the Council."
- SNIPPET (SDC announcement, not fetched): from 1 January 2029 foreign-trained dentists must pass a QE before conditional registration; those who apply before that date are not affected. Re-check before publishing.

### 4.6 Allied health: AHPC (physiotherapy, occupational therapy, speech-language therapy, diagnostic radiography, radiation therapy)

Source: https://www.ahpc.gov.sg/for-professionals/apply-for-registration/requirements-for-registration-application/ (DIRECT, summarised fetch; "Last updated on 24 July 2026").

- No ELP needed where the basic qualification is from Singapore, Australia, Canada (except Quebec), New Zealand, the Republic of Ireland, South Africa, the UK or the USA. Others must demonstrate ELP before conditional registration.
- OET minimums in Table 1: Diagnostic Radiography and Radiation Therapy: Grade B or above. Occupational Therapy and Physiotherapy: Grade B or above (the table adds that computer-based testing is also accepted for Physiotherapy). Speech-Language Therapy (with effect from 1 Aug 2025): Grade B (400) in three components and B (350) in one.
- IELTS Academic: 6.0 in all four (radiography/radiation therapy); 7.0 in all four (OT, physiotherapy); overall 7.5 with none below 7.0 (speech-language therapy).
- Only results taken within 2 years before the registration application date are accepted; all components must be met in a single examination sitting; no limit on attempts.
- Verbatim caution: these cells were returned as a summarised table, not as quoted sentences. Re-open the page and copy the wording before publishing quotes.
- Inconsistency to flag: OET "Grade B or above" for radiographers sits beside an IELTS minimum of only 6.0. It is what the table shows; confirm with AHPC.
- 2022 announcement (https://www.ahpc.gov.sg/publications-and-newsroom/announcements/revision-to-english-language-proficiency-elp-requirements-for-diagnostic-radiographers-and-radiation-therapists/, dated "26 September 2022", "with effect from 1 January 2023"): "There is no change to the ELP score requirement for IELTS and OET for the time being."
- Route: applicants whose qualification is not on AHPC's recognised list take a Qualifying Examination: "The examination provider will not accept applications to attempt the QE without AHPC's referral letter." Speech-language therapy applicants must meet ELP before enrolling for the QE (SNIPPET).
