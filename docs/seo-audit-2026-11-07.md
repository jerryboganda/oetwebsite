## 2. Checked and already fine

Canonicals, one H1 per page, title and description lengths, sitemap (78 read 7 Nov), robots, hreflang, schema, merchant listings (44 valid,
0 invalid), `www` and `http` redirects. The `www` URLs in Search Console are old records: they 301 correctly.

"Crawled, currently not indexed" listed 19 pages on 24 Oct, including `service.html` and the doctors course page. Most of them have since
earned impressions. Check this report again after the next update, and use Request indexing on any money page still listed.

## 3. What only the owner can do (highest impact first)

1. **Backlinks. This decides whether head terms move.**
   - OET Preparation Partner programme: free training and a listing on oet.com, the most authoritative link in the niche. Aim for Premium.
   - Reviews: ask every passing student for a Google and Trustpilot review with the score card. A review profile also gives you a link.
   - YouTube: put the matching guide URL at the top of every video description, and name the channel "OET with Dr Hesham".
   - "Best OET course" listicles and directories: talkdrill, learnenglish.life, collegedunia, Laimoon, Edarabia, practicetestgeeks.
   - NHS trust international-recruitment teams, IMG Connect, nursing recruiters, and university or hospital English-support pages. A free
     resource such as the score calculator or the fee guide is an easy thing for them to link to.
   - Answer questions as yourself, with disclosure, on r/OET, r/IMGreddit, r/NursingUK, Quora and LinkedIn, linking the guide that answers.
2. **Google Business Profile**: if none exists for the business, create and verify one. It supports the brand panel and reviews.
3. **Bing Webmaster Tools**: verify the site and submit the sitemap. Bing feeds Copilot and ChatGPT search, which already sends you traffic.
4. **Credentials in `site/src/data/person.json`**: degree, university, registration body and number. They appear in the schema and author box.
5. **Source or remove** "Rated 5.0 on Google" and the 15K / 20K / 25K counters.

## 4. Decision needed: a page for "oet recalls"

"oet recalls" has 16 impressions at position 10.8 with a click, and you sell recall-based material, so a page would probably rank. **Not built**:
OET's regulations treat sharing test content as malpractice, and a public page promoting recalls could hurt a Preparation Partner application.
Your call.

## 5. How to judge this work

Allow 4 to 8 weeks. Watch in Search Console: indexed page count, impressions and CTR on the new URLs, and position for "oet exam fees in uk",
"oet exam dates 2027", "oet passing score", "oet refund policy", "oet writing samples". Keep the dates page current: it is the top page, and
stale dates are what cost it this time.
