## P2: why it was not built

1. **Owner decision on file.** `docs/seo-audit-2026-11-07.md` section 4 records the owner's decision of 8 Nov 2026 *not* to publish a recalls page: OET's regulations treat sharing test content as malpractice, and a public recalls page could hurt a Preparation Partner application. `AGENTS.md` and `docs/growth-brief-2026-11/README.md` re-confirm it on 19 Nov. The audit says the model was "agreed with Dr Hesham", but nothing in the repo shows that decision was reversed.
2. **No data.** The page is a monthly archive of real candidate recalls from the WhatsApp groups. The repo holds none, and inventing exam questions is not an option. A page with an empty archive would be thin filler (`docs/editorial-plan.md`).

**Outcome (21 Nov 2026): the owner confirmed "keep it out".** The decision stands; the audit's recalls item is closed. Only if it is reopened would the owner need to (a) explicitly reverse the 8 Nov decision, ideally after checking the OET Preparation Partner terms, and (b) supply the monthly recall material; the page, its Arabic twin, FAQ and hreflang would then follow the same pattern as the other pairs.

- **Claims already on the English success-stories page** that nothing in the repo substantiates and that the Arabic page translates faithfully: "Verified, unedited results", "Scores are never edited", "most arrive on WhatsApp within hours". Keep only what can be evidenced.
- **Paper availability after January 2027** is still described as it is today on the pricing selector (Paper-Based for every profession) and on pages that list paper dates. Decide whether to change them (owner-controlled pricing UI).
- **Statements in the posts that are the site's reading, not OET's words**: "tests taken in 2026 are unaffected" and the OET@Home answer (the announcement is silent on both). Plausible, but confirm with OET customer care if you want certainty.
- The English pricing page's Packages 13/14 promise a "48-72h turnaround, excluding Friday"; 15/16 do not say it.
- The Arabic posts use Egyptian dialect as supplied; the other Arabic pages use Modern Standard Arabic.
