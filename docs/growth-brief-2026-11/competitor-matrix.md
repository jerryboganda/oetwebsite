# Competitor matrix (internal, unpublished)

Date checked: 19 Nov 2026.

Status: internal working document. It is not published and must not be linked from the site.
Publishing any comparison that names a competitor (page, post, ad, llms.txt, social) needs the owner's explicit approval first.

Method and limits:

- Every finding below comes from a page fetched on 19 Nov 2026; the source URL follows the finding. Anything not found on a fetched official page is marked "unknown".
- Pages were read through an automated fetch-and-extract tool, not by a person in a browser. Before any figure is reused (especially prices, ratings and counts), re-open the cited URL and confirm it by eye.
- Prices are as displayed on the date checked; providers change them without notice. Several OET Online pages carry old "last modified" dates, noted in the rows.
- No row characterises a provider's quality. "Not stated" means the fetched page did not say; it does not mean the provider lacks the feature.
- Provider 3 (OET Ultimacy) and provider 4 (Dr Ahmed El Gendy) turned out to be the same business: the OET Ultimacy site names Dr Ahmed Elgendy as its founder and tutor. They are kept as two sections because the brief listed them separately; section 4 points to section 3.
- The oet.com preparation partner directory could not be read (see "Could not verify"), so the "listed on oet.com" row records only what each provider says about itself.
