- The UAE is NOT in OET's list of countries where the OET on Computer Speaking sub-test can be taken at a venue. The list names Kuwait, Qatar and Saudi Arabia from the Gulf (https://oet.com/post/speaking-sub-test-available-for-select-countries, dated 20 Jul 2026; "Candidates in the below regions can now take the Speaking sub-test at a venue"). So for OET on Computer in the UAE, Speaking follows OET's standard arrangement (already on site: "Speaking usually online from home on a different day").
- OET tells candidates to check that the Speaking mode is accepted by their regulator (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "please ensure the Speaking mode you select is accepted by the organisation")
- OET@Home: no OET@Home option was listed in the UAE results (the filter offered only Computer and Paper), and OET says OET@Home exists where venue testing is not available (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; "offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available"). OET does not publish a country list, so "OET@Home is not offered in the UAE" is an inference from these two facts, not a sentence on oet.com.
- Booking deadlines: paper closes 24 days before the test, computer 7 days before (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "Bookings for OET Test on Paper close 24 days before the test date")
- Already on site (oet-test-dates-and-fees): OET on Paper covers 7 professions (Medicine, Nursing, Dentistry, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy, Radiography, Veterinary Science), OET on Computer and OET@Home all 12; computer results in as little as 48 hours, 95% within 6 calendar days; paper results from 13 days. With 10 of 12 UAE venues paper-only, candidates in the other five professions need one of the two computer venues.

## 2. Test fee and payment

- The full OET test costs AU$587 in the UAE, the same for paper, computer and OET@Home (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "costs AU$587 (or US$455 for OET on Computer at a venue and OET@Home in the US)")
- The OET@Home page shows the same fee (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home; "AUD $587")
- Payment is by card (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-do-I-book-OET; "be sure to have your Visa or MasterCard and ID ready")
- Single sub-test re-sits: AU$315 / AU$415 / AU$515 for 1 / 2 / 3 sub-tests, OET on Paper only, after a full test (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "Here are the prices (including the AU$7 online booking fee)")
- Taxes: the two OET fee pages read today say nothing about VAT, GST, a UAE price or AED payment. See "Could not verify".
- Already on site (oet-exam-fees): AU$587 is shown as approximately AED 1,480, with the note that the bank or card provider sets the real rate and may add a foreign-currency fee. This is the site's own conversion, not an OET price.

## 3. Do DHA, DOH and MOHAP require or accept an English test?

One rulebook covers all three: the Unified Healthcare Professional Qualification Requirements (PQR),
third version, dated April 2025 on its cover, issued jointly by MOHAP, DOH, DHA and the Sharjah Health
Authority (https://www.doh.gov.ae/en/pqr, page "Last updated: 17 Nov 2026"; "the Third version of Unified
Healthcare Professionals Qualification Requirements (PQR)"). PDF read today:
https://www.doh.gov.ae/-/media/D112FF75EF0140439A733B16081292DF.ashx

- The PQR has no general English test requirement in its "Licensure General Requirements" (qualification, experience, licence and good standing, primary source verification, examination). English proof appears only in specific exemption clauses (PQR PDF, sections 1.1 to 1.7 read in full).
- Where English proof is asked for, OET at grade C or above is one of four accepted proofs (PQR PDF, clause 13.1.15.4; "Occupational English Test (OET) of not less than C")
- The other accepted proofs are TOEFL 500, IELTS 5.0 and Cambridge 169 (PQR PDF, clause 13.1.15.4; "IELTS score of not less than 5.0")
- Case 1, nurses and midwives: the experience exemption for holders of a licence from a listed country needs English proof only in two situations (PQR PDF, clause 13.1.15.4; "if the healthcare professional did not complete the university studies in English")
- Case 2, allied health professionals: the same experience exemption and the same four proofs (PQR PDF, clause 17.1.15.4; "Occupational English Test (OET) of not less than C"). Note: this clause prints the IELTS figure as "0.5", an apparent typo for 5.0; do not quote it.
- Case 3, exam exemption (Chapter Six, Exam Equivalency Criteria): the note under the listed-country licence criteria repeats the same four proofs for several professions (PQR PDF, Chapter Six; "below evidence of English language proficiency must be provided")
- All authority examinations are held in English (PQR PDF, clause 1.5.3.1; "All examinations will be conducted in English, and no translator is allowed")
- DHA: the Sheryan "Get Registered" service page lists PSV and assessment as prerequisites and does not mention an English test (https://services.dha.gov.ae/sheryan/wps/portal/home/services-professional/service-description?scode=NRG&CATALOGUE_TYPE=PROFESSIONAL; "Pass any required Computer Based Testing (CBT) assessments")
- DHA licensing manual v1.2 (issued 08/05/2025): no IELTS or OET requirement in the text (full text searched); the only language rule is for documents (https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20For%20Licensing%20Healthcare%20Professionals-Final202555984.pdf; "translation if the original is not in English or Arabic")
- DHA oral assessments are conducted in English (https://www.dha.gov.ae/uploads/052025/Manual%20for%20DHA%20Professional%20Assessment-Final2025513536.pdf, clause 6.10; "conduct the assessment session in English language only")
- OET's own UAE page (not an authority page) claims acceptance without naming a body or score (https://oet.com/test/who-recognises-oet/recognising-organisations/united-arab-emirates; "OET is accepted for residency programs in Abu Dhabi and Dubai")
- Already on site (gulf-pathway-guide): "The UAE Unified PQR (MOHAP, DOH, DHA and SHA) lists OET at grade C or above as one accepted proof of English in specific cases, such as some experience or exam exemptions based on a licence from a listed country." Confirmed today against the PDF.
- Safe wording for the page: "UAE licensing does not ask every applicant for an English test. The Unified PQR accepts OET grade C or above as proof of English in specific exemption cases." Employers can still set their own requirement.

## 4. Licensing route (facts only)

Common to DHA, DOH and MOHAP (Unified PQR, April 2025)
- Documents are verified at source (PQR PDF, clause 1.4.1; "shall be verified directly from the original or primary source")
- An authority examination is required unless exempt; UAE nationals and children of Emirati women are exempt (PQR PDF, clause 1.5.1; "required to pass the Authority's examination to obtain a professional license in the UAE")
- Three attempts across the authorities, with one final attempt at a different authority (PQR PDF, clause 1.5.4; "three (3) attempts to pass the examination across the Authorities")
- Exam exemption covers the exam only (PQR PDF, clause 1.6.2; "It does not apply to any other requirements for licensure")
- A practice gap of more than three years triggers CME/CPD requirements (PQR PDF, clause 1.7.1; "discontinuity in clinical practice exceeding three (3) years")
- The PQR itself does not name DataFlow, Prometric, Pearson VUE, Sheryan or TAMM (full text searched: no match).

Dubai: DHA
- Portal: Sheryan (DHA licensing manual v1.2; "Sheryan: is the DHA digital platform/ e-licensing system")
- Steps: Sheryan account and self-assessment, meet the PQR, positive PSV, pass the assessment, registration certificate (DHA licensing manual v1.2, clause 5.6; "Create a Sheryan account and do self-assessment")
- Verification: DataFlow, and it can run in parallel with the exam (Sheryan Get Registered service page, URL in section 3; "you can perform your Dataflow verification and CBT Assessment in parallel")
- Exam: computer-based test delivered by Prometric, plus an oral assessment for some titles (Sheryan Get Registered service page; "CBT Assessment (If Required) By Prometric")
- The registration must then be activated as a licence by a facility (Sheryan Get Registered service page; "This service allows applicants to obtain a DHA registration")
- DHA accepts PSV reports transferred from the other UAE authorities (DHA licensing manual v1.2, clause 6.3; "DHA accepts transferred PSV reports from other regulatory authorities in UAE")
- Failing the third attempt blocks a new DHA application for two years (DHA licensing manual v1.2, clause 7.3; "blocking the applicant from reapplying for DHA license for two years")
- Credentialing fee shown: AED 200 (Sheryan Get Registered service page; "Credentialing Fees AED 200")

Abu Dhabi: DOH
- Portal: TAMM, service "Issue a Healthcare Professional Licence" (https://www.tamm.abudhabi/en/life-events/business/healthcare/healthcare-professionals/requestregistrationofnewlicenceforahealthcareprofessional; "apply for a new healthcare licence issued by the Department of Health")
- Steps on TAMM: log in with UAE PASS, submit the application and documents, pay the fees (same TAMM page; "Log in using UAE PASS.")
- Documents listed: passport, personal photo, academic qualifications (same TAMM page; "The following documents must be submitted during the application")
- Fees shown on TAMM: three amounts of 100.00, 100.00 and 50.00 AED (application fee and written-exam fees for physicians, dentists and pharmacists, and for other categories); the page layout does not make clear which amount belongs to which label, so do not quote them individually. Duration shown: 3 months.

Federal (other emirates): MOHAP
- MOHAP is a co-issuer of the Unified PQR, so the common rules above apply (PQR cover; "Ministry of Health and Prevention")
- MOHAP's own service pages could not be opened today (see "Could not verify").

Already on site (gulf-pathway-guide): "UAE Federal MOHAP / Dubai DHA / Abu Dhabi DOH: Unified PQR: the authority licensing exam (unless exempt) plus primary source verification."

## 5. Why candidates in the UAE still take OET (regulator facts)

- Verified today: OET grade C or above is an accepted English proof inside the UAE's own rules for some experience and exam exemptions (section 3).
All of the following are "already on site" (oet-score-requirements page, verified 2 Nov 2026), not re-fetched today:
- UK nurses and midwives (NMC): OET Nursing 350 in Listening, Reading and Speaking, 300 in Writing; 1 sitting or 2 within 12 months; OET@Home accepted; valid 2 years.
- UK doctors (GMC): OET Medicine 350 (grade B) in all four sub-tests in one sitting, the most recent test; OET@Home accepted; valid 2 years.
- UK pharmacists (GPhC): 350 in all four, one sitting, OET@Home not accepted.
- Ireland nurses (NMBI): 350 / 350 / 300 Writing / 350, one sitting, no combining, OET@Home not accepted.
- Ireland doctors (Medical Council of Ireland): 350 in each module in a single sitting (applications from 1 March 2025), OET@Home not accepted.
- Australia (Ahpra, tests from 23 April 2026): 350 Listening, 360 Reading, 350 Writing, 360 Speaking; 1 sitting or 2 within 12 months; OET@Home not accepted.
- New Zealand: Nursing Council 350 / 350 / 300 / 350; Medical Council 350 / 350 / 300 / 350.
- USA: ECFMG 2027 Pathways 350 / 350 / 300 Writing / 350 in one test taken on or after 1 January 2025; OET@Home only if there is no venue in the candidate's country (the UAE has venues, see section 1).
- Gap between the two targets: the UAE PQR asks for grade C where it asks at all, while the regulators above mostly ask for grade B (350), so a UAE-level result is not enough for them.

## 6. Time zone

- The UAE is UTC+4 all year, with no daylight saving rule (IANA Time Zone Database, release 2026f, file "asia", https://data.iana.org/time-zones/data/asia; "Zone Asia/Dubai 3:41:12 - LMT 1920" followed by "4:00 - %z")
- OET computer and OET@Home bookings close at 11:59 pm UTC, which is 3:59 am the next day in the UAE (calculated from the line above and https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "closes 7 days before the test date (11:59PM UTC time)")
