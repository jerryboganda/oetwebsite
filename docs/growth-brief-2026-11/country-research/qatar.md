# Qatar: verified facts for the OET preparation landing page

Research date: 19 Nov 2026. Every fact below was read on the stated primary source on that date unless it is marked "already on site" (taken from `site/src/partials/gulf-pathway-guide/main.html`, verified by the site on 2 Nov 2026) or "already on site, not re-fetched". Quotes are verbatim and under 15 words. Format: fact (source URL; "quote").

## 1. OET test venues in Qatar

- The oet.com venue finder returned exactly one venue for a "Doha, Qatar" search: Test Orbits (Prometric Test Center). (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test?location=Doha%2C%20Qatar&latLng=25.2854%2C51.5310; "1 results shown")
- The venue is labelled OET Test on Computer only. No OET on Paper venue appeared for Qatar. (same URL; "OET Test on Computer")
- Venue address as shown by oet.com. (same URL, "View venue details" panel; "Al Emadi Business Center, C Ring Road, 1st Floor, Doha, Qatar")
- oet.com describes the venue as an authorised Prometric centre. (same panel; "an authorized Prometric Test Center, primarily serving Qatar")
- Qatar is on OET's list of countries where OET on Computer candidates can take the Speaking sub-test at the test venue (post published 20 Jul 2026). (https://oet.com/en-us/post/speaking-sub-test-available-for-select-countries; "will be able to take Speaking sub-test at test venues")
- Otherwise Speaking for OET on Computer is done from home. (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; "Conducted at home via a secure platform or for select countries, conducted at venue.")
- OET@Home: oet.com does not list countries. Its rule is that OET@Home is for countries without a venue, and the finder showed no OET@Home option for Doha. (https://oet.com/en-us/test/oet-test-at-home; "offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available")
- Booking deadlines. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "closes 7 days before the test date (11:59PM UTC time)") and, for paper, (same URL; "close 24 days before the test date (subject to change)")
- Method note: the venue finder is JavaScript-only (it is not in the page HTML and a plain fetch cannot read it). It was read in a real browser. The search has no default distance limit (the page script sets the radius to "all" within the searched country), so one result means one listed venue in Qatar on 19 Nov 2026.
- Local detail worth using: DHP gives the same building as the Prometric centre for the Qatar qualifying exam. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/QualifyingExamination.aspx; "Al Emadi Business Center, C Ring Road")

## 2. OET test fee and payment

- Full test fee. (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; "AUD $587") and (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "AU$587")
- The same fee applies to paper, computer at a venue and OET@Home outside the USA. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test/third-party-payments; "Rest of the world" row, "AU$587")
- Single sub-test resits exist only for OET on Paper after a full test, so they are not bookable at a computer-only venue: 1 sub-test AU$315, 2 AU$415, 3 AU$515. (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "if you've previously taken the full OET Test")
- oet.com says nothing specific about Qatar: no local-currency (QAR) price, no tax statement and no Qatar payment method was found on the fee pages fetched.

## 3. Does the Qatar regulator (DHP, Ministry of Public Health) require an English test?

- No English test requirement was found in DHP's own documents. The Guidelines for Nurses, Guidelines for Physicians and Guidelines for Pharmacists (PDFs downloaded and text-searched today for English, IELTS, OET, TOEFL, language, proficiency) contain no English test requirement. The only language rule is about document translation. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Documents/Guidelines%20for%20Nurses.pdf; "languages other than Arabic or English must be translated")
- Same in the other two guidelines. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Documents/Guidelines%20for%20Physicians.pdf and https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Documents/Guidelines%20for%20Pharmacists.pdf; "must be translated to Arabic or English")
- The DHP Qualifying Examination page, Primary Source Verification page and FAQ page do not mention IELTS, OET or TOEFL. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/QualifyingExamination.aspx; https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/PrimarysourceVerification.aspx; https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/FAQs.aspx; no quote, absence checked)
- Already on site: "The DHP guidelines we checked do not set an OET score", and employers can set their own English requirement, so candidates should get it in writing. Today's check agrees. Safe wording: "DHP's published guidelines do not ask for an English test; your employer may."

## 4. Licensing route (facts from DHP)

- Applications go through DHP's online system. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Documents/Guidelines%20for%20Nurses.pdf; "must apply through the registration/licensing electronic system") The FAQ names it. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/FAQs.aspx; "Electronic Registration & Licensing System")
- Primary source verification is mandatory. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/PrimarysourceVerification.aspx; "one of the mandatory requirements for registration and licensing")
- DHP names three verification providers on that page: Dataflow, Quadrabay and Approved Verification Services (AVS). The site currently says "a DHP-approved provider such as DataFlow", which is consistent. (same URL; provider names "Dataflow", "Quadrabay")
- The verification report is attached to the evaluation and reviewed at licensing. (Guidelines for Nurses PDF; "Copy of primary source verification report.")
- Preliminary evaluation validity. (Guidelines for Nurses PDF; "Preliminary evaluation is valid only for 6 months.") Already on site.
- Qualifying exam: computer-based, Prometric. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/QualifyingExamination.aspx; "provides computer based examination (Prometric)") Already on site.
- Format: every exam on the list is 150 MCQs in 3 hours. Pass marks on the page: 60 percent General Practitioner, General Dentist, Pharmacist; 50 percent Registered General Nurse, Registered Midwife and most allied health; 65 percent most specialist exams. (same URL; "150 MCQs") Already on site.
- Attempts. (same URL; "may attempt the qualifying exam an unlimited number of times") Already on site.
- Booking. (same URL; "To register and schedule your exams please visit the online booking page", link prometric.com/exams/schq2)
- NEW, not on site: DHP's FAQ gives a validity for the exam result. (https://dhp.moph.gov.qa/en/Pages/FAQs.aspx, FAQ 51; "For 3 years.") The site currently says to confirm validity with DHP; this does not contradict it but could replace it after a second look at the FAQ wording.
- Temporary licence. (Guidelines for Nurses PDF; "valid for a maximum period of 6 months (non-renewable)") Already on site.
- Licensing documents include a Qatari police clearance and a medical report. (Guidelines for Nurses PDF; "Police Clearance Certificate from Qatari Ministry of Interior") Already on site.
- Exemptions: already on site, Circular DHP/2025/24 (30 Dec 2025) limits exam exemptions to Qataris, offspring of Qatari women and graduates of institutions in Qatar. Not re-read today. Note: the Qualifying Examination page today links a circular file named "DHP 19 2025 E.pdf" for exempting certificates; that file was not opened.

## 5. Why candidates living in Qatar still take OET (regulator facts only)

Qatar's regulator does not ask for it, so the demand is for registration abroad or for an employer. Scores below are already on site (`site/src/partials/oet-score-requirements/main.html`); those re-fetched today are marked.

- UK nurses and midwives (NMC), re-fetched today: B in Listening, Reading, Speaking and C+ in Writing; paper, computer and OET@Home accepted. (https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/oet/; "At least grade C+ (300 or above) for writing") and (same URL; "We accept the OET on Paper, OET on Computer, and OET@Home test.")
- UK doctors (GMC): OET Medicine, at least B in all four sub-tests in one sitting, valid 2 years. Already on site, not re-fetched (the GMC page returned HTTP 403 today). (https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/before-you-apply-guide-for-doctors/evidence-of-your-knowledge-of-english/using-your-oet-certificate)
- Australia (Ahpra, all National Boards), re-fetched today: for tests from 23 April 2026, Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360; at-home tests not accepted. (https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests; "a maximum of two test sittings within a 12-month period") and (same URL; "are not approved by National Boards and are not accepted")
- Ireland nurses (NMBI), re-fetched today. (https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/English-Language-Requirements; "Grade B in three components and C+ in one component") and (same URL; "We do not accept IELTS online or OET at home.")
- Ireland doctors (Medical Council), re-fetched today. (https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/english-language.html; "attained a minimum grade B in each module") and (same URL; "We do not accept OET @ Home online tests.")
- Practical consequence for Qatar: the only listed venue is OET on Computer at a test centre, which Ahpra, NMBI and the Medical Council of Ireland accept (it is OET@Home they refuse). The Speaking mode chosen must also be acceptable. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "ensure the Speaking mode you select is accepted by the organisation")

## 6. UTC offset

- Qatar is UTC+3 all year, no daylight saving. (IANA Time Zone Database, release 2026f, https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/asia; zone entry "Asia/Qatar", standard offset "3:00", no daylight-saving rule since 1972)
