# Oman: verified facts for the OET preparation landing page

Research date: 19 Nov 2026. Every fact below was read on the stated primary source on that date unless it is marked "already on site" (taken from `site/src/partials/gulf-pathway-guide/main.html`, verified by the site on 2 Nov 2026) or "already on site, not re-fetched". Quotes are verbatim and under 15 words. Format: fact (source URL; "quote").

## 1. OET test venues in Oman

- The oet.com venue finder returned exactly one venue for a "Muscat, Oman" search, listed under the name "Muscat , OMAN". (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test?location=Muscat%2C%20Oman&latLng=23.5880%2C58.3829; "1 results shown")
- The venue is labelled OET Test on Computer only. No OET on Paper venue appeared for Oman. (same URL; "OET Test on Computer")
- Venue address as shown by oet.com: National Education Network, 405 West Wing, Al Watan Newspaper Building, 4th Floor, Al-Azaiba North, Muscat. (same URL, "View venue details" panel; "National Education Network, 405 West Wing, Al Watan Newspaper Building, 4th Floor")
- Oman is NOT on OET's published list of countries where the Speaking sub-test can be taken at the venue (the list names Kuwait, Qatar and Saudi Arabia among Gulf countries). So, on the published information, Oman candidates on OET on Computer take Speaking from home. (https://oet.com/en-us/post/speaking-sub-test-available-for-select-countries, published 20 Jul 2026; "will be able to take Speaking sub-test at test venues") and (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; "Conducted at home via a secure platform or for select countries, conducted at venue.")
- OET@Home: oet.com does not list countries. Its rule is that OET@Home is for countries without a venue, and the finder showed no OET@Home option for Muscat. (https://oet.com/en-us/test/oet-test-at-home; "offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available")
- Booking deadline for computer tests. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "closes 7 days before the test date (11:59PM UTC time)")
- Method note: the venue finder is JavaScript-only (not in the page HTML; a plain fetch cannot read it). It was read in a real browser. The search has no default distance limit (the page script sets the radius to "all" within the searched country), so one result means one listed venue in Oman on 19 Nov 2026. Other cities (Salalah, Sohar) were not searched separately.

## 2. OET test fee and payment

- Full test fee. (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; "AUD $587") and (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "AU$587")
- The same fee applies to paper, computer at a venue and OET@Home outside the USA. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test/third-party-payments; "Rest of the world" row, "AU$587")
- Single sub-test resits exist only for OET on Paper after a full test, so they are not bookable at a computer-only venue. (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "if you've previously taken the full OET Test")
- oet.com says nothing specific about Oman: no local-currency (OMR) price, no tax statement and no Oman payment method was found on the fee pages fetched.

## 3. Do the Oman authorities (OMSB, Ministry of Health) require an English test for licensing?

- No English test requirement for licensing was found on any Oman official page fetched today. The OMSB Occupational Classification examinations page does not mention IELTS or OET. (https://omsb.gov.om/home/infoPage?id=53; no quote, absence checked)
- The OMSB verification and equivalency page does not mention English tests. (https://omsb.gov.om/home/infoPage?id=50; no quote, absence checked)
- The national e-government service "Obtain a Medical Staff License" (Ministry of Health, updated 2 July 2026) lists four required documents and no English test. (https://gov.om/en/w/obtain-a-medical-staff-license; "Dataflow certificate Identity card Insurance policy Course certificates")
- The Ministry of Health's private-sector nursing licensing procedure (MoH/DGPHE/SOP/009, effective Dec 2021) lists the required documents and no English test. (https://moh.gov.om/media/tklpor0j/licensing-and-assessing-nursing-categories.pdf; PDF text searched, no IELTS/OET line)
- The only OMSB mention of IELTS/OET found is for its own residents applying to INTERNATIONAL training programs, not for licensing in Oman. (https://www.omsb.gov.om/ContentFiles/FAQ's%20-%20Updated%2011%20September%202025YYYY10DD0910SS.pdf; "some universities do require that you have IELTS/OET at the time of application")
- Safe wording: "The Oman official pages we checked do not ask for an English test for a licence; an employer may set its own requirement." Do not write "Oman does not require English" as an absolute; no page states that in terms.

## 4. Licensing route (facts from OMSB, MOH and gov.om)

- Verification is done through DataFlow. (https://omsb.gov.om/home/infoPage?id=50; "This process is done Online through Dataflow Group")
- It is mandatory for foreign qualifications. (same URL; "mandatory for Omanis and non-Omanis who obtained qualifications from outside The Sultanate")
- Verification comes before evaluation. (same URL; "should be verified before evaluation")
- Report outcomes. (same URL; "Positive, Negative or Unable to verify result")
- Equivalency applications are filed by the employer through OMSB's e-Equivalency System (portal.omsb.gov.om), not by the candidate. (same URL; summarised, not quoted)
- Licensing exam: OMSB Occupational Classification (OC) examinations, computer-delivered, 19 health exams. Already on site. (https://omsb.gov.om/home/infoPage?id=53; "the candidates must sit the OMSB OC examinations")
- Who uses the result. Already on site. (same URL; "The Ministry of Health and other governmental and private institutions use OMSB Occupational Classification")
- Booking is through Pearson VUE. Already on site. (same URL; "To register for the OC examinations, please visit pearsonvue.com/omsb")
- Pearson VUE's own OMSB page confirms the exam name and that bookings are confirmed by email. (https://www.pearsonvue.com/us/en/omsb.html; "You will receive a confirmation of your booking by email within 48 hours.")
- Licence service (Ministry of Health, via gov.om, updated 2 July 2026): three steps, licence fee 100 for non-Omani doctors and 20 for Omani doctors as displayed (currency not shown in the extracted text; Omani rial assumed, not verified), service time shown as 7 days. (https://gov.om/en/w/obtain-a-medical-staff-license; "Submitting the request", "Reviewing the request", "Issuing the license")
- Special conditions on that service page. (same URL; "Having a medical malpractice insurance") and (same URL; "Having valid CPR courses certificates")
- Re-test rule on that service page (the page says "prometric"). (same URL; "must retake the prometric test")
- Private sector (Directorate General of Private Health Establishments), nursing, per the Dec 2021 SOP: documents include DataFlow and a Prometric pass. (https://moh.gov.om/media/tklpor0j/licensing-and-assessing-nursing-categories.pdf; "Prometric (pass grade, no less than 50%)")
- Same SOP, experience. (same URL; "diploma minimum 4 years and bachelor minimum 3 years")
- Same SOP, exam attempts. (same URL; "they are allowed 3 other attempts")
- Private sector, doctors, per SOP MoH/DGPHE/SOP/007 (effective Dec 2021, review date Dec 2025): GP applicants submit documents including DataFlow, then sit an MoH viva exam. (https://moh.gov.om/media/whinfxgy/licensing-and-assessing-medical-categories-doctors-license.pdf; "4 years work experience (as GP or in internal medicine") and (same URL; "or the exam can be taken online")
- Caution for the page: official sources use two exam-provider names. OMSB says its OC exams are booked on Pearson VUE; the MOH private-sector SOPs (2021) and the gov.om service page say "Prometric". Do not state one provider for all routes; tell candidates to confirm with their employer or the MOH directorate which exam applies.

## 5. Why candidates living in Oman still take OET (regulator facts only)

No Oman licensing page asks for it, so the demand is for registration or training abroad, or an employer's own rule. Scores below are already on site (`site/src/partials/oet-score-requirements/main.html`); those re-fetched today are marked.

- UK nurses and midwives (NMC), re-fetched today: B in Listening, Reading, Speaking and C+ in Writing; paper, computer and OET@Home accepted. (https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/oet/; "At least grade C+ (300 or above) for writing") and (same URL; "We accept the OET on Paper, OET on Computer, and OET@Home test.")
- UK doctors (GMC): OET Medicine, at least B in all four sub-tests in one sitting, valid 2 years. Already on site, not re-fetched (the GMC page returned HTTP 403 today). (https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/before-you-apply-guide-for-doctors/evidence-of-your-knowledge-of-english/using-your-oet-certificate)
- Australia (Ahpra, all National Boards), re-fetched today: for tests from 23 April 2026, Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360; at-home tests not accepted. (https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests; "are not approved by National Boards and are not accepted")
- Ireland nurses (NMBI), re-fetched today. (https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/English-Language-Requirements; "Grade B in three components and C+ in one component") and (same URL; "We do not accept IELTS online or OET at home.")
- Ireland doctors (Medical Council), re-fetched today. (https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/english-language.html; "attained a minimum grade B in each module") and (same URL; "We do not accept OET @ Home online tests.")
- Oman-specific reason, from OMSB itself: OMSB residents applying to international programs may be asked for IELTS or OET by the receiving university. (https://www.omsb.gov.om/ContentFiles/FAQ's%20-%20Updated%2011%20September%202025YYYY10DD0910SS.pdf; "you will be exempt from providing IELTS/OET" applies only to degrees from English-native countries)
- Ahpra says the computer test's remotely delivered Speaking element is acceptable, which matters in Oman where Speaking is not offered at the venue (already on site: "the computer test's remotely delivered Speaking component has been assessed as acceptable").

## 6. UTC offset

- Oman is UTC+4 all year, no daylight saving. (IANA Time Zone Database, release 2026f, https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/asia and https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/backward; "Asia/Muscat" is a link to "Asia/Dubai", standard offset "4:00", no daylight-saving rule; zone1970.tab lists "AE,OM" for Asia/Dubai)
