# Egypt: verified facts for the "OET preparation course for candidates in Egypt" page

Researched 19 Nov 2026. Every fact below was read on the cited primary source on that date.
Quotes are verbatim and under 15 words. "Already on site" = the fact is also published on
`oet-score-requirements` / `oet-exam-fees`; unless noted it was re-read on the source today.

Method note: the OET venue finder (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test) is JavaScript-only. A plain
fetch returns no venues; the results below were read in a real browser by searching
"Cairo, Cairo Governorate, Egypt" (7 results, default distance filter). Venue lists change, so
re-check before publishing and link to the finder instead of promising a venue.

## 1. OET test venues in Egypt

- The finder returned 7 results for Cairo: 5 OET Test on Paper, 2 OET Test on Computer (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test, search "Cairo, Egypt"; filter labels "OET Test on Computer (2)" and "OET Test on Paper (5)")
- Cairo / Giza (Dokki), paper: "AMIDEAST - Cairo Egypt (8708) - Online Speaking test at venue", address "AMIDEAST, 38 Mohie El Din Abo El Ezz Street, Dokki, Giza, Cairo, Egypt" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- Cairo / Giza (Dokki), computer: "AMIDEAST - Cairo", same Dokki address, mode "OET Test on Computer" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- New Cairo, paper: "Future University (FUE)", address "90th Street, Fifth Settlement New Cairo, Cairo 11835" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- Giza (Smart Village), paper: "IEC - AASTMT - Smart Village" (Arab Academy for Science and Technology, Building B2401), address "28th Km- Cairo-Alexandria Desert Road- Smart Village" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- Alexandria (Miami), paper: "IEC - AASTMT", address "Miami Campus, Gamal Abdel Nasser Street, Main Building, Ground Floor, Miami Alexandria Egypt" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- Alexandria (Azarita), paper: "American Center - Alexandria Egypt (8710) - Online Speaking test at venue", address "American Center, 3 Pharaana St., Azarita, Alexandria 21111, Egypt" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- Alexandria (Azarita), computer: "American Center", same Azarita address, mode "OET Test on Computer" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel)
- Summary: paper in Cairo/Giza (Dokki), New Cairo, Smart Village and Alexandria; computer at a venue in Cairo/Giza (Dokki) and Alexandria (Azarita) only. All seven were within about 181 km of central Cairo.
- The FUE and both AASTMT venues list test-day registration "Open: 7:00 AM Close: 8:30 AM" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; venue details panel). The AMIDEAST and American Center paper listings say "For more information, visit Prometric" (same source).
- Booking deadlines: "Bookings for OET Test on Paper close 24 days before the test date"; computer at a venue and OET@Home "closes 7 days before the test date" (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test)
- OET on Computer Speaking: "Conducted at home via a secure platform or for select countries, conducted at venue." (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer). oet.com does not name the "select countries"; see Could not verify.
- OET@Home in Egypt: oet.com publishes no country list. Its rule is that OET@Home is offered "in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home), and the regulations give the eligibility test "Sitting the test where there is no Venue available" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, section 6.2). Egypt has venues, and the Cairo search offered no OET@Home option (the test mode filter listed only Computer and Paper). Inference, not an oet.com statement: OET@Home is not normally offered to candidates in Egypt.
- OET warns: "OET@Home may not be accepted by all recognising organisations or authorities in every country." (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-at-home)
- Availability caveat: "not all OET Test delivery dates and testing modes are available in all countries" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, section 4.1)

## 2. Test fee and paying from Egypt

- Full test fee: the full OET Test "costs AU$587" (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost). Already on site.
- Same fee on the computer page: "AUD $587" (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer)
- The only published country exception is the USA ("US$455" for computer at a venue and OET@Home); the article has no Egypt-specific price (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost). Already on site.
- Paper sub-test re-sits after a full test: "1 sub-test: AU$315", "2 sub-tests: AU$415", "3 sub-tests: AU$515", including "the AU$7 online booking fee" (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost). Already on site.
- Payment timing: "Booking fees for the OET Test are paid at the time of booking." (https://oet.com/test-regulations, section 4.4)
- Refunds go "back into the card used for making the initial payment" (https://oet.com/test-regulations, section 9.2), which implies card payment; no other payment method is named.
- Nothing on the oet.com pages read today (fee article, regulations, book-a-test, computer page) mentions Egypt, Egyptian pounds, VAT/GST or local taxes. See Could not verify.
- The site's own Egyptian pound figure (21,730 on 7 Nov 2026) is our conversion, not an OET price; refresh it at publication. Already on site, not re-verified today.

## 3. Destinations: what each regulator requires

- GMC (UK doctors): OET Medicine, "at least a grade 'B' in each testing area", "in the same test", most recent sitting; paper, computer and OET@Home accepted; "OET certificates are valid for two years." (https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/before-you-apply-guide-for-doctors/evidence-of-your-knowledge-of-english/using-your-oet-certificate). Already on site.
- NMC (UK nurses, midwives): Reading, Listening, Speaking "At least grade B (350 or above)"; Writing "At least grade C+ (300 or above)"; two sittings can be combined; "We accept the OET on Paper, OET on Computer, and OET@Home test." (https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/oet/). Already on site.
- GPhC (UK pharmacists): Pharmacy OET on paper or computer, "at least a B in each of the four areas", "at one sitting of the test"; "We do not accept the OET@Home Test."; results older than two years only with evidence of maintained English (https://assets.pharmacyregulation.org/files/2025-06/gphc-guidance-on-evidence-of-english-language-skills-june-2025_0.pdf, guidance dated June 2025). Already on site.
- Medical Council of Ireland (doctors): OET Medicine, "a minimum grade B in each module", "in a single sitting within the last two years"; "We do not accept OET @ Home online tests." (https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/english-language.html). Already on site.
- NMBI (Irish nurses, midwives): "OET (Nursing) with Grade B in three components and C+ in one component" (C+ is Writing); "We only accept one test result and do not accept combined results."; "We do not accept IELTS online or OET at home."; results under two years old (https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/English-Language-Requirements). Already on site.
- Ahpra (Australia, all National Boards, tests on or after 23 April 2026): Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360; one sitting, or two within 12 months with "all four components in each sitting" and floors of 320 / 340 / 350 / 350; at-home versions are not approved (https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests). Already on site.
- ECFMG (USA, 2027 Pathways): OET Medicine, "minimum score of 350 on the Listening, Reading, and Speaking sub-tests" and 300 in Writing, "in one test administration", taken "on or after January 1, 2025" (https://www.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/oet.html). Already on site.
- ECFMG and Egypt specifically: OET@Home is "available only to applicants who do not have a physical test venue" in their country (https://www.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/oet.html). Egypt has venues (section 1), so Egyptian ECFMG applicants should plan on a venue sitting.
- ECFMG timing for the 2027 Match: take OET Medicine "on or before the last scheduled test date in December 2026" (https://www.ecfmg.org/certification-pathways/oet.html)
- Practical consequence for Egypt-based candidates (our synthesis): every regulator above accepts a paper or computer sitting at a venue, and Egypt has both modes, so no candidate in Egypt needs OET@Home.

## 5. Egypt time zone

- Zone Africa/Cairo, standard time UTC+2 (EET): zone line "2:00 Egypt EE%sT" (https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/africa, IANA Time Zone Database)
- Daylight saving time (UTC+3, EEST) has applied since 2023 from the last Friday of April to the last Thursday of October: "Rule Egypt 2023 max - Apr lastFri 0:00 1:00 S" and "Rule Egypt 2023 max - Oct lastThu 24:00 0 -" (https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/africa)
- So on 19 Nov 2026 Egypt is on UTC+2, and from the last Friday of April 2027 it moves to UTC+3 (derived from the rules above).
- Scheduling note (derived; the UK rules were not fetched today): UK and Egyptian clock changes fall on different dates, so state session times in Cairo time and convert per date instead of quoting a fixed difference from UK time.
