# Kuwait: verified facts for the OET preparation landing page

Research date: 19 Nov 2026. Every fact below was read on the stated primary source on that date unless it is marked "already on site". Quotes are verbatim and under 15 words. Format: fact (source URL; "quote").

IMPORTANT: no official Kuwait government source could be opened today (details in section 3). Sections 1, 2, 5 and 6 are verified. Sections 3 and 4 are NOT verified and must not be published as facts.

## 1. OET test venues in Kuwait

- The oet.com venue finder returned exactly one venue for a "Kuwait City, Kuwait" search: AMIDEAST - Kuwait. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test?location=Kuwait%20City%2C%20Kuwait&latLng=29.3759%2C47.9774; "1 results shown")
- The venue is labelled OET Test on Computer only. No OET on Paper venue appeared for Kuwait. (same URL; "OET Test on Computer")
- Venue address as shown by oet.com: Amideast, Dalal Complex, 8th floor, Salmiya (about 10 km from the Kuwait City search point). (same URL, "View venue details" panel; "Amideast, Dalal Complex 8Th Floor,Al Dimna St., 7 Ln, Salmiya")
- The panel adds an access note. (same panel; "Entrance Through Dalal City Hotel")
- Kuwait is on OET's list of countries where OET on Computer candidates can take the Speaking sub-test at the test venue (post published 20 Jul 2026). (https://oet.com/en-us/post/speaking-sub-test-available-for-select-countries; "will be able to take Speaking sub-test at test venues")
- Otherwise Speaking for OET on Computer is done from home. (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; "Conducted at home via a secure platform or for select countries, conducted at venue.")
- OET@Home: oet.com does not list countries. Its rule is that OET@Home is for countries without a venue, and the finder showed no OET@Home option for Kuwait. (https://oet.com/en-us/test/oet-test-at-home; "offered in countries where taking the test at a venue is not yet available")
- Booking deadline for computer tests. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "closes 7 days before the test date (11:59PM UTC time)")
- Method note: the venue finder is JavaScript-only (not in the page HTML; a plain fetch cannot read it). It was read in a real browser. The search has no default distance limit (the page script sets the radius to "all" within the searched country), so one result means one listed venue in Kuwait on 19 Nov 2026.

## 2. OET test fee and payment

- Full test fee. (https://oet.com/test/oet-test-on-computer; "AUD $587") and (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "AU$587")
- The same fee applies to paper, computer at a venue and OET@Home outside the USA. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test/third-party-payments; "Rest of the world" row, "AU$587")
- Single sub-test resits exist only for OET on Paper after a full test, so they are not bookable at a computer-only venue. (https://support.oet.com/articles/Knowledge/How-much-does-OET-cost; "if you've previously taken the full OET Test")
- oet.com says nothing specific about Kuwait: no local-currency (KWD) price, no tax statement and no Kuwait payment method was found on the fee pages fetched.

## 5. Why candidates living in Kuwait still take OET (regulator facts only)

These are facts about overseas regulators, so they hold wherever the candidate lives. Scores are already on site (`site/src/partials/oet-score-requirements/main.html`); those re-fetched today are marked.

- UK nurses and midwives (NMC), re-fetched today: B in Listening, Reading, Speaking and C+ in Writing; paper, computer and OET@Home accepted. (https://www.nmc.org.uk/registration/joining-the-register/english-language-requirements/accepted-english-language-tests/oet/; "At least grade C+ (300 or above) for writing") and (same URL; "We accept the OET on Paper, OET on Computer, and OET@Home test.")
- UK doctors (GMC): OET Medicine, at least B in all four sub-tests in one sitting, valid 2 years. Already on site, not re-fetched (the GMC page returned HTTP 403 today). (https://www.gmc-uk.org/registration-and-licensing/join-our-registers/before-you-apply-guide-for-doctors/evidence-of-your-knowledge-of-english/using-your-oet-certificate)
- Australia (Ahpra, all National Boards), re-fetched today: for tests from 23 April 2026, Listening 350, Reading 360, Writing 350, Speaking 360; at-home tests not accepted. (https://www.ahpra.gov.au/Registration/Registration-Standards/English-language-skills/Accepted-English-language-tests; "are not approved by National Boards and are not accepted")
- Ireland nurses (NMBI), re-fetched today. (https://www.nmbi.ie/Registration/Qualified-outside-the-EU/Application-Process/English-Language-Requirements; "Grade B in three components and C+ in one component") and (same URL; "We do not accept IELTS online or OET at home.")
- Ireland doctors (Medical Council), re-fetched today. (https://www.medicalcouncil.ie/registration-applications/first-time-applicants/english-language.html; "attained a minimum grade B in each module") and (same URL; "We do not accept OET @ Home online tests.")
- Practical consequence for Kuwait: the only listed venue is OET on Computer at a test centre, which Ahpra, NMBI and the Medical Council of Ireland accept (it is OET@Home they refuse). The Speaking mode chosen must also be acceptable. (https://oet.com/test/book-a-test; "ensure the Speaking mode you select is accepted by the organisation")

## 6. UTC offset

- Kuwait is UTC+3 all year, no daylight saving. (IANA Time Zone Database, release 2026f, https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/asia and https://data.iana.org/time-zones/tzdb/backward; "Asia/Kuwait" is a link to "Asia/Riyadh", standard offset "3:00", no daylight-saving rule; zone1970.tab lists "SA,AQ,KW,YE" for Asia/Riyadh)
