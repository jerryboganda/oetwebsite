# Weekly update and monthly dashboard (brief deliverable G)

## Weekly update (one page)

Week ending: 

1. **Shipped** (each with its live URL or screenshot, and the `task-board.csv` id)
2. **What changed in the numbers** (Search Console clicks and impressions vs the previous week; registrations; purchases)
3. **Evidence** (links)
4. **Blockers** (what is waiting, and on whom)
5. **Next week** (three items at most)

A task counts as done only when it is live, crawlable or publicly visible, correctly linked, checked against current facts and recorded in `task-board.csv`.
