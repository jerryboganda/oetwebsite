How we teach

Teaching is led by a doctor. Dr Ahmed Hesham records the lessons, and he personally assesses the Writing letters in every package that includes personal Writing assessments: you receive an estimated score, a detailed correction and voice-note feedback. Private Speaking sessions are live and one to one with an expert tutor.

Courses are specific to your profession: Medicine, Nursing, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy and Modified Allied Health each have their own course. Website practice follows the computer-based test for Listening and Reading, and includes Writing assessments and live virtual patient Speaking role-plays. The 2026 TutorBook adds 10 full recall-based exams, and SAMI is the study assistant available at any hour.

Read more on our teaching approach, OET Writing correction, OET Speaking practice, OET mock tests and the OET practice hub.

Questions people ask about us

What is the official website of OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham?

The official website is https://oetwithdrhesham.co.uk and the official learner app is https://app.oetwithdrhesham.co.uk. Addresses that begin with www redirect to the same website.

What is the official WhatsApp number and email?

The official WhatsApp number is +44 7961 725989 (https://wa.me/447961725989) and the support email is support@oetwithdrhesham.co.uk. Messages from any other number or address are not from our team.

Is there an official OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham app?

Yes. The app is called OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham. It is on the Apple App Store for iPhone and iPad and on Google Play for Android, and the same account works in a browser at app.oetwithdrhesham.co.uk.

Who is Dr Ahmed Hesham?

Dr Ahmed Hesham is a medical doctor and OET educator who has coached OET candidates since 2018. He is the founder and lead teacher, and he personally assesses the Writing letters in the packages that include personal Writing assessments.

Is OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham affiliated with OET?

No. We are an independent preparation provider and are not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to OET or Cambridge Boxhill Language Assessment. Official information about the test is published on oet.com.

Are the published student results genuine?

The Success Stories page shows score cards that students sent us after receiving their official OET results, from 2021 to 2026. The scores are not edited, and each card can be opened to see the original file. They are those students' own results, and no score is guaranteed.

Which course is for nurses?

Nursing has two packages. The Classic Nursing OET Course is the recorded course. The Nursing Premium Bundle is the recommended option and adds 5 personal Writing assessments, 1 private Speaking session, the 2026 TutorBook and the Basic English Course. Current prices are on the pricing page.

What is included in the Full OET Course?

The Full OET Course + Website Practice includes the full recorded course, 5 personal Writing letters assessed by Dr Ahmed Hesham, 1 private Speaking session with an expert tutor, website practice and the SAMI 24/7 study assistant, with 6 months of access. The Complete OET Bundle adds the 2026 TutorBook, and the Ultimate OET Course adds unlimited eligible website practice, the Basic English Course and 12 months of access. These tiers are sold for Medicine, Pharmacy, Physiotherapy and Modified Allied Health.

How does live virtual patient Speaking practice work?

You choose a Speaking card on the website and hold a live, two-way role-play with a responsive virtual patient. Afterwards you receive a transcript and detailed OET Speaking feedback. It is automated website practice, and it is separate from the private one-to-one Speaking sessions with a tutor.

What does recall-based practice mean?

The TutorBook contains 10 full recall-based exams: practice exams built from what candidates recalled after recent tests, with model answers and answer rationales, and it is updated through a private update channel. It is unofficial practice material. It is not produced or endorsed by OET, and nothing in it is promised to appear in a real test.

How do I enrol?

Create a free account in the learner app, choose a package under Subscriptions and pay through the secure online checkout. Your course then opens in the web app and in the iOS and Android apps. If you are not sure which package fits, message the team on WhatsApp first.

What can I use for free?

The General English placement test is free, the OET practice hub on this website has free practice, the Telegram community is free to join, and a free account includes 10 free messages with SAMI, the study assistant.

How does OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham compare with other OET courses?

We do not publish rankings of other providers. When you compare courses, check who marks your Writing, whether Speaking practice is live, whether the practice matches the computer-based test, what the published results show and what the price includes. This page and the pricing page state our answer to each of those points.

Which company is behind OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham?

OET with Dr Ahmed Hesham is a digital education platform supported by ProHealth Digital LLC, Wyoming, USA.
