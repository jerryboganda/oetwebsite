Free General English Placement Test

Get an indicative CEFR placement estimate across Listening, Reading, Writing and Speaking — on the CEFR scale from Pre‑A1 to C2. Suitable for OET, IELTS, PTE, TOEFL and General English learners.

Listening

Everyday conversations and announcements that test real understanding.

Reading

Short practical texts that pinpoint how much you genuinely comprehend.

Writing

Brief written tasks that reveal your grammar, vocabulary and organisation.

Speaking

Spoken responses measured for fluency, clarity and range.
